Selection Process

The selection process for IBPS Customer Service Associate (Clerk) Recruitment 2026 consists of the following stages:

  • Stage 1 – Preliminary Examination
  • Stage 2 – Main Examination
  • Stage 3 – Document Verification
  • Stage 4 – Final Allotment
Important: There is no interview for the IBPS Customer Service Associate (Clerk) recruitment. Final selection is based on the Main Examination score and successful document verification.
