Key Instructions for Applicants

Ensure you follow these fundamental preparation steps to avoid last-minute hassles during exam day:

  • Document Verification: Check if your admit card details match your official Government ID card (e.g., Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID).
  • Reporting Time: Note the gate closing time specified on your admit card and plan travel accordingly.
  • Prohibited Items: Electronic devices, smartwatches, and unauthorized paper items are strictly banned inside testing centers.

💡 Expert Tip for Aspirants

Download multiple printed copies of your admit cards and carry two recent passport-size photographs to the test center to prevent delays.
