2. Admit Cards & Exam Dates Updates

Various recruitment boards are scheduling their computer-based tests (CBT) and offline examinations. Check the latest status below:

  • SSC Examinations: Admit cards for upcoming SSC exams are released on respective regional websites 4 days prior to the exam date.
  • Railway Recruitment Board (RRB): City intimation slips and hall tickets will be made available on official regional portals soon.
  • Banking & Insurance Exams: IBPS and SBI call letters for prelims and mains can be downloaded using your registration number and date of birth.

Warning for Candidates

Do not share your login credentials or registration password with anyone. Download hall tickets exclusively from official government portals.

Quick Preparation Tips for Aspirants

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. How can I get daily job updates on SelectionPro.in?

You can bookmark SelectionPro.in in your browser or regularly visit our 'Latest Updates' section to stay informed about the newest job alerts.

Q2. What should I do if my fee payment fails during online application?

Wait for 24 to 48 hours before attempting a re-payment. Check your bank account status, and if the money was debited, contact the respective portal's helpdesk email.

Q3. Why am I unable to download my Admit Card?

Clear your browser cache, re-verify your registration number and date of birth, or try using an incognito window. If the issue persists, contact the official helpdesk.
