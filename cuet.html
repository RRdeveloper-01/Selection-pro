How to Crack CUET UG 2026

Common University Entrance Test (CUET UG) India ki top Central Universities jaise DU, BHU, JNU, AMU aur Jamia Millia Islamia me admission ka main entrance exam hai. Achhi rank lane ke liye smart strategy aur regular practice bahut zaroori hai.

  • Understand the Exam Pattern: Language, Domain Subjects aur General Test ke syllabus aur marking scheme ko pehle achchhi tarah samjhein.
  • NCERT First: Domain Subjects ke liye Class 12 NCERT ko primary study material banayein.
  • Language Practice: Daily Reading Comprehension, Vocabulary aur Grammar ki practice karein.
  • General Test: Quantitative Aptitude, Logical Reasoning aur Current Affairs par roz practice karein.
  • Online Mock Tests: CUET Computer Based Test (CBT) hai, isliye online mock tests zaroor dein.
  • Avoid Guessing: Negative marking ko dhyan me rakhte hue sirf wahi questions attempt karein jinme confidence ho.
  • Time Management: Daily practice se speed aur accuracy dono improve karein.
