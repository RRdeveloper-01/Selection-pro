Welcome to Selection Pro

Welcome to Selection Pro — a student-focused platform created to make exam preparation simpler, more accessible, and more organized.

Selection Pro brings together useful learning resources and preparation tools in one place, helping students and aspirants prepare for competitive and academic examinations without having to search across multiple platforms.

Our Journey

Selection Pro was founded in 2026 by RR Developer. The platform initially started with a simple goal: to make Previous Year Question Papers (PYQs) easier for students to find and use.

Over time, Selection Pro has grown beyond PYQs into a broader exam-preparation platform covering Current Affairs, Mock Tests, Scholarships, study resources, preparation guides, educational updates, and useful learning tools.

Our goal is to continue expanding Selection Pro into a practical one-stop platform where students can discover reliable resources, practice regularly, stay updated, and prepare more effectively.

What Selection Pro Offers

Previous Year Question Papers

Access Previous Year Question Papers and exam-related practice resources to understand exam patterns, question trends, and the level of preparation required.

Current Affairs & News

Stay updated with important national, international, economy, science and technology, sports, and other exam-relevant current affairs. Our current affairs content is designed with competitive examinations in mind.

Free Mock Tests & Practice

Practice through free mock tests and other interactive resources designed to help students improve their preparation and test-taking skills.

Scholarship Information

Discover useful scholarship opportunities and information about government, institutional, and other educational scholarships that may help eligible students explore financial support for their education.

Study Resources & Preparation Guides

We provide useful study resources, preparation guides, educational information, and tools designed to make exam preparation more structured and convenient.

Learning & Productivity Tools

Selection Pro is also working towards building practical tools that can make studying, revision, note-making, and exam preparation more convenient for students.

Exams We Aim to Support

Selection Pro is designed to support students preparing for a wide range of competitive and academic examinations, including SSC, UPSC, Banking, Railway, Defence, State PCS, JEE, CUET and other examinations.

As the platform grows, we plan to expand our coverage and introduce more useful resources for students preparing for different examinations and educational opportunities.

Our Approach

We believe useful educational resources should be easy to discover, simple to understand, and accessible to students. We therefore focus on creating a clean and practical platform rather than simply providing a large amount of information.

Wherever possible, we aim to use information from official or publicly available sources and encourage students to verify important examination, scholarship, admission, and deadline-related information with the relevant official authority.

Free Resources & Future Premium Content

Our aim is to keep providing a strong collection of useful resources that students can access for free.

In the future, Selection Pro may also introduce carefully selected premium content and advanced learning resources at affordable prices. Any premium offerings will be designed to provide additional value while keeping quality educational resources accessible to students.

Our long-term vision is to build a sustainable platform where free resources remain valuable while premium features can support the continued development of better content, tools, and learning experiences.

Our Commitment to Students

We are committed to continuously improving Selection Pro by adding useful resources, updating existing content, improving the user experience, and developing practical tools for students and exam aspirants.

Selection Pro is being built with a simple purpose: to help students prepare smarter, stay informed, and access useful educational resources in one place.
