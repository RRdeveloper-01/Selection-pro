What is the PM Scholarship Scheme?

The Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme (PMSS) was introduced to encourage higher technical and professional education among eligible dependents of Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) and Assam Rifles personnel. The scheme is administered under the framework of the National Defence Fund and is associated with the Ministry of Home Affairs for the CAPF and Assam Rifles category.

The scholarship is intended for eligible students pursuing recognized professional degree programmes. Selection is subject to the eligibility conditions, available scholarships and applicable PMSS guidelines.

📌 PMSS at a Glance

  • Scheme: Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme (PMSS)
  • Main Ministry: Ministry of Home Affairs
  • Purpose: Support higher technical and professional education
  • Main beneficiaries: Eligible wards/widows of CAPFs & Assam Rifles personnel
  • Fresh applicant minimum: Generally 60% in applicable MEQ
  • Scholarship duration: Up to 5 years, depending on the approved course duration

PMSS Eligibility Criteria 2026

The PMSS eligibility criteria depend on the category of the applicant and the professional course being pursued. For the CAPF and Assam Rifles category, eligible applicants can include wards/widows covered under the scheme guidelines, including specified categories of serving, retired, deceased or disabled personnel.

Academic Eligibility

  • The applicant should be pursuing an eligible first professional degree programme.
  • A fresh applicant generally needs at least 60% marks in the applicable Minimum Entry Qualification (MEQ).
  • The applicable MEQ may be 10+2, Diploma or Graduation depending on the course.
  • Lateral-entry students admitted to the second year of certain technical/professional bachelor degree courses after a Diploma may also be eligible under the guidelines.

Family Eligibility

The scheme is not a general scholarship available to every college student. Eligibility is linked to the service category and status of the parent/personnel as specified in the official PMSS guidelines.

⚠️ Important Eligibility Note

Simply securing 60% marks does not make every student eligible for PMSS. The applicant must also belong to an eligible beneficiary category and pursue a course covered under the scheme.

How Many Marks Are Required for PMSS?

For a fresh PMSS applicant, the official guidelines specify a minimum of 60% marks in the applicable Minimum Entry Qualification (MEQ).

The qualification used for calculating the percentage depends on the professional course. For example, the applicable entry qualification can be 10+2 for some courses, while Diploma or Graduation may apply to others.

🎯 Quick Example

If the applicable MEQ for a course is Class 12 and the applicant has secured at least 60% in that qualification, the academic minimum may be satisfied. However, the applicant must also meet the family/service-category and course requirements.

PMSS Scholarship Duration

The PMSS scholarship can continue for the duration of the eligible professional course, subject to the applicable rules. The official guidelines provide for a scholarship period of up to five years, depending on the duration of the course approved by the relevant regulatory body.

How to Apply for PM Scholarship Scheme?

Eligible applicants generally apply online through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP). The application is then subject to verification by the concerned institution and the authorities specified under the scheme.

📝 PMSS Application Process

  1. Visit the National Scholarship Portal.
  2. Register/login using the applicable student credentials.
  3. Search for the relevant Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme (PMSS).
  4. Read the eligibility conditions carefully.
  5. Fill in personal, academic and service-related information.
  6. Upload the required documents in the prescribed format.
  7. Submit the application before the closing date.
  8. Complete institute and other applicable verification stages.
  9. Keep the application/registration details safely for future reference.

🌐 Official Application Portal

Applications for eligible PMSS categories are handled through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) when the scheme is open for applications.

Documents Required for PMSS

The exact documents can vary according to the applicant category, course and current application instructions. Applicants should check the latest NSP instructions before uploading documents. Commonly relevant information/documents may include:

  • Academic marksheet/certificate
  • Admission or course details
  • Student bank account details
  • Aadhaar-related details where required by the portal
  • Parent/personnel service-related certificate
  • Disability/death/gallantry/service certificate, wherever applicable
  • Institution verification details

⚠️ Don't Upload Random Documents

Only upload documents requested by the official application portal and applicable to your category. Requirements can change between application cycles.

PMSS Renewal Rules

Students who have already received PMSS may need to apply for renewal for subsequent academic years. The official guidelines state that renewal applicants are required to pass each subsequent academic year of the professional course with the applicable minimum requirement, including 50% marks under the stated PMSS guideline.

🔄 Remember

Fresh application and renewal are not the same. A student already receiving PMSS should follow the renewal instructions and deadlines shown on the official scholarship portal.

Important Points About PM Scholarship Scheme

  • PMSS is not a general scholarship for all students.
  • Eligibility depends on the beneficiary category defined under the scheme.
  • Fresh applicants generally need 60% marks in the applicable MEQ.
  • The scholarship is intended for eligible professional degree courses.
  • Renewal has separate academic requirements.
  • Only the number of scholarships approved under the scheme are available for fresh applicants.
  • Applicants should use the official scholarship portal and follow the latest notification.
  • Do not rely on old scholarship amounts or old application dates without checking the current notification.

Frequently Asked Questions About PMSS

1. What is PMSS?

PMSS stands for Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme. It supports eligible students pursuing higher technical and professional education under the specified beneficiary categories.

2. What is the PMSS scholarship amount for girls?

For the CAPF and Assam Rifles category, the scholarship amount for girls is ₹3,000 per month, equivalent to ₹36,000 annually.

3. What is the PMSS scholarship amount for boys?

For the CAPF and Assam Rifles category, the scholarship amount for boys is ₹2,500 per month, equivalent to ₹30,000 annually.

4. What is the minimum percentage for a fresh PMSS applicant?

The PMSS guidelines specify a minimum of 60% marks in the applicable Minimum Entry Qualification for a fresh applicant.

5. Can PMSS be renewed every year?

Eligible students can apply for renewal subject to the applicable PMSS renewal rules and academic performance requirements.

6. Is PMSS available for every college course?

No. PMSS is intended for eligible professional degree courses covered by the scheme guidelines. The course must meet the applicable recognition and eligibility conditions.

7. Can every student apply for PM Scholarship?

No. PMSS is not a general scholarship for every student. Applicants must belong to an eligible beneficiary category and meet the academic and course requirements.

8. Where can I apply for PMSS?

Eligible applicants can apply through the National Scholarship Portal when the relevant PMSS application window is open.
