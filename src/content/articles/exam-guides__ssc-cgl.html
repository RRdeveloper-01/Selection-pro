The Staff Selection Commission - Combined Graduate Level (SSC CGL) exam is one of the most sought-after national-level competitive exams in India for recruiting candidates into various Group 'B' and Group 'C' administrative posts across government ministries and departments.

1. SSC CGL Overview

SSC CGL offers a premier gateway to respected government roles. Selected candidates are appointed to posts such as Income Tax Inspector, Assistant Section Officer (ASO), Central Excise Inspector, Sub-Inspector in CBI, and Auditor.

Key Highlights

The exam is conducted annually in online computer-based mode (CBT). It evaluates candidates across Quantitative Aptitude, English, Reasoning, General Awareness, and Computer Knowledge.

2. Eligibility Criteria

A. Educational Qualification

  • General Posts: Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized university.
  • Junior Statistical Officer (JSO): Bachelor’s Degree with at least 60% Marks in Mathematics at 12th standard level OR Bachelor’s Degree with Statistics as one of the main subjects.

B. Age Limit & Relaxations

The standard age requirement ranges from 18 to 32 years depending on the specific post applied for. Age relaxation rules apply as per government standards:

Category Age Relaxation
OBC 3 Years
SC / ST 5 Years
PwBD (Unreserved) 10 Years

3. Selection Process

The selection process comprises two sequential stages:

  1. Tier 1: Preliminary Examination (Computer Based Test - Qualifying Nature).
  2. Tier 2: Mains Examination + Computer Proficiency Test & Data Entry Speed Test (Merit Deciding).

Important Note on Negative Marking

There is a penalty of 0.50 marks for each incorrect answer in Tier 1. In Tier 2, 1 mark is deducted for each wrong answer in Paper 1.

5. Detailed Syllabus Overview

Reasoning & Intelligence

Analogies, Series completion, Coding-Decoding, Venn Diagrams, Blood Relations, Syllogism, Spatial Orientation, and Non-verbal reasoning.

Quantitative Aptitude

Number System, Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Trigonometry, Profit & Loss, Percentages, Ratio & Proportion, Average, and Data Interpretation.

English Language

Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Error Spotting, Fillers, Synonyms & Antonyms, Idioms & Phrases, and Active/Passive Voice.

General Awareness

History, Culture, Geography, Indian Polity & Constitution, Indian Economy, General Science, and Current Affairs.

Preparation Tip

Focus heavily on Previous Year Questions (PYQs) as standard concepts and question patterns frequently repeat across exam shifts.

7. Frequently Asked Questions

Is there an interview in SSC CGL?

No, interviews were completely abolished for Group B and C posts in SSC CGL exams.

Can final year graduation students apply?

Yes, provided you acquire the essential qualification certificate on or before the crucial cutoff date specified in the official notification.

Is Tier 1 qualifying in nature?

Yes, Tier 1 marks are qualifying for shortlisting candidates for Tier 2. Final selection is based on Tier 2 performance.
