Brain Booster Memory & Focus Game for Students

Selection Pro Brain Booster is a free browser-based memory and concentration game designed as a short mental focus activity for students. The challenge requires you to remember a sequence of tiles and reproduce it correctly.

Improve Memory and Concentration

The sequence-based gameplay gives students a quick way to test visual memory, concentration, reaction speed and attention. It is designed as a short study-break activity rather than a replacement for regular exam preparation.
