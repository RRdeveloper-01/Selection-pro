🧠 How to Play Brain Booster

1. Choose Your Study Session

Select a 60, 90 or 180-minute study session. Start the session and focus on your preparation. The Brain Booster remains locked while you study.

2. Unlock Your 5-Minute Break

When your selected study session finishes, a 5-minute brain break automatically becomes available. The break timer starts automatically.

3. How to Play the Memory Game

  • Watch the tiles carefully as they light up.
  • Remember the exact order of the highlighted tiles.
  • When the sequence finishes, tap the same tiles in exactly the same order.
  • Complete the sequence to reach the next level.
  • As the level increases, the sequence becomes longer and the challenge increases.

4. What Happens If You Select the Wrong Box?

A wrong box ends only the current game attempt. It does not end your entire 5-minute break. Simply tap Play Again and start a fresh attempt.

5. Can I Play Multiple Times?

Yes. You can play multiple times during the same 5-minute break. The Play Again button never resets the break timer.

6. What Happens After 5 Minutes?

When the 5-minute break ends, the Brain Booster automatically locks. The Go Study section appears with useful Selection Pro pages such as Current Affairs, Exam Practice, Blog and Mock Tests.

7. When Can I Play Again?

After your break ends, choose another 60, 90 or 180-minute study session. Complete that study session to unlock your next 5-minute brain break.

8. Does Refresh Reset the Timer?

No. Your study and break timers are saved using your browser's local storage and are calculated using the actual end time. Refreshing the page does not restart the timer.

❓ Brain Booster FAQ

How do I play this memory game?
Watch the highlighted boxes, remember their order, and tap the same boxes in the same sequence.
Can I play again after making a mistake?
Yes. A wrong selection only ends the current attempt. You can play again while your 5-minute break is active.
Does Play Again restart the 5-minute timer?
No. The break timer continues independently from the game attempts.
What happens if I refresh the page?
The active study or break session is restored. The timer continues based on the actual remaining time.
How do I unlock another break?
Complete another 60, 90 or 180-minute study session after your current break ends.
