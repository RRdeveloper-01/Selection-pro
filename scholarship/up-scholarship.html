What is UP Scholarship?

UP Scholarship उत्तर प्रदेश सरकार की एक Financial Assistance Scheme है जिसका उद्देश्य आर्थिक रूप से कमजोर विद्यार्थियों को शिक्षा जारी रखने में सहायता प्रदान करना है।

Scholarship का लाभ सरकारी, सहायता प्राप्त (Aided), मान्यता प्राप्त Private Schools, Colleges, Universities, Polytechnic तथा Professional Institutions में अध्ययनरत योग्य छात्रों को दिया जाता है।

UP Scholarship Eligibility 2026-27

  • Applicant must be a resident of Uttar Pradesh.
  • Student must be studying in a recognized School, College or University.
  • Admission should be taken in an eligible course.
  • Student should have a valid Aadhaar Number.
  • Bank Account should be linked with Aadhaar.
  • The institution should be registered on the UP Scholarship Portal.
  • Income of family should be within the prescribed limit.
  • Required educational qualification should be fulfilled.

How to Apply for UP Scholarship 2026-27?

Eligible students can apply online through the UP Scholarship Portal by following these steps.

  1. Visit the Official UP Scholarship Portal.
  2. Click on Student from the top menu.
  3. Select Fresh Login or Renewal Login.
  4. Complete the Registration using the required details.
  5. Login using the Registration Number and Password.
  6. Fill in Personal, Academic and Bank Details carefully.
  7. Upload the required documents.
  8. Preview the application before final submission.
  9. Submit the application form.
  10. Download and print the final application for future reference.

Always verify all details before Final Submit. Incorrect information may lead to rejection.

UP Scholarship Renewal Process

Students who have already received the scholarship in the previous academic session can apply through the Renewal option.

  • Login using Renewal Login.
  • Update current academic details.
  • Upload the latest required documents.
  • Verify bank details.
  • Submit the application before the last date.

How to Check UP Scholarship Status?

  1. Open the Official Scholarship Portal.
  2. Click on Status.
  3. Select the Academic Session.
  4. Enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth.
  5. Click on Search.
  6. Your Scholarship Status will appear on the screen.

Application Correction

If the Government opens a correction window, students can edit permitted fields during the correction period.

  • Login to your account.
  • Open the submitted application.
  • Edit allowed information.
  • Save and Final Submit again.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Who can apply for UP Scholarship?

Students studying in eligible schools, colleges, universities, ITI, Polytechnic, UG, PG and Professional Courses in Uttar Pradesh can apply if they meet the eligibility criteria.

Is Aadhaar mandatory?

Yes. Aadhaar is generally required, and the bank account should preferably be linked with Aadhaar.

Can first-year students apply?

Yes. Newly admitted eligible students can apply under the Fresh category.

Who should apply under Renewal?

Students who received the scholarship in the previous academic session and are continuing their studies.

Can students of private colleges apply?

Yes, if the institution is recognized and registered on the UP Scholarship Portal.

How will I receive the scholarship amount?

The scholarship amount is transferred directly to the student's eligible bank account through DBT.

Can I edit my application after submission?

Only if the Government provides a correction window for the current session.
