What is AICTE Pragati Scholarship?

The AICTE Pragati Scholarship Scheme is a scholarship programme intended to support eligible girl students pursuing technical education. The scheme is implemented by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and applications are handled through the National Scholarship Portal for the applicable scholarship cycle.

🎯 Main Objective

The main objective of the Pragati Scholarship is to provide financial support to eligible girl students and encourage their participation in technical education.

AICTE Pragati Scholarship 2026 Eligibility

Students should carefully check the current NSP and scheme conditions before submitting an application.

  • The scholarship is intended for eligible girl students.
  • The student should be pursuing an eligible technical Degree or Diploma programme.
  • The institution should be AICTE-approved.
  • For the Degree scheme, eligibility includes students admitted to the first year of the degree course and eligible second-year students admitted through lateral entry, subject to the applicable scheme rules.
  • The annual family income should generally be not more than ₹8 lakh.
  • Students must satisfy the current NSP and AICTE conditions applicable to the academic year.

⚠️ Important Eligibility Note

Eligibility rules can differ between the Degree and Diploma versions of the scheme and may be updated for a new academic cycle. Always verify the eligibility shown on the current NSP application before submitting the form.

AICTE Pragati Scholarship Amount 2026

Eligible selected students can receive financial assistance under the Pragati Scholarship scheme. Current government information describes the scholarship benefit as ₹50,000 per annum.
