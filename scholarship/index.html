📄 Common Documents Required for Scholarships

The exact documents depend on the individual scholarship. However, students may commonly need:

  • Identity proof such as Aadhaar or another accepted document.
  • Previous examination marksheet or certificate.
  • Bank account details where required.
  • Income certificate, if required.
  • Caste/category certificate, where applicable.
  • Domicile or residence certificate, where applicable.
  • Disability certificate, where applicable.
  • Admission, institution or fee-related details, where required.

📌 Document Tip

Keep valid documents ready, but upload only the documents requested by the official scholarship application portal.

✅ Important Tips Before Applying

Apply Carefully

  • Read the complete eligibility criteria before applying.
  • Check the official application portal and current deadline.
  • Enter your personal and academic information correctly.
  • Check bank account details carefully.
  • Save your application number or acknowledgement.
  • Do not share OTP, password or other confidential login information.
  • Check application status after submission when available.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Scholarship 2026 Hub?

The Scholarship 2026 Hub is a central resource where students can discover important government and private scholarships along with eligibility, documents and application guidance.

Who can apply for scholarships?

Eligibility depends on the individual scheme. Educational qualification, income, course, category, institution and other conditions may apply.

What documents are commonly required?

Students may need identity proof, marksheets, bank details, income certificate, domicile proof and other documents depending on the scholarship.

Where should I apply for a scholarship?

Apply through the official portal specified by the individual scholarship scheme and verify the latest instructions before submission.

Can I apply for more than one scholarship?

Whether multiple scholarships can be received or applied for depends on the rules of the individual schemes. Always check the applicable terms before submitting applications.

How can I check my scholarship application status?

Use the official portal of the relevant scholarship scheme and follow its application-status process. Keep your application or registration details safe.
