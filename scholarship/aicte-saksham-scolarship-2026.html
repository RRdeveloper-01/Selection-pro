AICTE Saksham Scholarship 2026-27

The AICTE Saksham Scholarship Scheme provides financial assistance to eligible specially abled students pursuing technical education.

The scheme covers eligible students taking admission in AICTE-approved technical institutions at Degree and Diploma level.

AICTE describes the scholarship as scholarship/contingency support of ₹50,000 per year. 2

2026-27 Update: The National Scholarship Portal currently lists separate AICTE Saksham Scholarship schemes for Technical Degree and Technical Diploma students.

AICTE Saksham Scholarship Amount 2026-27

According to AICTE's student development schemes page, the Saksham Scholarship provides:

₹50,000 per year

AICTE describes this amount as scholarship/contingency support for eligible differently abled students. 3

The actual payment and continuation are subject to the applicable scheme rules, verification and approval.

AICTE Saksham Scholarship Eligibility

1. Specially Abled Student

The Saksham Scholarship is intended for eligible specially abled students.

2. Technical Degree

Students pursuing eligible technical degree programmes in AICTE-approved institutions may apply under the Degree scheme.

3. Technical Diploma

Students pursuing eligible technical diploma programmes may apply under the Diploma scheme.

4. AICTE-Approved Institution

The student should be admitted to an institution/course that satisfies the applicable AICTE scheme requirements.

5. Disability Certificate

Applicants should have valid disability-related documentation as required under the current scholarship guidelines.

6. NSP Application

The eligible student must complete the scholarship application through the National Scholarship Portal and complete the required verification.

Disability Certificate for Saksham Scholarship

Because Saksham is specifically intended for specially abled students, applicants should keep their valid disability certificate/documentation ready.

The certificate should be issued by the appropriate competent authority and should satisfy the requirements specified in the current scholarship application.

Important: Do not enter an incorrect disability percentage or upload an invalid certificate. The information submitted on NSP should match the official documents.

Documents Required for AICTE Saksham Scholarship

Keep the following documents ready before starting the online application. The exact documents requested can depend on the current NSP application and verification requirements.

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Valid Disability Certificate
  • Recent academic marksheet/certificate
  • Admission proof
  • Institute/college details
  • Family Income Certificate, wherever required
  • Tuition fee/admission fee details, wherever applicable
  • Bank account details
  • Aadhaar-linked bank details, wherever required
  • Passport-size photograph, if requested
  • Other documents requested by NSP or the applicable scheme
Always follow the document list shown inside your current NSP application. Upload clear, valid and readable documents.

AICTE Saksham Scholarship Selection & Verification Process

  1. Student completes the online application through NSP.
  2. Required documents are submitted with the application.
  3. The application is checked through the applicable verification process.
  4. The institute verifies the student's application where required.
  5. Further designated verification is completed as applicable.
  6. Eligible and verified applications proceed for scholarship processing.
  7. Payment is processed according to the applicable scholarship rules.

Common Mistakes to Avoid in Saksham Scholarship Application

  • Entering incorrect Aadhaar or personal information
  • Mismatch between application and official documents
  • Uploading an invalid or unclear disability certificate
  • Incorrect bank account details
  • Incorrect institute or course information
  • Submitting incomplete information
  • Not completing institute verification
  • Missing the application deadline

Before final submission, carefully compare the information in the application with your original documents.
