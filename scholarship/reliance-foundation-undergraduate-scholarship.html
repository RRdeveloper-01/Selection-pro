Reliance Foundation Undergraduate Scholarship Eligibility

Students need to satisfy the applicable eligibility conditions before submitting the scholarship application.

1. Indian Citizenship

The applicant should be a resident Indian citizen.

2. Class 12 Qualification

Applicants must have passed Class 12 with the required academic performance specified by the scholarship programme. The published eligibility information has used a minimum 60% in Class 12 criterion.

3. First-Year Undergraduate Student

The applicant should currently be enrolled in the first year of a regular full-time undergraduate degree programme.

4. Full-Time Degree Programme

The programme must be a regular full-time undergraduate degree programme. Online, distance, remote or other non-regular modes are not eligible under the published programme conditions.

5. Family Income

The household income should be ₹15 lakh or less. Preference is given to applicants from households with income below ₹2.5 lakh.

Who should consider applying?

A first-year undergraduate student with the required Class 12 performance, a regular full-time degree programme and household income within the specified limit can check eligibility and apply.

Reliance Foundation Scholarship Family Income Limit

Family income is an important part of the merit-cum-means selection process. The published eligibility criteria state:

Below ₹2.5 Lakh Preference is given

Important

Do not enter an incorrect household income. Income information should match the supporting documents submitted with the application.

How to Apply for Reliance Foundation Undergraduate Scholarship

Step 1: Visit the Official Scholarship Portal

Open the official Reliance Foundation Scholarship website and select the Undergraduate Scholarship application.

Step 2: Complete Eligibility Questionnaire

Applicants first need to provide the information requested in the eligibility/registration questionnaire.

Step 3: Create Application

Use your personal email/mobile details as requested by the application system and begin the scholarship application.

Step 4: Fill Personal and Academic Details

Enter your personal information, Class 10 and Class 12 details, college information, undergraduate course details and other requested information.

Step 5: Enter Family Income Details

Provide the required household income information accurately.

Step 6: Upload Supporting Documents

Upload the required documents in the format and size specified by the application portal.

Step 7: Complete Aptitude Assessment

The Undergraduate Scholarship selection process includes an aptitude assessment. Applicants should complete the assessment according to the instructions and assigned schedule.

Step 8: Submit Application

Review all information carefully before final submission.

No Application Fee

Reliance Foundation states that there is no fee at any stage of the scholarship application process.

How to Continue the Scholarship?

Receiving the scholarship does not mean that all future payments are automatically guaranteed. Selected scholars are required to satisfy the applicable continuation conditions.

  • Remain enrolled full-time in the undergraduate programme.
  • Maintain good academic standing.
  • Pass each academic year as required.
  • Provide the required academic documentation.
  • Follow the terms and conditions of the scholarship.

Keep Your Academic Records Safe

Keep your marksheets, fee receipts and other academic documents safe because they may be required during the scholarship period.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying when you are not in the first year of your UG programme.
  • Entering incorrect Class 10 or Class 12 marks.
  • Entering incorrect household income.
  • Uploading unclear documents.
  • Using incorrect college or course information.
  • Not completing the aptitude assessment.
  • Creating multiple applications.
  • Waiting until the final day to submit the application.

Avoid Multiple Applications

The published application FAQs instruct students to create and submit only one application. Creating more than one application may lead to disqualification.
