How to Crack IBPS PO 2026

IBPS PO is one of India's most competitive banking examinations. Candidates have to clear the Preliminary Exam, Main Exam and Interview to become a Probationary Officer. Consistent practice, speed and accuracy are the keys to success.

  • Master the Exam Pattern: Understand sectional timing, negative marking and topic-wise weightage before starting your preparation.
  • Quantitative Aptitude: Practice Data Interpretation, Number Series, Simplification, Quadratic Equations and Arithmetic every day.
  • Reasoning Ability: Focus on Seating Arrangement, Puzzles, Syllogism, Blood Relations and Coding-Decoding.
  • English Language: Improve Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Error Detection and Vocabulary by reading newspapers daily.
  • General Awareness: Cover Banking Awareness, RBI Updates, Financial News and Current Affairs of the last 6 months.
  • Mock Tests: Attempt full-length mock tests every week and analyse your mistakes carefully.
  • Revision: Revise formulas, shortcuts and important concepts regularly to improve speed and confidence.
