ISRO Scientist Engineer Recruitment 2026 – Latest Update

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), through its ISRO Centralised Recruitment Board (ICRB), has released Advertisement No. ISRO:ICRB:02(EMC-CEPO):2026 dated 28 July 2026 for recruitment to Scientist/Engineer ‘SC’ posts.

The recruitment covers engineering disciplines including Electronics, Mechanical, Computer Science, Civil, Electrical, Refrigeration & Air Conditioning and Architecture. The notification lists a total of 92 tentative vacancies.

Important: The online application deadline is 17 August 2026. The application fee can be paid up to 19 August 2026, as specified in the official ISRO notification.

ISRO Scientist Engineer Recruitment 2026 Eligibility

Educational Qualification

Candidates must have the relevant BE/B.Tech degree or equivalent qualification in the discipline specified for the post.

The prescribed qualification generally requires an aggregate minimum of 65% marks across semesters or 6.84 CGPA on a 10-point scale.

GATE Qualification

Candidates are required to have a valid GATE 2025 or GATE 2026 score in the relevant discipline specified in the recruitment notification.

Age Limit

The maximum age limit is 28 years as on 17 August 2026. Applicable age relaxation is available according to Government of India rules for eligible categories.

ISRO Recruitment 2026 Application Fee

The application fee is ₹250. The fee is payable online through the Bharatkosh portal.

The official notification states that the last date for payment of the application fee is 19 August 2026.

Women, SC, ST, Ex-servicemen and PwBD candidates are exempted from payment of the application fee as specified in the notification.

ISRO Scientist Engineer Selection Process 2026

Candidates are shortlisted for interview based on their valid GATE score. The notification specifies shortlisting in a 1:7 ratio.

Interview Assessment

  • Technical / Academic Knowledge – 40 marks
  • General Awareness related to the technical specialization – 20 marks
  • Presentation / Communication Skill – 20 marks
  • Comprehension – 10 marks
  • Academic Achievements – 10 marks

For preparation of the final panel, the notification gives 50% weightage to GATE Score and 50% weightage to Interview marks.

ISRO Scientist Engineer Salary 2026

Selected candidates will be appointed as Scientist/Engineer ‘SC’ in Level 10 of the Pay Matrix.

The minimum basic pay is ₹56,100 per month, along with applicable allowances such as Dearness Allowance, House Rent Allowance and Transport Allowance as per rules.
