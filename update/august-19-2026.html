How to Apply for Government Jobs?

Before submitting an application, candidates should follow these basic steps:

  • Read the complete official recruitment notification.
  • Check age limit and educational qualification.
  • Check category-wise vacancy and reservation details.
  • Keep photograph, signature and required documents ready.
  • Complete the online application before the deadline.
  • Save the application form and payment receipt, if applicable.
  • Keep checking the official website for admit card and examination updates.
