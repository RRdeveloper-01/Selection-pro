JIPMER Admission Strategy 2026

JIPMER Puducherry is one of India's top medical institutes. Admission is based on NEET UG scores, so students must prepare with a strong strategy, regular mock tests and consistent revision.

  • Aim for a High NEET Score: To increase your chances of admission, target 690+ marks through consistent practice and revision.
  • Master NCERT: Read Biology, Chemistry and Physics NCERT thoroughly. Most NEET questions are directly or indirectly based on NCERT concepts.
  • Strengthen Biology: Focus on Genetics, Human Physiology, Ecology and Biotechnology. Biology carries the highest weightage in NEET.
  • Improve Physics & Chemistry: Practice numerical problems daily and strengthen Organic Chemistry reaction mechanisms.
  • Take Weekly Mock Tests: Solve full-length NEET mock tests under real exam conditions to improve speed and accuracy.
  • Analyse Your Mistakes: Review every mock test carefully and maintain an error notebook for regular revision.
  • Revise Frequently: Short notes, formula sheets and repeated revision are essential for scoring high in NEET.
