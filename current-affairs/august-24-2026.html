🔥 Most Important Current Affair

India-Russia Relations: External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar is visiting Russia on 23–24 August 2026 to co-chair the 27th Session of the India-Russia Inter-Governmental Commission on Trade, Economic, Scientific, Technological and Cultural Cooperation (IRIGC-TEC).

This is especially important for UPSC International Relations, SSC Current Affairs and Defence exams.

01 INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS

S. Jaishankar visits Russia for 27th India-Russia IRIGC-TEC

External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar is undertaking an official visit to Russia from 23–24 August 2026.

During the visit, he will co-chair the 27th Session of the India-Russia Inter-Governmental Commission on Trade, Economic, Scientific, Technological and Cultural Cooperation (IRIGC-TEC).

He is also scheduled to meet Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov to discuss regional and global issues.

Why It Matters

India-Russia relations cover defence, energy, trade, science and technology and strategic cooperation. Therefore, this development is relevant for UPSC International Relations and SSC Current Affairs 2026.

📌 Exam Fact

IRIGC-TEC stands for India-Russia Inter-Governmental Commission on Trade, Economic, Scientific, Technological and Cultural Cooperation.

Possible Question:

Who is visiting Russia on 23–24 August 2026 to co-chair the 27th IRIGC-TEC session?

Answer: S. Jaishankar
02 EDUCATION

National Teachers Awards 2026: 48 School Teachers Selected

The Government of India has announced that 48 school teachers will receive the National Teachers Awards 2026.

President Droupadi Murmu will confer the awards at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi on 5 September 2026, Teachers' Day.

The 48 teachers were selected through a three-stage selection process at the District, State and National levels.

Why It Matters

Education awards are important for SSC, UPSC, Railway and State PCS current affairs, especially questions related to government initiatives and awards.

📌 Exam Facts

  • Award: National Teachers Awards 2026
  • Awardees: 48 school teachers
  • Venue: Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi
  • Date: 5 September 2026
Possible Question:

How many school teachers have been selected for the National Teachers Awards 2026?

Answer: 48

Dak Sewa, Dak Mitra and Jan Sewa Connect Post Offices

The programme is aimed at strengthening citizen-centric postal services and improving access to digital and postal facilities.

Why It Matters

India Post's digital transformation is relevant to government schemes, digital governance, e-governance and SSC/UPSC current affairs.

📌 Exam Fact

The Department of Posts functions under the Ministry of Communications.

Possible Question:

Answer: 14
04 EDUCATION & STATE GOVERNMENT

Rajasthan Announces ₹12,500 Crore School Infrastructure Plan

Rajasthan has announced a ₹12,500 crore roadmap over five years to improve government school infrastructure.

The plan includes construction of new school buildings, repairs, maintenance and improvement of school facilities.

The development comes amid concerns regarding infrastructure gaps in government schools across the state.

Why It Matters

State government education initiatives are useful for State PCS, SSC and general awareness current affairs.

📌 Exam Fact

Total planned allocation: ₹12,500 crore over five years.

Possible Question:

How much has Rajasthan planned to spend over five years on the government school infrastructure overhaul?

Answer: ₹12,500 crore

08 SPORTS • ATHLETICS

Neeraj Chopra Finishes Second at Lausanne Diamond League

Indian javelin thrower Neeraj Chopra finished second at the Lausanne Diamond League with a season-best throw of 88.05 metres.

Sri Lanka's Rumesh Tharanga Pathirage won the event with a throw of 88.14 metres, just 9 centimetres ahead of Chopra.

Neeraj Chopra-related achievements are important for Sports Current Affairs, SSC GK and Railway Current Affairs.

  • Winner: Rumesh Tharanga Pathirage

09 INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS

India-Nepal Joint Working Group Reviews Border Cooperation

The 14th India-Nepal Joint Working Group on Border Management and Security was held in Dehradun on 20–21 August 2026.

Both sides reviewed bilateral cooperation on border management and security.

Discussions included cross-border crime, border infrastructure, Integrated Check Posts (ICPs), roads, railway networks, encroachments and maintenance of boundary pillars.

Why It Matters

India-Nepal relations and open-border management are important areas for UPSC International Relations, Defence and SSC Current Affairs.

📌 Exam Facts

  • Meeting: 14th Joint Working Group
  • Countries: India and Nepal
  • Venue: Dehradun, Uttarakhand
  • Next meeting: Nepal
Possible Question:

Where was the 14th India-Nepal Joint Working Group meeting held?

Answer: Dehradun, Uttarakhand
10 ECONOMY • INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION

Maharashtra and Germany's Hesse Sign MoU

Maharashtra and the German state of Hesse signed a Memorandum of Understanding to strengthen cooperation in investment, industry, innovation, employment and skill development.

The agreement was signed by Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis and Hesse Minister-President Boris Rhein.

Potential areas of cooperation include pharmaceuticals, healthcare, engineering, automobiles, financial services, logistics, digital economy and artificial intelligence.

Why It Matters

This development connects India-Germany relations, investment, skill development and international economic cooperation.

📌 Exam Fact

German state involved: Hesse.

Possible Question:

Which German state signed an MoU with Maharashtra for cooperation in investment and skill development?

Answer: Hesse
