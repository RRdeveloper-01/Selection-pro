India Celebrates 80th Independence Day in 2026

What Happened?

India celebrated its 80th Independence Day on 15 August 2026. The main national celebration was held at the historic Red Fort in New Delhi.

Why It Matters

Independence Day is one of India's most important national events and is frequently relevant to General Awareness and Current Affairs sections of competitive examinations.

Current Affair #3

Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers – Disclosure Scheme 2026

What Happened?

The Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers – Disclosure Scheme 2026 came into force on 16 August 2026.

The scheme provides an opportunity for eligible taxpayers to disclose specified undisclosed foreign income and assets subject to the conditions of the scheme.

Why It Matters

This is an important topic for questions related to Union Budget, taxation, income tax, financial reforms and economic current affairs.

Important Exam Facts

  • Scheme: Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers – Disclosure Scheme 2026
  • Commencement: 16 August 2026
  • Eligibility and payable amount are governed by the notified provisions.
  • The scheme deals with specified undisclosed foreign income and assets.

Possible Question

Q. The Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers – Disclosure Scheme 2026 relates primarily to:

A. Domestic agricultural loans
B. Undisclosed foreign income and assets
C. Railway recruitment
D. Export subsidies

Answer: B. Undisclosed foreign income and assets

Current Affair #5

UPI Merchant Charges and MDR Become an Important Banking Topic

What Happened?

Recent changes in the legal framework have brought renewed attention to Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) and the possibility of charges on selected high-value merchant UPI transactions.

The issue is particularly relevant to digital payments, banks, payment service providers and the sustainability of India's digital payment infrastructure.

Why It Matters

UPI is one of India's most important digital public infrastructure systems, making UPI-related policy changes highly relevant for Banking, SSC and other competitive exams.

Important Note

Do not interpret recent MDR discussions as a blanket charge on every UPI transaction. The applicable rules depend on the transaction type and merchant category.

Possible Question

Q. What does MDR stand for in digital payments?

A. Merchant Digital Regulation
B. Merchant Discount Rate
C. Monetary Deposit Rate
D. Market Digital Reserve

Answer: B. Merchant Discount Rate
