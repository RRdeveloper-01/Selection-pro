1. SEMICON India 2026 Set to Highlight India's Semiconductor Push

SEMICON India 2026 is scheduled to be held at Yashobhoomi in New Delhi from 17 to 19 September. Prime Minister Narendra Modi is scheduled to inaugurate the event on 17 September.

The event will bring together semiconductor manufacturers, technology companies, researchers, investors and other stakeholders as India works to develop a complete semiconductor ecosystem.

Technology News Exam Point: SEMICON India 2026 — 17 to 19 September; venue — Yashobhoomi, New Delhi.

2. Gujarat to Host Six of India's 12 Semiconductor Facilities

Gujarat is emerging as a major semiconductor manufacturing hub in India. According to a recent statement by Gujarat's Science and Technology Minister, six of India's twelve semiconductor manufacturing plants are expected to come up in the state.

Major projects are being developed around Sanand and Dholera, strengthening India's domestic electronics and semiconductor supply chain.

India Technology News Exam Point: Gujarat, Sanand, Dholera, semiconductor manufacturing and India Semiconductor Mission.

3. ISRO Successfully Tests CE20 Cryogenic Engine at 220 kN Thrust

The Indian Space Research Organisation successfully conducted a flight acceptance hot test of the indigenous CE20 cryogenic engine at a thrust level of 220 kN at the ISRO Propulsion Complex in Mahendragiri, Tamil Nadu.

The CE20 engine powers the upper stage of the LVM3 launch vehicle. The higher-thrust configuration is intended to improve the payload-carrying capability of future LVM3 missions.

Space News Exam Point: CE20 — cryogenic engine; LVM3 — India's heavy-lift launch vehicle; test location — Mahendragiri, Tamil Nadu.

4. AI Boom Increases Demand for Advanced Semiconductor Chips

Rapid growth in artificial intelligence is increasing demand for high-performance processors and memory chips used in AI systems and data centres.

The strong demand is also contributing to higher prices for some memory components and electronics, a trend increasingly described as "chipflation."

AI & Technology News Exam Point: AI infrastructure, memory chips, data centres, semiconductor supply chains and chipflation.

5. India Strengthens Indigenous Technology for Space Missions

India's semiconductor and electronics capabilities are increasingly supporting its space programme. Indigenous semiconductor components have been used in important missions and satellite systems, strengthening India's technological independence.

The growing connection between domestic chip manufacturing and space technology is important for India's long-term strategic technology capabilities.

Science News Exam Point: Indigenous semiconductors, space technology, satellite systems and technological self-reliance.
