Science and Technology Current Affairs 6 September 2026: ISRO EOS-05 & Engine Test

Today's science and technology current affairs focus on ISRO's EOS-05 mission, the GSLV-F17 launch vehicle, a 200-tonne semi-cryogenic engine test and India-France space cooperation.

1. ISRO successfully launches EOS-05 on GSLV-F17

ISRO successfully launched the EOS-05 Earth observation satellite using the GSLV-F17 rocket on 4 September 2026. The mission is a major science and technology current affairs topic because it demonstrates India's growing capability in placing large Earth-observation payloads into high orbits.

OrganisationISRO
Launch vehicleGSLV-F17
SatelliteEOS-05
Launch siteSatish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota

2. ISRO's semi-cryogenic engine completes full-thrust test at 200-tonne thrust

ISRO tested the semi-cryogenic engine powerhead at full 200-tonne thrust at Mahendragiri. The development is aimed at strengthening future launch-vehicle capability, including support for heavier LVM3-class missions.

Exam Point: A semi-cryogenic engine uses liquid oxygen with a hydrocarbon fuel; it is designed to provide high performance while supporting larger launch systems.

3. EOS-05 adds a new Earth-observation capability

EOS-05 is designed for Earth imaging and observation. High-orbit Earth-observation capability can support applications such as weather and environmental monitoring, disaster management, agriculture and strategic observation.

4. India-France cooperation expands into human spaceflight and advanced space technology

Recent discussions between India and France have highlighted deeper cooperation in human spaceflight and advanced space technologies, strengthening the broader ISRO-CNES relationship.

Interview Angle: Remember that ISRO is India's national space agency, while CNES is France's national space agency.
