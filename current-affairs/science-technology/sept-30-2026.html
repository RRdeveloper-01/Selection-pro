1. ISRO Holds National Meet on Scientific Exploration of the Moon

ISRO is conducting a three-day national meeting from September 28 to 30 in Bengaluru on scientific exploration of the Moon through Chandrayaan missions. Researchers, academics, students and ISRO scientists are participating.

Exam Fact: The Chandrayaan programme is India's lunar exploration programme.

2. India-France Space Cooperation Expands

India and France are exploring a new phase of space cooperation involving human spaceflight, research, satellite missions and private-sector participation, connecting government institutions, researchers, industry and start-ups.

Exam Fact: Gaganyaan is India's human spaceflight programme.

3. Indigenous Technology Development Gets Greater Policy Attention

India continues supporting indigenous technology development in advanced electronics, sensing, space technologies and research. RDI initiatives aim to strengthen domestic capabilities and encourage industry participation.

Exam Fact: RDI means Research, Development and Innovation.

4. AI and Advanced Technologies Remain Strategic Priorities

Artificial intelligence and advanced digital technologies are increasingly used across public services, industry, research and strategic sectors, increasing the importance of computing infrastructure, reliable data and cybersecurity.

Exam Fact: AI stands for Artificial Intelligence.

5. Semiconductor Capability Remains Important for India's Technology Ecosystem

Semiconductors are fundamental to electronics, telecommunications, vehicles, computing and strategic systems. India's chip manufacturing and design efforts are therefore important for industrial development and technological resilience.

Exam Fact: Semiconductors are materials whose electrical conductivity can be controlled.
