1. India's semiconductor sector attracts $1.4 billion in funding

India's semiconductor sector has attracted about $1.4 billion in cumulative equity funding across 281 companies, with nearly half raised since 2025. Bengaluru is a major hub for semiconductor-related funding.

India has highlighted critical minerals as strategically important for technology and industrial resilience. These minerals are essential for advanced technologies, electronics and clean-energy applications.

3. BRICS highlights AI and emerging technology cooperation

The BRICS agenda includes cooperation in artificial intelligence, digital technologies and innovation. India has also emphasized preventing technology from becoming a tool of geopolitical coercion.

Geopolitical tensions have made resilient technology and mineral supply chains an important policy issue. India is seeking diversified and reliable access to critical technologies and resources.
