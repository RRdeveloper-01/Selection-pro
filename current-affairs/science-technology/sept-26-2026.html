1. 'Vigyan Ki Baat' Second Edition Focuses on Quantum Technology

What happened: The second edition of the Department of Science and Technology's 'Vigyan Ki Baat' lecture series is scheduled for September 26, 2026. The programme focuses on foundational physical sciences and quantum technology and aims to connect scientific research with the wider public.

Why it matters: Quantum technology is an emerging area with applications in computing, communication, sensing and advanced measurement. Public understanding of such technologies can also strengthen India's science ecosystem.

Exam Fact: The Department of Science and Technology functions under India's Ministry of Science and Technology.

2. GalaxEye Gets ₹63.84 Crore RDI Support for Multisensor Satellite Technology

What happened: The Technology Development Board and Department of Science and Technology signed an agreement with GalaxEye for ₹63.84 crore in RDI support to develop next-generation multisensor satellite technology.

Why it matters: Multisensor satellite systems can combine observations from different sensing technologies and support applications such as Earth observation, mapping, disaster management and environmental monitoring.

Exam Fact: Remote sensing enables information about Earth's surface to be collected without direct physical contact with the object being observed.

3. India Commercialises Bio-Based Biodegradable Plastic Under BioE3

What happened: The Ministry of Science and Technology highlighted the commercial launch of a bio-based biodegradable plastic developed under the BioE3 initiative. The development connects biotechnology research with sustainable materials.

Why it matters: Bio-based materials can help create alternatives to conventional petroleum-based materials and support more sustainable manufacturing.

Exam Fact: BioE3 stands for Biotechnology for Economy, Environment and Employment.

4. Indian Researchers Explore a New Tool for Quantum Measurement

What happened: Indian researchers have reported an experiment that could lead to a new approach for quantum measurement. The research explores the behaviour of quantum systems and the possibilities of obtaining precise information from them.

Why it matters: Advanced quantum measurement is important for emerging technologies including quantum sensing, precision measurement and quantum information science.

Exam Fact: Quantum mechanics is the branch of physics used to describe matter and energy at atomic and subatomic scales.

5. India and EU Discuss Cooperation in AI, Quantum Technology and Semiconductors

What happened: An India–EU engagement on science, technology and emerging technologies discussed deeper cooperation in areas including artificial intelligence, quantum technologies, semiconductors and clean-energy research.

Why it matters: International cooperation in emerging technologies can support research, investment, knowledge exchange and development of advanced technology ecosystems.

Exam Fact: The India–EU cooperation framework includes the Trade and Technology Council, which provides a platform for discussions on technology and strategic economic issues.
