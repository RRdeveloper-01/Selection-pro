1. ISRO Targets NVS-03 Navigation Satellite Launch in October

What happened: ISRO is preparing for the NVS-03 navigation satellite mission, with launch targeted for 15–20 October 2026 on a GSLV-Mk II rocket.

Why it matters: The mission is relevant to India’s regional navigation capabilities and NavIC ecosystem.

Exam Fact: NavIC is India’s regional satellite-navigation system.

2. India’s Semiconductor Ecosystem Draws Global Participation

What happened: SEMICON India 2026 brought together companies across semiconductor manufacturing, materials, logistics, chip design and advanced packaging.

Why it matters: Building a complete semiconductor value chain is important for India’s electronics ambitions.

Exam Fact: Packaging and testing are important stages after chip fabrication.

3. Applied Materials Announces Major India Investment

What happened: Applied Materials announced plans to invest about $5 billion in India over the next decade, focusing on research, supply chains and workforce development.

Why it matters: The investment adds to international participation in India’s semiconductor ecosystem.

Exam Fact: Applied Materials supplies semiconductor manufacturing equipment.

4. AI Demand Is Driving Semiconductor Capacity Expansion

What happened: Growing AI demand is increasing requirements for processors, memory, networking and data-centre infrastructure.

Why it matters: The link between AI and semiconductor capacity is an important technology-policy issue.

Exam Fact: AI accelerators are specialised processors designed for AI workloads.

5. India Continues Building Chip Design and Manufacturing Capability

What happened: India’s semiconductor programme covers chip design, fabrication, packaging, materials, equipment and skilled workforce development.

Why it matters: A wider ecosystem can strengthen electronics manufacturing and supply-chain resilience.

Exam Fact: RISC-V is an open instruction-set architecture used by processor designers.
