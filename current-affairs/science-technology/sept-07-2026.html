Science & Technology Current Affairs 7 September 2026: ISRO, EOS-05 & Space Reforms

ISRO, EOS-05, GSLV-F17, IN-SPACe, space-sector reforms and human-spaceflight cooperation selected for science and technology exams.

Published: 7 September 2026 · Exam-focused current affairs.

1. ISRO says it will remain at the centre of India's space programme

ISRO has clarified that it is not being privatised and will remain central to India's space programme. Routine manufacturing and commercial activities can involve public-sector entities and private companies while ISRO focuses on advanced and strategic capabilities.

2. IN-SPACe is central to India's private space ecosystem

The Indian National Space Promotion and Authorisation Centre (IN-SPACe) promotes, enables and regulates participation of non-government entities in India's space activities.

3. EOS-05 successfully launched aboard GSLV-F17

The successful launch of EOS-05 aboard GSLV-F17 on 4 September is a major science and technology current-affairs topic. EOS-05 is an Earth-observation satellite.

4. US Ambassador Sergio Gor meets astronaut Shubhanshu Shukla

US Ambassador to India Sergio Gor met Indian astronaut Shubhanshu Shukla and highlighted his journey as an example of India-US space cooperation. Shubhanshu Shukla became the first Indian to travel to the International Space Station.

5. India's space reforms expand public-private participation

India's space-policy framework encourages private companies to participate in launch services and satellite systems while ISRO continues advanced research and strategic missions.
