Current Affair #1

Independence Day 2026: Major Announcements by Prime Minister

During the Independence Day address on 15 August 2026, Prime Minister Narendra Modi highlighted several long-term development and technology goals for India.

What happened?

The address included targets related to artificial intelligence, nuclear energy, semiconductor manufacturing, sports development, energy infrastructure and the vision of a developed India by 2047.

Important Exam Facts

  • India's development vision is linked with Viksit Bharat 2047.
  • A target of 100 GW nuclear power capacity by 2047 was highlighted.
  • India also aims to strengthen semiconductor manufacturing.
  • AI skill development for young people was also emphasised.

Possible Question

Q. The term Viksit Bharat 2047 is associated with which goal?

Answer: India's long-term vision of becoming a developed nation by 2047.

Current Affair #3

Green India Mission: CAG Flags Major Shortfall in Targets

The Comptroller and Auditor General of India has highlighted a major shortfall in the implementation of the Green India Mission.

What happened?

The CAG's findings indicate that the mission achieved only a small fraction of its intended forest-cover increase during the period examined.

Important Numbers

  • The audit period covered 2015–16 to 2024–25.
  • The reported shortfall in the forest-cover target was around 98%.
  • The Green India Mission is one of the missions under India's National Action Plan on Climate Change.

Possible Question

Q. Green India Mission is associated with which broad area?

Answer: Forest and tree-cover enhancement, ecosystem restoration and climate-change mitigation.

Current Affair #4

CAG Flags Water Quality Problems at Railway Stations

A recent CAG audit of railway stations highlighted gaps in basic passenger amenities and sanitation facilities.

Key Finding

The audit covered 512 railway stations. The report noted that 458 stations lacked certain basic facilities and E. coli contamination was detected in water coolers at eight stations.

Why It Matters?

The issue is important from the perspective of public health, sanitation, railway infrastructure and passenger safety.

Possible Question

Q. Which constitutional authority audits government expenditure and public-sector functioning in India?

Answer: Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG).

Current Affair #5

Cauvery Water Dispute: Supreme Court Hearing

The Supreme Court is scheduled to hear Tamil Nadu's plea concerning the release of its share of Cauvery river water from Karnataka.

What is the issue?

Tamil Nadu has sought directions regarding the release of its due share of Cauvery water, while the matter involves the long-running inter-state water-sharing dispute between Karnataka and Tamil Nadu.

Exam Fact

The Cauvery River originates in Karnataka and flows through Karnataka and Tamil Nadu before reaching the Bay of Bengal through the Cauvery delta region.

Possible Question

Q. The Cauvery water dispute primarily involves which two states?

Answer: Karnataka and Tamil Nadu.

Current Affair #6

India Sets Cooking Gas Production Target Amid Global Energy Disruptions

India has introduced measures aimed at strengthening domestic cooking-gas availability amid disruptions in global energy markets linked to geopolitical tensions.

Important Fact

A government order has set a maximum daily production target of approximately 63,810 metric tonnes of cooking gas for state-run and private refineries.

Why It Matters?

The move is aimed at strengthening domestic supply, energy security and preparedness against international supply disruptions.

Possible Question

Q. The recent cooking-gas production target is mainly linked with which issue?

Answer: Domestic energy security and supply stability.

Current Affair #7

Mineral Exchange Rules 2026 Notified

The Government of India has notified the Mineral Exchange Rules, 2026, creating a regulatory framework for mineral exchanges.

What is a Mineral Exchange?

A mineral exchange is an electronic marketplace where eligible buyers and sellers can trade minerals and specified mineral products under a regulated framework.

Exam Facts

  • Rules: Mineral Exchange Rules, 2026
  • Ministry: Ministry of Mines
  • Regulatory authority: Indian Bureau of Mines
  • The framework focuses on transparent and regulated mineral trading.

Possible Question

Q. Which organisation is associated with regulatory oversight of mineral exchanges?

Answer: Indian Bureau of Mines (IBM).

Current Affair #8

Odisha Raises Concerns Over New Minerals Law

The debate over India's new minerals-related legal framework has also drawn attention from mineral-rich states, including Odisha.

Why in News?

Former Odisha Chief Minister Naveen Patnaik has raised concerns over provisions of the new minerals law and its possible impact on Odisha's interests, including state revenue and mineral resources.

Exam Relevance

This topic is useful for questions related to federalism, mineral resources, state revenue, mining policy and Centre-State relations.

Possible Question

Q. Odisha is particularly important in Indian economy because of its significance in which sector?

Answer: Mineral resources and mining.

Atal Bihari Vajpayee Remembered on His Death Anniversary

16 August marked the death anniversary of former Prime Minister Atal Bihari Vajpayee, who passed away on 16 August 2018.

Important Exam Facts

  • Born: 25 December 1924
  • Born in: Gwalior
  • Died: 16 August 2018
  • He served as Prime Minister of India in three separate terms.
  • He was awarded the Bharat Ratna in 2015.
  • His birthday, 25 December, is observed as Good Governance Day.

Possible Question

Q. Good Governance Day is observed on the birth anniversary of which former Prime Minister?

The India Meteorological Department has forecast widespread rainfall and episodes of heavy to very heavy rainfall in several parts of India during the ongoing monsoon season.

Exam Fact

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) is India's principal agency for meteorological observations, weather forecasting and related climate services.

Possible Question
