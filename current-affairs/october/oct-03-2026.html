Indian Railways raises speed limit on Katra–Banihal section to 100 kmph

The maximum permissible speed on the Katra–Banihal railway section has been increased from 75 kmph to 100 kmph. The development is significant for rail connectivity in Jammu and Kashmir and the wider Udhampur–Srinagar–Baramulla Rail Link network.

Exam Fact

The Katra–Banihal section is part of the important railway connectivity network in Jammu and Kashmir.

Answer: 100 kmph

  • Region: Jammu and Kashmir

The World Congress of Gastroenterology 2026 is being held in New Delhi from September 30 to October 3. This is the first time that the international congress is being hosted in India.

WCOG 2026 is being held in New Delhi and its theme is "Transforming Gastrointestinal Health, Preserving Nature" .

  • Theme: Transforming Gastrointestinal Health, Preserving Nature

Quick Revision

  • Focus: Wildlife protection and enforcement

5th Kautilya Economic Conclave begins in New Delhi

The 5th Kautilya Economic Conclave is being held in New Delhi from October 3 to October 5, 2026. The three-day event brings together policymakers, economists, academics, financial experts and international participants.

The theme of KEC 2026 is "Resilience in an Age of Flux" . Discussions cover economic resilience, global fragmentation, technology, trade, finance and India's economic priorities.

India considers lower import duties on selected pulses to improve domestic supplies

India is considering reducing import duties on some pulses to increase supplies and help manage food-price pressures. The proposal is being examined in the context of domestic production concerns and demand for pulses.

India is the world's largest producer, consumer and importer of pulses, making changes in pulse import policy important for food supply and prices.

Exam Fact

Pulses are an important source of plant-based protein and form a major part of India's agricultural and food-consumption system.

Possible Question

Answer: Pulses

Quick Revision

  • Commodity: Pulses
  • Policy focus: Import duties
  • Objective: Improve supply and manage prices
