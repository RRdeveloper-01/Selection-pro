News 01 · Infrastructure

Prime Minister to Visit Chennai for Development Projects Worth Over ₹13,000 Crore

The Prime Minister is scheduled to visit Chennai, Tamil Nadu, on 11 October 2026 to participate in development programmes covering transport, infrastructure, ports, petroleum and education. The projects announced by the Prime Minister's Office have a combined value of more than ₹13,000 crore.

Key Details

  • The programme includes the inauguration of the Poonamallee–Vadapalani stretch of Phase II of the Chennai Metro Rail project.
  • The Metro corridor includes a 3.7-kilometre double-decker structure.
  • The visit also includes projects related to national highways, ports and railway stations.
  • The Sudha Gopalakrishnan Brain Centre at IIT Madras is also scheduled to be dedicated to the nation.

Why Is It Important?

Infrastructure projects can improve urban mobility, strengthen transport connectivity and support economic activity. The Brain Centre initiative is also relevant to India's research capabilities in neuroscience and medical science.

Exam Fact

City: Chennai | State: Tamil Nadu | Metro: Chennai Metro Rail

Possible Question: The Poonamallee–Vadapalani stretch belongs to which urban transport system?
Answer: Chennai Metro Rail, Phase II.
One-liner: Chennai is scheduled to receive development projects worth more than ₹13,000 crore.

News 02 · Research and Innovation

Fourth All Science and Technology Clusters' Meet Reviews Innovation Initiatives in Chandigarh

The Fourth All Science and Technology Clusters' Meet was held on 9–10 October 2026 at Panjab University in Chandigarh. Principal Scientific Adviser to the Government of India, Professor Ajay Kumar Sood, chaired the meeting.

Main Highlights

  • The meeting reviewed the progress and plans of eight Science and Technology Clusters.
  • Discussions focused on collaboration between research institutions, universities, industry, government and startups.
  • Technology translation and the practical deployment of research outcomes were important areas of discussion.
  • The initiative aims to strengthen regional innovation ecosystems and support solutions to societal and economic challenges.

Why Is It Important?

Research becomes more useful when scientific discoveries are converted into practical products, services and public solutions. Collaboration among academia, industry and government can help bridge the gap between research and real-world application.

Exam Fact

Venue: Panjab University, Chandigarh.
Chair: Professor Ajay Kumar Sood, Principal Scientific Adviser to the Government of India.

Possible Question: What is a key objective of Science and Technology Clusters?
Answer: To strengthen research collaboration and translate scientific knowledge into practical solutions.
One-liner: The Fourth All S&T Clusters' Meet was held at Panjab University, Chandigarh, on 9–10 October 2026.

News 03 · Economic Policy

Fifth Kautilya Economic Conclave Concludes in New Delhi

The Fifth Kautilya Economic Conclave concluded successfully in New Delhi. It was organised by the Institute of Economic Growth in partnership with the Ministry of Finance, Government of India. The three-day event was held from 3 to 5 October 2026.

Theme and Discussions

The theme was “Resilience in an Age of Flux.” Discussions covered economic resilience, global fragmentation, technology, trade and India's economic priorities.

  • The conclave brought together policymakers, economists, academics and business leaders.
  • It included international participants from around 30 countries.
  • The event provided a platform to discuss economic challenges and policy responses.

Why Is It Important?

Economic resilience refers to the ability of an economy to withstand shocks and adapt to changing conditions. International trade, technological changes and global economic uncertainty make this subject relevant to economic policymaking.

Exam Fact

Event: Fifth Kautilya Economic Conclave (KEC 2026)
Theme: Resilience in an Age of Flux
Venue: New Delhi

Possible Question: What was the theme of the Fifth Kautilya Economic Conclave?
Answer: Resilience in an Age of Flux.
One-liner: KEC 2026 was organised by the Institute of Economic Growth in partnership with the Ministry of Finance.
News 04 · Corporate Governance

National Conference on Corporate Social Responsibility Concludes in New Delhi

The Third National Conference and Exhibition on Corporate Social Responsibility (NCCSR 2026) concluded in New Delhi on 10 October 2026. The conference was organised by the Indian Institute of Corporate Affairs under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.

Key Highlights

  • The conference theme was “Re-envisioning the Spirit of CSR for Viksit Bharat: From Compliance to Commitment.”
  • Discussions highlighted stronger governance, collaboration and outcome-oriented CSR.
  • The conference emphasised the role of CSR in supporting India's wider development objectives.

Why Is It Important?

Corporate Social Responsibility concerns the role of businesses in contributing to social and environmental objectives. For examinations, this topic connects corporate governance, business ethics and inclusive development.
