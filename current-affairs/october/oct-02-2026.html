🇮🇳 National News

India celebrates 157th Birth Anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi and 122nd Birth Anniversary of Lal Bahadur Shastri

On October 2, 2026, the nation honors the 157th birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi and the 122nd birth anniversary of India's second Prime Minister, Lal Bahadur Shastri. Prime Minister Narendra Modi paid tributes at Raj Ghat and Vijay Ghat in New Delhi.

The United Nations also observes October 2 as the International Day of Non-Violence in recognition of Mahatma Gandhi's philosophy of Ahimsa. Nationwide cleanliness drives, community service events, and cultural exhibitions were organized to mark the occasion.

Exam Fact

The UN General Assembly established the International Day of Non-Violence on June 15, 2007. Lal Bahadur Shastri famously gave the slogan "Jai Jawan Jai Kisan" during the 1965 Indo-Pak war.

Possible Question

Q. October 2 is commemorated internationally by the United Nations as which day?

Answer: International Day of Non-Violence

Swachh Bharat Diwas 2026 celebrated with the culmination of 'Swachhata Hi Seva' Campaign

The Ministry of Jal Shakti and the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs celebrated Swachh Bharat Diwas on October 2, 2026, marking the 12th anniversary of the launch of the Swachh Bharat Mission (launched on Oct 2, 2014).

The day marked the successful conclusion of the fortnightly nationwide campaign 'Swachhata Hi Seva 2026'. Awards were conferred upon top-performing Gram Panchayats, Urban Local Bodies, and sanitation workers (Swachhata Doots) for excellence in solid waste management and liquid waste processing.

Exam Fact

The Swachh Bharat Mission was officially launched on October 2, 2014. Swachh Bharat Mission-Urban 2.0 and SBM-Gramin Phase II aim for Garbage-Free Cities and ODF Plus status respectively.

Possible Question

Q. In which year was the Swachh Bharat Mission launched in India?

Answer: 2014

Quick Revision

  • Event: Swachh Bharat Diwas 2026
  • Culmination of: 'Swachhata Hi Seva' fortnight
  • SBM Launch Year: 2 October 2014
  • Focus: ODF Plus Sustainability & Waste Processing

Ministry of Jal Shakti launches 'Jal Sanchay Jan Bhagidari 2.0' for rainwater harvesting

On the occasion of Gandhi Jayanti, the Union Minister for Jal Shakti inaugurated the second phase of the 'Jal Sanchay Jan Bhagidari' initiative, expanding community-led rainwater harvesting recharge structures across 150 water-stressed districts in India.

The initiative combines artificial recharge structures, GIS-based mapping of local water bodies, and active involvement of self-help groups (SHGs) and local Panchayats to boost groundwater table levels prior to the retreating monsoon.

Exam Fact

The National Water Mission operates under the Ministry of Jal Shakti with the motto "Catch the Rain, Where it Falls, When it Falls".

Possible Question

Q. What is the core objective of the Jal Sanchay Jan Bhagidari initiative?

Answer: Community-led rainwater harvesting and groundwater recharge

Quick Revision

  • Scheme: Jal Sanchay Jan Bhagidari 2.0
  • Ministry: Ministry of Jal Shakti
  • Target: 150 water-stressed districts
  • Key Focus: GIS mapping & Artificial Groundwater Recharge

🌍 International News

UNGA Adopts Resolution on Global AI Governance Framework for Ethical AI Usage

The United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) has adopted a milestone resolution establishing the framework for the Global AI Safety Network. The initiative seeks to promote safe, secure, and trustworthy Artificial Intelligence systems worldwide while bridging the digital divide between developed and developing nations.

India welcomed the resolution, emphasizing the inclusion of the Global South in decision-making and advocating for open-source AI tools for public good.

Exam Fact

The United Nations Headquarters is located in New York, USA. The current UN Secretary-General is António Guterres.

Possible Question

Q. Where is the headquarters of the United Nations located?

Answer: New York City, USA

Quick Revision

  • Body: UN General Assembly (UNGA)
  • Key Resolution: Global AI Safety Network Framework
  • Objective: Ethical AI, digital equality, Global South representation
  • UN HQ: New York

India and Japan sign MoU on Sustainable Clean Energy Transit Systems

India and Japan have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) during the bilateral trade dialog in Tokyo to promote green hydrogen infrastructure and eco-friendly urban transit systems.

The partnership will facilitate technical technology transfers and long-term concessional financing from the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) for Metro expansion projects in India.

Exam Fact

Japan's capital is Tokyo, its currency is the Japanese Yen (JPY), and its prime minister's office works closely with India under the Special Strategic and Global Partnership.

Possible Question

Q. Which Japanese agency provides funding for major infrastructure and metro projects in India?

Answer: JICA (Japan International Cooperation Agency)

Quick Revision

  • Countries: India and Japan
  • MoU Domain: Clean Energy & Urban Transit
  • Financing Partner: JICA
  • Capital of Japan: Tokyo

💰 Economy & Business News

GST Collections for September 2026 reach ₹1.89 Lakh Crore showing robust 9.2% YoY Growth

Gross Goods and Services Tax (GST) collections for September 2026 (collected in October) reached ₹1,89,450 crore, registering a strong year-on-year growth of 9.2%.

The growth was primarily driven by heightened festival demand, domestic transactions, and improved tax compliance mechanisms implemented by the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC).

Exam Fact

GST was introduced in India via the 101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016 and came into effect on July 1, 2017.

Possible Question

Q. Which Constitutional Amendment Act implemented the Goods and Services Tax (GST) in India?

Answer: 101st Constitutional Amendment Act

Quick Revision

  • GST Revenue (Sept 2026): ₹1.89 Lakh Crore
  • YoY Growth Rate: 9.2%
  • GST Implementation Date: 1 July 2017
  • Administering Body: CBIC

SEBI introduces enhanced risk management guidelines for Index F&O Derivatives Segment

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has released strict guidelines for retail investors participating in Equity Index Futures and Options (F&O). The regulatory updates increase minimum contract sizes and mandate real-time margin monitoring by stockbrokers.

The step aims to safeguard retail investors from extreme market volatility and prevent systemic risk in financial markets.

Exam Fact

SEBI was established as a statutory body on April 12, 1992 under the SEBI Act, 1992. Its headquarters is located in Mumbai.

Possible Question

Q. Where is the headquarters of SEBI located?

Answer: Mumbai

Quick Revision

  • Regulator: SEBI
  • Sector: Capital Markets / F&O Derivatives
  • Objective: Retail investor protection & risk management
  • SEBI Statutory Status: 1992

🔬 Science & Technology

ISRO successfully tests Next-Gen Electric Propulsion System for Future Satellite Missions

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) successfully ground-tested its indigenous 300mN High-Thrust Stationary Plasma Thruster (SPT) designed for electric propulsion in geostationary satellites.

Electric propulsion drastically reduces the chemical fuel requirement, allowing communication satellites to carry heavier scientific payloads and increasing operational lifespans up to 20 years.

Exam Fact

ISRO was established on 15 August 1969. Its headquarters is in Bengaluru, Karnataka.

Possible Question

Q. What is the main advantage of Electric Propulsion Systems in satellites?

Answer: Reduces chemical propellant weight, increasing payload capacity and mission life.

Quick Revision

  • Agency: ISRO
  • Technology: High-Thrust Stationary Plasma Thruster (SPT)
  • Domain: Satellite Electric Propulsion
  • ISRO HQ: Bengaluru

IISc Bengaluru researchers develop low-cost Graphene-based Biosensor for Rapid Water Quality Testing

Scientists at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, have invented a portable, graphene-backed biosensor that detects heavy metals and bacterial pathogens in drinking water within 60 seconds.

The device uses microfluidic channels and electrical conductivity variations to spot contaminants like Lead, Arsenic, and E. coli at ultra-low concentrations.

Exam Fact

Graphene is an allotrope of carbon consisting of a single layer of atoms arranged in a two-dimensional hexagonal lattice. It is renowned for its exceptional electrical and thermal conductivity.

Possible Question

Q. Graphene is a two-dimensional allotrope of which chemical element?

Answer: Carbon

Quick Revision

  • Institute: IISc Bengaluru
  • Innovation: Graphene-based Water Quality Biosensor
  • Target Contaminants: Lead, Arsenic, E. coli
  • Detection Time: 60 Seconds
