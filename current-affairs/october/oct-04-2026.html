Great Indian Bustard rewilding begins at Desert National Park in Rajasthan

India has taken an important step in the conservation of the critically endangered Great Indian Bustard (GIB). The process for releasing captive-bred birds into the wild has been initiated at Desert National Park in Jaisalmer, Rajasthan.

The initiative is part of a decade-long conservation breeding and recovery programme involving the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Rajasthan Forest Department and Wildlife Institute of India.

Exam Fact

The Great Indian Bustard is the State Bird of Rajasthan and is a critically endangered grassland bird.

Possible Question

Q. The Great Indian Bustard is the State Bird of which Indian state?

Answer: Rajasthan

Quick Revision

  • Species: Great Indian Bustard
  • Status: Critically endangered
  • State Bird: Rajasthan
  • Rewilding site: Desert National Park
  • Location: Jaisalmer, Rajasthan

Five Centres of Excellence approved for Classical Languages

The Ministry of Education has approved five Centres of Excellence for studies in Classical Assamese, Classical Bengali, Classical Marathi, Classical Pali and Classical Prakrit.

The centres will focus on academic research, preservation, digitisation and wider dissemination of India's linguistic and literary heritage.

Exam Fact

The five centres are located across Assam, West Bengal, Maharashtra, Odisha and Gujarat.

Possible Question

Q. Which five Classical Languages were included in the newly approved Centres of Excellence?

Answer: Assamese, Bengali, Marathi, Pali and Prakrit

Quick Revision

  • Classical Assamese — Tezpur University
  • Classical Bengali — Visva-Bharati University
  • Classical Marathi — Deccan College, Pune
  • Classical Pali — Central University of Odisha
  • Classical Prakrit — Central University of Gujarat

Jal Arpan strengthens community ownership of rural water systems

Under Jal Jeevan Mission 2.0, the Jal Arpan initiative has been used to strengthen community ownership of completed rural drinking-water supply schemes.

Special Gram Sabha activities were conducted across more than 1,850 Gram Panchayats, with emphasis on local participation, water-quality monitoring and long-term maintenance of rural water infrastructure.

Jal Arpan is associated with Jal Jeevan Mission 2.0 and community management of rural water systems.

Green Energy Corridor Phase-III gets ₹1.86 lakh crore outlay

The Union Cabinet has approved the Green Energy Corridor Phase-III scheme to strengthen India's intra-state transmission system and support the integration of renewable energy into the power grid.

The scheme has a total project outlay of ₹1,86,405 crore and is designed to enable evacuation of up to 135 GW of renewable energy.

Exam Fact

GEC-III also provides for deployment of 50 GWh of Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS).

Possible Question

Answer: ₹1,86,405 crore

  • Outlay: ₹1,86,405 crore
  • Renewable power evacuation: Up to 135 GW
  • Battery storage: 50 GWh
  • Focus: Intra-state transmission system

India demonstrates first 5.56 km free-space Quantum Key Distribution link

India has demonstrated its first free-space Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) link over a distance of 5.56 km.

The demonstration was carried out by QNu Labs in collaboration with BISAG-N and IIT Gandhinagar. The field trial established a secure quantum communication channel and generated secure keys for encrypted communication.

Exam Fact

QKD is a technology that uses principles of quantum mechanics to establish secure cryptographic keys.

Possible Question

Q. Over what distance did India demonstrate its first free-space QKD link?

Answer: 5.56 km

Quick Revision

  • Technology: Quantum Key Distribution
  • Distance: 5.56 km
  • Partners: QNu Labs, BISAG-N and IIT Gandhinagar
  • Purpose: Secure quantum communication
  • Related concept: Post-quantum cryptography

Asian Games 2026: India finishes with 85 medals, including 21 gold

India ended its Asian Games 2026 campaign in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, with a total of 85 medals, including 21 gold medals.

India's campaign featured strong performances across wrestling, shooting, athletics, archery, hockey, cricket and other sports. The 85-medal haul became India's second-highest total at an Asian Games, behind the 106 medals won at Hangzhou 2022.

Exam Fact

The 2026 Asian Games were hosted by Aichi-Nagoya, Japan.

Possible Question

Q. How many medals did India win at the 2026 Asian Games?

Answer: 85 medals

Quick Revision

  • Event: Asian Games 2026
  • Host: Aichi-Nagoya, Japan
  • India's total medals: 85
  • Gold medals: 21
  • Previous Indian record: 106 medals at Hangzhou 2022
