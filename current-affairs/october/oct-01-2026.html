UPSC completes 100 years of its journey as a constitutional public service institution

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) completes 100 years on October 1, 2026. The institution was originally established on October 1, 1926, as the Public Service Commission and later evolved into the Union Public Service Commission under the constitutional framework of independent India.

The centenary programme is being held at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi. The programme includes the release of a commemorative coin, stamp and a book titled “100 Landmark Judgements – Shaping the Policies of UPSC and Public Service Commissions in India.”

Exam Fact

UPSC was established on 1 October 1926. It is a constitutional body responsible for conducting major recruitment examinations for All India and Central Services.

Possible Question

Q. On which date was the Public Service Commission established in India?

Answer: 1 October 1926

Quick Revision

  • UPSC established: 1 October 1926
  • UPSC completed 100 years in 2026
  • Centenary event: Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi
  • UPSC is a constitutional body

Green Energy Corridor Phase-III approved to strengthen renewable-energy transmission

The Union Cabinet approved the Green Energy Corridor Phase-III scheme to strengthen intra-state transmission systems and support the evacuation of renewable energy.

The scheme is designed to enable evacuation of up to 135 GW of renewable energy and includes deployment of 50 GWh of Battery Energy Storage Systems.

Exam Fact

Green Energy Corridor Phase-III has a total project outlay of approximately ₹1.86 lakh crore and is targeted for completion by FY 2032-33.

Possible Question

Q. Green Energy Corridor Phase-III aims to enable evacuation of how much renewable energy?

Answer: Up to 135 GW

Quick Revision

  • Scheme: Green Energy Corridor Phase-III
  • Renewable-energy evacuation: 135 GW
  • Battery storage: 50 GWh
  • Target: FY 2032-33

💰 Economy & Business News

Government increases MSP for all mandated Rabi crops for Marketing Season 2027-28

The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs approved higher Minimum Support Prices (MSP) for all mandated Rabi crops for the 2027-28 marketing season.

The highest absolute increase is for safflower at ₹675 per quintal, followed by rapeseed & mustard at ₹413 per quintal and lentil (masur) at ₹390 per quintal.

The MSP of wheat has been increased by ₹25 per quintal to ₹2,610 per quintal.

Exam Fact

For Rabi Marketing Season 2027-28, wheat MSP is ₹2,610 per quintal and safflower received the highest absolute MSP increase of ₹675 per quintal.

Possible Question

Q. What is the MSP of wheat approved for Rabi Marketing Season 2027-28?

Answer: ₹2,610 per quintal

Quick Revision

  • Marketing Season: 2027-28
  • Wheat MSP: ₹2,610/quintal
  • Safflower increase: ₹675/quintal
  • Rapeseed & Mustard increase: ₹413/quintal
  • Lentil increase: ₹390/quintal

New CAFE norms notified for passenger vehicles from April 2027

The government has notified new Corporate Average Fuel Economy (CAFE) norms for passenger vehicles. The new framework will apply from 1 April 2027 to 31 March 2032.

The framework progressively tightens fuel-efficiency targets and is intended to encourage cleaner and more energy-efficient vehicle technologies.

Exam Fact

The notified framework targets an improvement of around 16.7% in the fuel-consumption benchmark over the five-year period.

Possible Question

Q. The new CAFE norms for passenger vehicles will come into effect from which date?

Answer: 1 April 2027

Quick Revision

  • Policy: CAFE Norms
  • Sector: Passenger vehicles
  • Effective: 1 April 2027
  • Applicable until: 31 March 2032
  • Fuel-efficiency improvement target: about 16.7%

🔬 Science & Technology

CHIME telescope detects the faint glow of hydrogen from about five billion years ago

An international research collaboration involving researchers from India has reported a standalone detection of the faint radio glow of hydrogen from the distant universe using the Canadian Hydrogen Intensity Mapping Experiment (CHIME) telescope.

The observation is significant because hydrogen intensity mapping can help scientists study the large-scale distribution of matter and understand cosmic expansion and dark energy.

Exam Fact

CHIME stands for Canadian Hydrogen Intensity Mapping Experiment. Researchers from India's Raman Research Institute are part of the collaboration.

Possible Question

Q. CHIME is primarily associated with the study of which cosmic signal?

Answer: Hydrogen's radio glow through intensity mapping.

Quick Revision

  • Instrument: CHIME telescope
  • Country: Canada
  • Indian institute involved: Raman Research Institute
  • Focus: Hydrogen intensity mapping
  • Applications: Cosmic expansion and dark energy studies
