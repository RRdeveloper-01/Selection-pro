Two Great Indian Bustards released into the wild at Desert National Park

Union Environment Minister Bhupender Yadav released two captive-bred male Great Indian Bustards into the wild at Desert National Park in Jaisalmer, Rajasthan, marking an important step in India's Great Indian Bustard conservation and rewilding programme.

The release is part of Project Great Indian Bustard and aims to strengthen the recovery of the critically endangered grassland species through captive breeding, habitat protection and carefully managed reintroduction.

Exam Fact

The Great Indian Bustard is the State Bird of Rajasthan and is classified as Critically Endangered.

Possible Question

Answer: Desert National Park, Jaisalmer, Rajasthan.
