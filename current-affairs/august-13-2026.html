Current Affair #01

Deepak Dhar Wins the 2026 Dirac Medal

Science & Technology

Indian theoretical physicist Professor Deepak Dhar has been honoured with the prestigious 2026 Dirac Medal for his contributions to theoretical physics, particularly statistical mechanics.

The award is associated with the International Centre for Theoretical Physics (ICTP) and is one of the important international recognitions in theoretical physics.

📌 Exam Point

Person: Deepak Dhar
Award: 2026 Dirac Medal
Field: Theoretical Physics / Statistical Mechanics

Current Affair #02

N. Chandrasekaran to Step Down as Tata Sons Chairman

Corporate Affairs

N. Chandrasekaran, Chairman of Tata Sons, has announced that he will not seek another term after his current tenure ends in February 2027.

Chandrasekaran became Chairman of Tata Sons in 2017 and has led the Tata Group through expansion in sectors including aviation, electronics, semiconductors, digital businesses and new-age energy.

📌 Important Fact

N. Chandrasekaran has served as Chairman of Tata Sons since 2017. His current term is scheduled to end in February 2027.
