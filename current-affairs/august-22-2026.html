08 SCIENCE & SPACE

NASA Calls Off Rescue Mission for Swift Space Telescope

NASA and its partners have called off an attempt to rescue the ageing Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory.

The rescue effort involved the Swift Boost Mission, which aimed to raise the observatory's orbit and extend its scientific life.

The rescue mission was abandoned after problems with the rescue spacecraft's attitude-control system.

Why It Matters

The development is relevant to space science and astronomy current affairs. The Swift Observatory is known for studying gamma-ray bursts and other high-energy astronomical events.

Exam Fact

Space Observatory: Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory

Agency: NASA

Possible Question

Which NASA space observatory was the subject of the recently abandoned rescue mission?

Answer: Neil Gehrels Swift Observatory
