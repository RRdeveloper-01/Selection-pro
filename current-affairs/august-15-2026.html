Current Affair #1

India Celebrates Its 80th Independence Day

What Happened?

India is celebrating its 80th Independence Day on 15 August 2026. The day marks 79 completed years since India became independent from British rule on 15 August 1947.

The distinction is important: India completed 79 years of independence, while the 2026 observance is officially the 80th Independence Day.

Why It Matters

Independence Day is a frequently asked topic in General Awareness, History and Indian Polity examinations. The 2026 celebrations also carry significance because of the focus on India's development journey and the vision of Viksit Bharat.

Exam Fact

Independence Day: 15 August
First Independence Day: 15 August 1947
2026: 80th Independence Day

Possible Question

Q. Which Independence Day is India celebrating in 2026?

Answer: 80th Independence Day.

Current Affair #2

1,057 Gallantry and Service Medals Announced Ahead of Independence Day

What Happened?

On the eve of Independence Day, the Government of India announced 1,057 Gallantry and Service Medals for personnel serving in the police, fire services, Home Guard and Civil Defence, and correctional services.

Why It Matters

The awards recognise exceptional bravery, distinguished service and dedication among personnel responsible for public safety and internal security.

Exam Fact

Total Gallantry and Service Medals announced: 1,057

Possible Question

Q. How many Gallantry and Service Medals were announced ahead of India's Independence Day in 2026?

Answer: 1,057.

Current Affair #3

President Approves 78 Gallantry Awards

What Happened?

President Droupadi Murmu approved 78 Gallantry Awards on the eve of India's 80th Independence Day.

The awards recognise acts of bravery and exceptional service by personnel of the Defence Forces and Central Armed Police Forces. The list includes 13 posthumous awards.

Why It Matters

Gallantry awards are important for questions related to Indian defence, national security, awards and important constitutional authorities.

Exam Fact

President: Droupadi Murmu
Gallantry Awards approved: 78
Posthumous awards: 13

Possible Question

Q. Who approved the 78 Gallantry Awards announced on the eve of Independence Day 2026?

Answer: President Droupadi Murmu.

Current Affair #4

RBI to Close FCNR(B) Forex Swap Facility Early

What Happened?

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) decided to close its discounted foreign-exchange swap facility for banks to hedge overseas deposits from non-resident Indians earlier than initially planned.

The deadline was moved from 30 September 2026 to 31 August 2026, following strong foreign currency inflows.

The facility had attracted more than $50 billion in inflows, including substantial FCNR(B) deposits.

Why It Matters

This development is important for Banking and Economy sections of competitive examinations. It also highlights the role of foreign currency deposits and RBI's liquidity and foreign-exchange management.

Exam Fact

Institution: Reserve Bank of India
Revised closure date: 31 August 2026
Major inflow: More than $50 billion

Possible Question

Q. Which institution announced the early closure of the discounted forex swap facility?

Answer: Reserve Bank of India (RBI).

Current Affair #5

India Wins Maiden Overall Commonwealth Fencing Championship Title

What Happened?

India won its maiden overall title at the Commonwealth Fencing Championships 2026 held in Lagos, Nigeria.

India finished the championship with a total of 35 medals, including 13 gold, 8 silver and 14 bronze medals.

India also won the Wilkinson Sword Trophy for the best overall performance.

Why It Matters

The achievement is important for sports-related questions and shows India's growing performance in international fencing competitions.

Exam Fact

Event: Commonwealth Fencing Championships 2026
Host city: Lagos, Nigeria
India's medals: 35
Gold: 13

Possible Question

Q. Which trophy did India win for the best overall performance at the Commonwealth Fencing Championships 2026?

Answer: Wilkinson Sword Trophy.

Current Affair #6

India-US Navy Conducts EOD Exercise 2026

What Happened?

The Indian Navy and US Navy conducted a five-day Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) Exercise 2026 at Kochi.

The exercise included expert exchanges, cross-training sessions, equipment demonstrations and scenario-based practical drills. The exercise concluded on 14 August 2026.

Why It Matters

India-US defence exercises are important for understanding strategic cooperation, interoperability and maritime security.

Exam Fact

Exercise: EOD Exercise 2026
Participants: Indian Navy and US Navy
Location: Kochi
Duration: Five days

Possible Question

Q. The India-US Navy EOD Exercise 2026 was conducted at which Indian city?

Answer: Kochi.

Current Affair #8

Neeraj Chopra and Pullela Gopichand Launch Coaching Excellence Initiative

What Happened?

Olympic and world champion Neeraj Chopra and renowned badminton coach Pullela Gopichand joined hands to launch the Indian School of Coaching Excellence (ISCE).

The initiative aims to develop a stronger pool of Indian sports coaches and improve coaching standards in the country.

Why It Matters

Quality coaching is an important part of India's long-term sports development. The initiative focuses not only on athletes but also on building professional coaching capacity.

Exam Fact

Initiative: Indian School of Coaching Excellence
Key figures: Neeraj Chopra and Pullela Gopichand

Possible Question

Q. Who collaborated to launch the Indian School of Coaching Excellence?

Answer: Neeraj Chopra and Pullela Gopichand.

Current Affair #9

BIRAC Showcases Biotech and Medtech Startup Ecosystem

What Happened?

The Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council (BIRAC) showcased India's biotechnology and medical technology startup ecosystem during the Bharatiya Vyapar Mahotsav 2026 at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi.

The event highlighted innovations involving areas such as robotics, assistive technology, prosthetics, rehabilitation and healthcare.

Why It Matters

BIRAC plays an important role in supporting biotechnology innovation, entrepreneurship, funding, incubation and technology development in India.

Exam Fact

Full Form: Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council
Sector: Biotechnology and Medtech
Event: Bharatiya Vyapar Mahotsav 2026
Venue: Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi

Possible Question

Q. BIRAC is associated primarily with which sector?

Answer: Biotechnology research, innovation and entrepreneurship.

Current Affair #10

Government Seeks Applications for Two CCI Members

What Happened?

The Government of India invited applications for the appointment of two new members of the Competition Commission of India (CCI).

The appointments are aimed at ensuring that the competition regulator has adequate strength to carry out its statutory functions and maintain the required quorum.

Why It Matters

CCI is an important statutory body associated with competition law, anti-competitive practices, mergers and market competition in India. This topic can be useful for Economy, Polity and Governance sections.

Exam Fact

Organisation: Competition Commission of India (CCI)
Appointments proposed: 2 members
Related area: Competition regulation

Possible Question

Q. CCI is primarily responsible for regulating which area?

Answer: Competition in the Indian market and anti-competitive practices.
