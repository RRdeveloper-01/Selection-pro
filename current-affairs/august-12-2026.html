Current Affair 01

Lok Sabha Passes Kerala Alteration of Name Bill, 2026

What happened?

The Lok Sabha passed the Kerala Alteration of Name Bill, 2026, which seeks to change the official name of the state from Kerala to Keralam.

The Bill was passed by voice vote without a formal discussion amid Opposition protests.

Exam Point

Proposed new name: Keralam
Bill: Kerala Alteration of Name Bill, 2026
House: Lok Sabha

Current Affair 02

India Adds 11 New International Entry Points for E-Visa

India has added 11 new international entry points for eligible foreign nationals holding valid Indian e-visas.

The expansion includes nine land ports and two airports. The newly included airports are Bhopal and Tirupati.

Following the expansion, India now has a total of 88 designated ports for e-visa entry.

Important Fact

The expansion is aimed at improving ease of entry for eligible international travellers, including tourism and business visitors.

Current Affair 03

7.4-Magnitude Earthquake Strikes Western Colombia

Western Colombia was struck by a powerful 7.4-magnitude earthquake. The earthquake caused major destruction and triggered rescue operations.

Reports on 12 August said that more than 100 people had died, with the reported toll rising as rescue teams continued operations.

Remember

Country: Colombia
Earthquake magnitude: 7.4
Region: Western Colombia

Current Affair 04

US to Make Several Immigration Applications Online-Only

The United States government is moving towards mandatory online filing for several immigration applications.

The changes cover applications related to areas such as green cards, citizenship and asylum.

The policy is linked to a new rule from the US Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS).

Exam Point

US agency: USCIS
Major change: Online filing for several immigration applications

Current Affair 06

RPSG to Acquire 1.4 GW Operating Solar Portfolio

The RP-Sanjiv Goenka Group (RPSG) announced that it is set to acquire a 1.4 GW operating solar power portfolio from ReNew Solar Power.

The reported value of the transaction is approximately ₹4,859 crore.

The portfolio consists of six solar projects located in Rajasthan and Karnataka.

Exam Point

Capacity: 1.4 GW
Value: ₹4,859 crore
Projects: Rajasthan and Karnataka

Current Affair 08

India Wins One Gold and Three Bronze Medals at IOL 2026

The Indian team performed strongly at the International Linguistics Olympiad (IOL) 2026 held in Romania.

India won one gold medal and three bronze medals.

Shrilakshmi Venkatraman won the gold medal. Aarav Anil Rao, Nishanth Shankar Lakshmanan and Advay Misra won bronze medals.

Current Affair 10

Total Solar Eclipse Occurs on 12 August 2026

A total solar eclipse occurs on 12 August 2026. During a total solar eclipse, the Moon passes between the Earth and the Sun and completely blocks the Sun for observers within the path of totality.

According to NASA, the path of totality crosses areas including Greenland, Iceland, the Atlantic region and northern Spain.

The total eclipse is not visible from India.

Science & Geography Fact

Event: Total Solar Eclipse
Date: 12 August 2026
Major visibility: Greenland, Iceland and northern Spain
India: Total eclipse not visible
