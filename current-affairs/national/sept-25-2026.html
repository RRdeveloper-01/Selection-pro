1. Second Saraighat Rail-Cum-Road Bridge Project

What happened: The Ministry of Railways is set to begin construction of the Second Saraighat Rail-Cum-Road Bridge in Assam along with doubling of the Agthori-Kamakhya railway track.

Why it matters: The project is important for connectivity and railway capacity in the Northeast.

Exam Fact: Saraighat Bridge is located over the Brahmaputra River near Guwahati.

What happened: The Bihar Public Service Commission has opened applications for 32,388 school-teacher posts under Teacher Recruitment Examination 4.0 from 25 September 2026.

Why it matters: The recruitment is an important education and government-job development for teaching candidates.

Exam Fact: BPSC stands for Bihar Public Service Commission.
