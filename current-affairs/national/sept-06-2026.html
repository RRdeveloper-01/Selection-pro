1. Teachers' Day 2026: PM Modi interacts with National Award-winning teachers

India observed Teachers' Day on 5 September, the birth anniversary of Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan. Prime Minister Narendra Modi interacted with National Teachers' Award winners and highlighted the contribution of teachers to students and nation-building.

Exam Point: Teachers' Day in India is observed on 5 September in honour of Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan, India's first Vice-President and second President.

2. UGC-NET June 2026 re-examination issue reaches Delhi High Court

The Delhi High Court has sought responses from the Centre and the National Testing Agency on a petition challenging the re-conduct of the UGC-NET June 2026 English examination. The development is relevant for students because UGC-NET is a major national eligibility examination for teaching and research.

Exam Point: UGC-NET is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA).

3. NMMSS 2026-27 scholarship application deadline extended

The Ministry of Education has extended the application deadline for the National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS) 2026-27 to 30 September 2026. The scheme is important in education current affairs because it supports meritorious students from economically weaker sections to continue secondary education.

Exam Point: NMMSS is a scholarship scheme of the Ministry of Education and is designed to reduce the dropout rate after Class VIII.

4. Vice-President C. P. Radhakrishnan's recent official engagements

Recent official releases from the Vice-President's Secretariat include engagements in Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh and a scheduled visit to Chennai. Such constitutional-office updates are useful for interviews and competitive exams.
