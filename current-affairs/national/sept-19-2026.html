1. 20th Rozgar Mela to distribute over 51,000 appointment letters

What happened: Prime Minister Narendra Modi is scheduled to distribute more than 51,000 appointment letters to newly appointed youth on 19 September through the 20th Rozgar Mela.

Why it matters: The recruits will join departments including Railways, Revenue, Home Affairs, Health, Defence and Financial Services.

Source: PIB

2. International Conference on Future of Environment and Climate Dynamics begins

What happened: The National Green Tribunal is organising a two-day international conference on September 19 and 20 at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi.

Why it matters: Judicial representatives from 17 countries and Indian environmental institutions are expected to participate.

Source: PIB

3. SEMICON India 2026 enters its final day

What happened: SEMICON India 2026 is being held from September 17 to 19 at Yashobhoomi, New Delhi, bringing together global semiconductor companies, policymakers and researchers.

Why it matters: The event covers semiconductor design, manufacturing, advanced packaging, R&D, supply chains and talent development.

Source: PIB

4. NTA invites EoIs for examination-related work

What happened: The National Testing Agency has invited Expressions of Interest for question-paper setting, translation and confidential examination-related work.

Why it matters: The development is relevant to India's competitive-examination ecosystem and national test administration.

Source: Hindustan Times

5. NEET-MDS 2026 Round 2 counselling registration opens

What happened: The Medical Counselling Committee has opened registration for NEET-MDS 2026 Round 2 counselling, according to recent education reporting.

Why it matters: The update is relevant for postgraduate dental admissions and students tracking national counselling.

Source: Times of India
