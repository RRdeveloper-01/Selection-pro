1. ISRO Successfully Launches EOS-05 Earth Observation Satellite

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) successfully launched the EOS-05 Earth Observation Satellite aboard the GSLV-F17 launch vehicle on 4 September 2026.

The launch took place from the Second Launch Pad at Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota, Andhra Pradesh. ISRO confirmed that GSLV-F17 successfully placed EOS-05 into its intended Sub-Geosynchronous Transfer Orbit (Sub-GTO).

EOS-05 is a state-of-the-art Earth observation spacecraft and is India's first imaging satellite designed to operate from a geosynchronous orbit.

Why is this important? The mission strengthens India's satellite-based Earth observation capabilities and represents an important milestone for the country's space programme.

Satellite EOS-05
Launch Vehicle GSLV-F17
Launch Location Sriharikota, Andhra Pradesh
Satellite Type Earth Observation

3. India Reaffirms Support for Dialogue and Diplomacy to Resolve Russia-Ukraine Conflict

External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar has reiterated India's position that the Russia-Ukraine conflict should be addressed through dialogue and diplomacy.

During his visit to Ukraine, Jaishankar highlighted India's willingness to contribute to efforts aimed at finding a path towards peace.

India has consistently maintained that lasting solutions to major conflicts should come through diplomatic engagement rather than military action.

Key takeaway: India's approach focuses on dialogue, diplomacy and negotiations as the preferred route towards resolving the conflict.

5. India's Space Programme Gains Momentum with GSLV-F17 Mission

The successful GSLV-F17/EOS-05 mission is also significant in the wider context of India's expanding space programme.

GSLV-F17 is the 19th flight of India's Geosynchronous Satellite Launch Vehicle (GSLV). The mission successfully demonstrated the launch vehicle's ability to place the EOS-05 spacecraft into its planned orbit.

EOS-05 is designed for Earth observation and represents an important addition to India's imaging capabilities from geosynchronous orbit.

Why is this important? India's space missions frequently form part of Current Affairs and General Awareness questions. The EOS-05 mission is therefore important for students preparing for competitive examinations.

  • Organisation: ISRO
  • Vehicle: GSLV-F17
  • Satellite: EOS-05
  • Application: Earth Observation
  • Launch Site: Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota
