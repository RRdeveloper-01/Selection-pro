National Current Affairs 7 September 2026: Important India & Education News

Education, governance, internal security, weather and important national developments selected for competitive exams and student interviews.

Published: 7 September 2026 · Exam-focused current affairs.

1. PM Modi addresses students at Shri Ram College of Commerce

Prime Minister Narendra Modi addressed students at Shri Ram College of Commerce (SRCC), discussing India's economic strength, policy dynamism and the country's development journey. The interaction is relevant to student interviews and questions on higher education and India's economic transformation.

Exam Point: SRCC is a constituent college of the University of Delhi.

2. India backs UN resolution on equal-area world map representation

India voted in favour of a UN General Assembly resolution promoting more equitable cartographic representation. The Ministry of External Affairs reiterated India's position that its official map should be followed for Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh.

Exam Point: The resolution concerns equal-area map projections and does not itself settle political boundaries.

3. NIA special court convicts ten accused in Jhiram Valley Maoist attack case

A National Investigation Agency special court convicted all ten accused in the Jhiram Valley Maoist attack case. The development is relevant to internal security, NIA and Left-Wing Extremism questions.

4. IMD forecasts heavy rainfall in several states

The India Meteorological Department forecast heavy rainfall in several states and parts of the northeast. This is useful for questions on monsoon, extreme weather and disaster management.

5. Research and innovation must reach citizens and markets

Union Minister of State Dr Jitendra Singh stressed converting research outcomes into useful technologies and products that can reach citizens and markets.

Quick Revision: Most Important Exam Points

  • SRCC is a constituent college of the University of Delhi.
  • India voted in favour of the UN equal-area cartographic resolution.
  • The Jhiram Valley attack occurred in Chhattisgarh in 2013.
  • NIA is India's national investigation agency for scheduled offences.
  • IMD stands for India Meteorological Department.
