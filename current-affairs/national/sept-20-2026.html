1. Rashtriya Sankalp Samaroh to Mobilise Youth Across 1,067 Locations

What happened: A nationwide Rashtriya Sankalp Samaroh is being organised on 20 September 2026 across 1,067 locations through MY Bharat and partner organisations. The programme is part of the Nasha Mukt Yuva for Viksit Bharat – 100 Weeks Nasha Mukt Yuva Abhiyan.

Why it matters: The nationwide mobilisation focuses on youth participation, public awareness and community engagement through a collective pledge and signature campaign.

Exam Fact: MY Bharat is a youth-focused government platform aimed at providing young people opportunities for participation and engagement.

2. NIOS Launches Six-Month Bridge Course for Primary Teachers

What happened: The Ministry of Education has introduced a six-month Certificate Bridge Course in Primary Teacher Education through the National Institute of Open Schooling for eligible in-service primary teachers holding a B.Ed degree.

Why it matters: The programme is designed to strengthen teaching competencies for Classes 1 to 5, with emphasis on child-centred, inclusive and developmentally appropriate education.

Exam Fact: NIOS stands for National Institute of Open Schooling and functions under the Ministry of Education.

3. Southwest Monsoon Begins Its Retreat From Northwest India

What happened: The India Meteorological Department confirmed the beginning of southwest monsoon withdrawal from parts of west Rajasthan around the normal withdrawal period.

Why it matters: The monsoon remains important for India's agriculture, water resources, food prices and rural economy. Weather conditions also influence several economic sectors.

Exam Fact: The southwest monsoon is India's principal seasonal rainfall system and plays a major role in the country's hydrological cycle.

4. Low-Pressure System Develops Over North Andaman Sea

What happened: A low-pressure system has formed over the North Andaman Sea and is expected to strengthen while moving towards the eastern coast, with rainfall activity forecast across several eastern and central parts of India.

Why it matters: Such weather systems can influence rainfall distribution, agriculture, transport and disaster preparedness across affected states.

Exam Fact: A low-pressure area is a region where atmospheric pressure is lower than surrounding areas and can sometimes intensify into a depression or cyclone.

5. International Conference on Environment and Climate Dynamics Held in New Delhi

What happened: The International Conference on “The Future of Environment and Climate Dynamics” was held in New Delhi with participation from national and international stakeholders. The conference focused on collaborative action on environmental and climate challenges.

Why it matters: Climate change, environmental governance, sustainable development and climate justice are important areas of India's national policy and international commitments.

Exam Fact: The National Green Tribunal is a specialised judicial body dealing with environmental protection and related legal matters in India.
