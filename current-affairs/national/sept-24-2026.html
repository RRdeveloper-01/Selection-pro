1. MY Bharat Crosses 3 Crore Registered Youth

What happened: MY Bharat, the Government of India's youth engagement platform, has crossed the milestone of 3 crore registered youth since its launch in 2023.

Why it matters: The platform connects young people with experiential learning, volunteering and youth-development opportunities across India. It is aimed at increasing youth participation in nation-building activities.

Exam Fact: MY Bharat is an initiative under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports.

2. JMI Introduces One-Year PG Programmes in 13 Disciplines

What happened: Jamia Millia Islamia has introduced one-year postgraduate programmes in 13 disciplines through Open and Distance Learning and Online Learning modes.

Why it matters: The initiative expands flexible higher-education opportunities and is linked with the provisions of the National Education Policy 2020 and applicable UGC regulations.

Exam Fact: Jamia Millia Islamia is a Central University located in New Delhi.

3. RBI Approves Public Sector Banks to Operate on Sunday

What happened: The Reserve Bank of India has approved Public Sector Banks and Regional Rural Banks to remain fully operational on Sunday, 27 September 2026.

Why it matters: The decision has been taken ahead of the proposed three-day nationwide bank strike scheduled from 28 to 30 September, helping maintain continuity of essential banking services.

Exam Fact: Regional Rural Banks were established to improve banking and credit access in rural areas.

4. Deep Depression Brings Heavy Rain to Odisha and Andhra Pradesh

What happened: A deep depression over the Bay of Bengal is affecting coastal areas of Odisha and Andhra Pradesh, with the India Meteorological Department warning of heavy rainfall and strong winds.

Why it matters: Severe weather conditions can affect transport, schools, agriculture, infrastructure and disaster-management operations.

Exam Fact: The India Meteorological Department, or IMD, is India's national meteorological service.

5. 72nd National Film Awards Highlight Indian Cinema

What happened: The 72nd National Film Awards have brought national attention to achievements across Indian cinema and recognised outstanding contributions by film professionals.

Why it matters: The National Film Awards are among India's major national recognitions for excellence in cinema and culture.

Exam Fact: The National Film Awards were instituted in 1954.
