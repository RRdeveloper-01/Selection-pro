1. SEMICON India 2026 Begins in New Delhi

Prime Minister Narendra Modi is scheduled to inaugurate the fifth edition of SEMICON India 2026 at Yashobhoomi in New Delhi on September 17.

The three-day event, being held from September 17 to 19, focuses on India's semiconductor and electronics ecosystem, including investment, manufacturing, technology partnerships and supply-chain opportunities.

India News Exam Point: SEMICON India 2026 — 5th edition; venue — Yashobhoomi, New Delhi; dates — September 17–19, 2026.

2. Government Raises EPF Wage Ceiling to ₹25,000

The government has raised the wage ceiling used for mandatory EPF coverage from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month.

The move expands the coverage base of the Employees' Provident Fund framework and is an important development for India's formal employment and social-security system.

National News Exam Point: EPF wage ceiling — increased from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month.

3. Swachhata Hi Seva 2026 Campaign Begins

The Swachhata Hi Seva 2026 campaign begins across the country on September 17.

The campaign focuses on cleanliness, public participation and creating greater awareness around sanitation and waste management.

India Current Affairs Exam Point: Swachhata Hi Seva 2026 begins on September 17, 2026.

4. India Strengthens Engagement with Nepal

India's outgoing Ambassador to Nepal, Naveen Srivastava, met Nepal's Foreign Minister Shisir Khanal and discussed strengthening multifaceted bilateral relations.

India-Nepal cooperation includes areas such as connectivity, energy, trade, development and people-to-people relations.

National & International Exam Point: India and Nepal continue diplomatic engagement to strengthen bilateral cooperation.

5. Education News: UP Super TET 2026 Opens Applications

The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission has started the application process for UP Super TET 2026.

The recruitment notification includes 12,405 Assistant Teacher vacancies. The application process opened on September 16, 2026.

The recruitment is an important education and employment update for candidates preparing for teaching examinations in Uttar Pradesh.

Education News Exam Point: UP Super TET 2026 — 12,405 Assistant Teacher vacancies.
