1. BRICS New Delhi Declaration adopted at 18th Summit

The 18th BRICS Summit is being held in New Delhi under India's chairship. BRICS leaders adopted the New Delhi Declaration, covering major issues related to global governance, international cooperation, peace, trade and sustainable development.

The summit is an important national and international development for India because it highlights India's role in shaping discussions involving major emerging economies.

Exam Point:
Event: 18th BRICS Summit
Venue: New Delhi
Host country: India

2. India-China leaders discuss border peace and economic cooperation

Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Chinese President Xi Jinping met on the sidelines of the BRICS Summit in New Delhi. The discussions covered border peace, economic cooperation, trade imbalances, supply chains and greater business connectivity.

The meeting is significant for India because stable border relations and balanced economic ties are important components of India's long-term strategic interests.

Exam Point: Important themes:
India-China relations · Border peace · Trade · Supply chains · BRICS

3. India calls for a stronger role for the Global South

Prime Minister Narendra Modi emphasized the need for developing countries to have a stronger voice in international decision-making.

India has continued to support reforms in global institutions and greater representation for developing and emerging economies.

Exam Point: Key concept:
Global South refers broadly to developing and emerging countries seeking greater representation in global institutions.

4. BRICS strengthens cooperation in education, AI and skills

Education has emerged as an important area of BRICS cooperation. BRICS leaders supported greater cooperation in areas including artificial intelligence, digital literacy, vocational education, STEM learning and workforce preparedness.

The leaders also supported continued cooperation through the BRICS Network University and discussed improving academic cooperation, skills and alignment between education and changing labour-market needs.

Education Exam Point: Important areas:
AI in education · Digital literacy · STEM · Vocational education · Skills · BRICS Network University

5. BRICS promotes education-to-employment connection

BRICS countries are working to strengthen the connection between education and employability. The focus includes vocational training, digital skills, STEM education and artificial intelligence.

The objective is to help students and workers develop skills that match changing industry and labour-market requirements.

Exam Point: Education + Skills + Employability is an important theme in current education news.
