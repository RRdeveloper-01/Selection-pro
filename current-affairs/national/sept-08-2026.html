National Current Affairs 8 September 2026: India, Education & Important News

Important National and Education Current Affairs for 8 September 2026, covering electoral rolls, LPG distribution, India-China border talks, education and International Literacy Day. These topics are useful for UPSC, SSC, Railway, State PCS and interview preparation.

Published: 8 September 2026 · Category: National · Education News Included

1. Electoral rolls: Special Intensive Revision in focus

The Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls has become an important national issue, with reports indicating that around 1.85 lakh names were removed from electoral rolls in Manipur and Sikkim after the revision exercise.

Electoral-roll revision is intended to improve the accuracy of voter lists by identifying eligible voters, correcting records and removing entries that no longer meet eligibility requirements.

Exam Point: SIR stands for Special Intensive Revision. Electoral rolls and elections in India are administered under the constitutional framework involving the Election Commission of India.

2. LPG refill booking interval reduced for rural consumers

The minimum interval for booking another domestic LPG refill has been reduced to 25 days for rural consumers as part of measures aimed at improving access to cooking gas.

LPG availability and distribution are important socio-economic issues because clean cooking fuel affects household welfare, public health and energy access.

Interview Point: LPG-related policies are frequently connected with clean cooking fuel, energy access, women's welfare, rural development and environmental benefits.

3. India-China hold Corps Commander-level talks in Arunachal Pradesh sector

India and China are holding Corps Commander-level talks at Wacha-Damai in the Arunachal Pradesh sector.

The Indian delegation is being led by Lieutenant General Girish Kalia. Such military-level dialogue is important for maintaining communication and managing the situation along the India-China border.

Region Arunachal Pradesh sector
Talks Corps Commander-level
Exam Connection: India-China relations are important for questions on border management, Line of Actual Control (LAC), national security and India's neighbourhood policy.

4. International Literacy Day 2026: education and literacy remain key global priorities

International Literacy Day is observed every year on 8 September. The day highlights the importance of literacy as a foundation for education, social development and equal opportunity.

In 2026, UNESCO's global celebration is being held in Mexico on 8 and 9 September. Education ministers, experts, teachers and students are participating in discussions on literacy challenges and possible solutions.

Exam Point: International Literacy Day is observed on 8 September every year.

5. Literacy linked with education, skills and inclusive development

Current discussions around literacy increasingly connect basic reading and writing abilities with digital skills, employability, lifelong learning and inclusive education.

For India, literacy and education are important policy areas connected with human-capital development, school education, adult education and access to opportunities.

Interview Question: Why is literacy important for economic development?

Answer: Literacy improves access to education, employment, information and public services and contributes to human-capital development.
