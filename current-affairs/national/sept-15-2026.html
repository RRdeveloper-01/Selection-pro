1. National Engineers' Day 2026 Celebrated Across India

India observes National Engineers' Day on 15 September every year to commemorate the birth anniversary of Sir Mokshagundam Visvesvaraya. The day recognises the contribution of engineers to India's infrastructure, technology, industrial development and nation-building.

Sir M. Visvesvaraya was a renowned engineer and statesman who played an important role in major engineering and public-works projects.

India News Exam Point: National Engineers' Day — 15 September. It commemorates the birth anniversary of Sir M. Visvesvaraya.

2. BRICS New Delhi Summit Highlights India's Global Role

The 18th BRICS Summit in New Delhi brought together leaders of the expanded BRICS grouping. India highlighted the importance of multilateralism, global cooperation, economic resilience and solutions to international challenges.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi also emphasised cooperation in areas including health, technology, energy, food security and global governance.

National News Exam Point: 18th BRICS Summit — New Delhi, India. Key themes included multilateralism, cooperation and global governance.

3. India-China Relations Remain Important After Modi-Xi Meeting

India-China relations remained an important national and international issue after Prime Minister Narendra Modi met Chinese President Xi Jinping on the sidelines of the BRICS Summit.

The discussions focused on improving bilateral relations, maintaining peace and stability along the border and strengthening economic and connectivity ties.

India News Exam Point: India-China relations, border peace, bilateral trade, connectivity and BRICS.

4. UGC Registration Deadline for Online and ODL Programme Recognition

The University Grants Commission has set 15 September 2026 as the deadline for eligible higher-education institutions to register for recognition of online and Open and Distance Learning programmes for the upcoming academic session.

The development is important for universities, colleges and students because UGC recognition plays an important role in the validity of recognised online and distance-learning programmes.

Education News Exam Point: UGC, Online Learning, Open and Distance Learning and higher-education recognition.

5. UGC-NET 2026 Re-Examination Updates Remain in Focus

Education news continues to focus on the UGC-NET June 2026 examination after the National Testing Agency ordered re-examinations for certain papers following complaints about factual, typographical, translation and other errors.

The development is relevant for candidates preparing for higher-education teaching and research eligibility examinations.

Education News Exam Point: UGC-NET, NTA, re-examination and examination quality-control procedures.
