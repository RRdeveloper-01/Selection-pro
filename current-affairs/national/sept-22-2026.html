1. 72nd National Film Awards to Be Presented in Gujarat

What happened: President Droupadi Murmu is scheduled to present the 72nd National Film Awards at Ekta Nagar, Kevadia, Gujarat, on 22 September 2026.

Why it matters: The awards recognise achievements in Indian cinema and are an important national cultural event.

Exam Fact: The National Film Awards are major national honours for cinema.

2. National Anti-Narcotics Task Force Conference Begins

What happened: The third National Conference of Heads of Anti-Narcotics Task Forces is scheduled in New Delhi on 22 September.

Why it matters: The conference focuses on coordination between central and state anti-narcotics units.

Exam Fact: ANTF stands for Anti-Narcotics Task Force.

3. India International Water Week Opens in New Delhi

What happened: India International Water Week begins in New Delhi with focus on water security, management and sustainable water resources.

Why it matters: Water management is important for agriculture, cities, industry and climate resilience.

Exam Fact: Integrated water-resource management links water use, conservation and ecosystems.

4. IMD Issues Orange Alert for Six Kerala Districts

What happened: The India Meteorological Department has issued an orange alert for six Kerala districts because of possible heavy to very heavy rainfall and thunderstorms.

Why it matters: Heavy rainfall can affect transport, agriculture, infrastructure and disaster preparedness.

Exam Fact: An orange alert signals potentially severe weather requiring preparedness.

5. DPIIT Holds Industry Workshop on Trade and Ease of Doing Business

What happened: DPIIT is scheduled to hold an industry workshop covering free-trade agreements, ease of doing business and regulatory compliance.

Why it matters: The discussions are relevant to exporters, MSMEs, startups and India’s trade environment.

Exam Fact: DPIIT works under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
