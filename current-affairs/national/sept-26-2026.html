1. Education Minister Urges Young Innovators to Solve Real-Life Problems

What happened: Union Education Minister Pralhad Joshi urged young innovators to identify real-life problems faced by people and develop practical and affordable solutions through innovation. The remarks were made at the launch of the Seva First Innovation Challenge – South Zone at IISc Bengaluru.

Why it matters: The initiative connects education, innovation and problem-solving and encourages students and young people to work on practical challenges.

Exam Fact: IISc stands for the Indian Institute of Science and is located in Bengaluru, Karnataka.

2. Delhi Schools Launch 'Meri Seva, Mera Sankalp' Competition

What happened: The Directorate of Education, Delhi has launched the 'Meri Seva, Mera Sankalp' competition as part of the month-long Seva Sankalp Abhiyan. Students from Delhi government schools are being encouraged to create short reels, drawings and speeches around public service and civic responsibility.

Why it matters: The programme promotes civic participation among students and connects education with themes such as cleanliness, environmental responsibility, helping vulnerable people and respect for public property.

Exam Fact: The Seva Sankalp Abhiyan is linked with the broader vision of Viksit Bharat @2047.

3. Assam Universities Asked to Prepare Higher-Education Roadmap

What happened: The Assam government has asked universities to prepare a roadmap for strengthening higher education. Officials reviewed measures related to higher-education participation and improving the Gross Enrolment Ratio in the state.

Why it matters: Higher-education access and enrolment are important indicators of educational development and human-capital formation.

Exam Fact: Gross Enrolment Ratio, or GER, measures total enrolment in a level of education relative to the population of the official age group for that level.

4. SBI Clerk Preliminary Examination Begins on September 26

What happened: The State Bank of India Clerk Preliminary Examination 2026 is scheduled for September 26 and 27. The admit card for the examination has already been released through SBI's official recruitment process.

Why it matters: The SBI Clerk examination is one of India's major banking recruitment examinations and is important for candidates preparing for Banking Current Affairs and competitive examinations.

Exam Fact: SBI Clerk recruitment is conducted for Junior Associate positions in customer support and sales.

5. Centre Highlights Innovation and Research Growth in India

What happened: Union Minister Jitendra Singh highlighted India's growth in innovation and research over the past decade during a national science and technology-related programme.

Why it matters: Research and innovation contribute to India's technology ecosystem, scientific capacity and long-term development.

Exam Fact: India's Department of Science and Technology functions under the Ministry of Science and Technology.
