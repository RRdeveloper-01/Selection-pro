India opener Shafali Verma reached 3,000 runs in Women's T20 Internationals during the Pakistan match. Reports noted that she became the youngest and fastest player to reach the milestone in the format.

3. Smriti Mandhana becomes leading run-scorer in women's international cricket

Smriti Mandhana moved past Mithali Raj's previous mark of 10,868 international runs during India's Women's Asia Cup match against Hong Kong. Her 124-run innings also took her international century tally to 18.

Exam Point: Smriti Mandhana is the leading run-scorer in women's international cricket after surpassing Mithali Raj's record.
