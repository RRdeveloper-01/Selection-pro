Sports Current Affairs 9 September 2026: India Hockey, Cricket & US Open

Today's sports current affairs feature Indian hockey, cricket and tennis updates, with quick exam-focused facts for students and competitive-exam preparation.

Published: 9 September 2026 · Exam-focused current affairs.

1. Indian women's hockey team leaves for Asian Games 2026

The Indian women's hockey team departed for Japan for the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games. Led by captain Salima Tete and coached by Sjoerd Marijne, the team is targeting the gold medal and a direct qualification place for the Los Angeles 2028 Olympics.

Exam Point: Aichi-Nagoya 2026 is the 20th edition of the Asian Games.

2. India's junior women hockey team defeats Japan 7-0

India recorded a commanding 7-0 victory over Japan in the Women's Hockey Junior Asia Cup and secured a place in the quarter-finals. The result underlined the team's strong form in the tournament.

Exam Point: Hockey India governs and promotes the sport of hockey in India.

3. Ishan Kishan hits 270 in Duleep Trophy final

Ishan Kishan scored 270 runs for East Zone in the Duleep Trophy final against South Zone, becoming one of the major cricket headlines in India news on September 9. The innings was a standout performance in domestic first-class cricket.

Exam Point: The Duleep Trophy is a major domestic first-class cricket competition in India.

4. Alexander Zverev reaches US Open quarter-finals

Alexander Zverev advanced to the US Open quarter-finals after defeating Darderi. The result was among the major tennis developments in the September 9 sports news cycle.

Exam Point: The US Open is one of tennis's four Grand Slam tournaments.

5. Japan announces squad for one-off T20I against India

Japan announced its squad for the one-off T20 International against India, adding another international cricket fixture to the current calendar. The match is part of the growing global spread of cricket beyond its traditional full-member countries.

Exam Point: T20I stands for Twenty20 International, a recognised international format of cricket.
