1. India Women Face Sri Lanka in Asian Games Cricket Final

What happened: India’s women’s cricket team is scheduled to face Sri Lanka in the gold-medal match at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on 22 September.

Why it matters: The final is one of India’s major medal events on the day.

Exam Fact: Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games are being held in Japan from 19 September to 4 October 2026.

2. India Women Face Japan in Badminton Quarter-Final

What happened: India’s women’s badminton team is scheduled to face Japan in the Asian Games team quarter-final.

Why it matters: The knockout match is part of India’s multi-sport campaign.

Exam Fact: Badminton is an Asian Games and Olympic sport.

3. India Men Meet South Korea in Kabaddi

What happened: India’s men’s kabaddi team is scheduled to face South Korea in the Asian Games group stage.

Why it matters: Kabaddi is a major Asian multi-sport team event with strong Indian participation.

Exam Fact: Kabaddi is played by teams taking turns sending a raider into the opponent’s half.

4. India Men Face Sri Lanka in Hockey

What happened: India’s men’s hockey team is scheduled to play Sri Lanka in Asian Games Pool A.

Why it matters: The match is part of India’s campaign in a major team sport.

Exam Fact: Hockey is an established Asian Games sport.

5. Shooting and Other Medal Events Continue

What happened: Indian athletes are also scheduled to compete in shooting, boxing, wushu, karate, fencing and swimming.

Why it matters: The broad schedule provides medal opportunities across individual and team disciplines.
