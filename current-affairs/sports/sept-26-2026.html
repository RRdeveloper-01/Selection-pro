Sports News 26 September 2026: Asian Games & India Sports

1. India Men's and Women's Kabaddi Teams Face Iran in Gold-Medal Finals

What happened: India's men's and women's kabaddi teams are scheduled to face Iran in their respective gold-medal matches at the 2026 Asian Games on September 26. The women's final is scheduled for the morning, followed by the men's final later in the day.

Why it matters: Kabaddi is one of India's traditional strengths at the Asian Games and the two finals form an important part of India's medal programme on September 26.

Exam Fact: Kabaddi has been part of the Asian Games programme since 1990, while the women's event was introduced in 2010.

2. PV Sindhu and Lakshya Sen Begin Asian Games Singles Campaigns

What happened: Indian badminton players PV Sindhu and Lakshya Sen are scheduled to compete in their singles matches on September 26 at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games. Lakshya Sen is scheduled to face Singapore's Loh Kean Yew, while PV Sindhu is scheduled to face Chinese Taipei's Chiu Pin-chian.

Why it matters: Badminton is an important sport for India's Asian Games campaign, with several Indian singles and doubles players competing during the tournament.

Exam Fact: PV Sindhu is an Olympic medallist and Lakshya Sen won a bronze medal at the 2022 BWF World Championships.

3. Indian Athletes Set for Multiple Asian Games Athletics Events

What happened: Indian athletes are scheduled to compete in several athletics events on September 26, including the men's shot put final, women's 100m hurdles, women's and men's 400m finals, women's 1500m final and other track and field events.

Why it matters: Athletics is one of the largest sports at the Asian Games and includes multiple medal events across track and field disciplines.

Exam Fact: Athletics includes track events as well as field events such as jumping and throwing.

4. Indian Shooters Compete in Women's 50m Rifle 3 Positions

What happened: Indian shooters Ashi Chouksey, Tilottama Sen and Vidarsa K Vinod are scheduled to compete in the women's 50m Rifle 3 Positions event at the Asian Games on September 26, subject to qualification for the final.

Why it matters: Shooting has been an important medal sport for India at major Asian multi-sport competitions.

Exam Fact: The 50m Rifle 3 Positions event involves the three positions of kneeling, prone and standing.

5. India Continues Asian Games Campaign Across Archery and Squash

What happened: Indian athletes are also scheduled to compete in archery qualification events and squash matches on September 26. Indian archers are involved in recurve and compound events, while Anahat Singh and Abhay Singh are among the Indian squash players in action.

Why it matters: The broad participation across precision and racket sports adds to India's overall medal programme at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games.

Exam Fact: Archery and squash are both included in the Asian Games sports programme.
