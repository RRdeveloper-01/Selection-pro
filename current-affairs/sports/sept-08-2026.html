Sports Current Affairs 8 September 2026: Badminton, Football, Tennis & F1

Important Sports Current Affairs for 8 September 2026, covering Indian badminton, India's AFC U20 Asian Cup qualification, Formula 1, US Open tennis and China Masters. These developments are useful for SSC, Railway, State PCS and competitive-exam interviews.

Published: 8 September 2026 · Category: Sports · Exam-focused Current Affairs

1. Satwik-Chirag win maiden China Masters Super 750 title

India's men's doubles badminton pair Satwiksairaj Rankireddy and Chirag Shetty won their maiden China Masters title in Shenzhen.

The Indian pair defeated China's He Ji Ting and Ren Xiang Yu 11-21, 21-13, 21-17 in the final.

The victory is significant for Indian badminton as it gave Satwik and Chirag their first men's doubles China Masters title.

2. India qualify for AFC U20 Asian Cup after 20 years

India's men's U20 football team qualified for the AFC U20 Asian Cup main tournament for the first time in 20 years.

India defeated Bangladesh 3-0 in their final qualifying match in Tashkent to secure qualification.

India last appeared in the main tournament in 2006.

Competition AFC U20 Asian Cup
India's last appearance 2006
Final qualifying result India 3-0 Bangladesh
Venue Tashkent
Exam Point: AFC stands for Asian Football Confederation, the governing body for football in Asia.

3. Kimi Antonelli wins 2026 Italian Grand Prix at Monza

Mercedes driver Andrea Kimi Antonelli won the 2026 Formula 1 Italian Grand Prix at Monza on 6 September 2026.

The victory was particularly significant because Antonelli became the first Italian driver to win his home Grand Prix in the modern Formula 1 era.

4. Carlos Alcaraz reaches US Open quarterfinal

Spain's Carlos Alcaraz advanced to the quarterfinal of the 2026 US Open after defeating Tommy Paul.

The result keeps Alcaraz in contention in the men's singles competition at the US Open.

Exam Point: The US Open is one of tennis's four Grand Slam tournaments. The other three are the Australian Open, French Open and Wimbledon.

5. An Se-young wins third consecutive China Masters women's singles title

Republic of Korea badminton star An Se-young won the women's singles China Masters title for the third consecutive time.

She defeated Japan's Tomoka Miyazaki 21-17, 21-6 in the final.

Exam Point: An Se-young is one of the leading women's singles badminton players from the Republic of Korea.
