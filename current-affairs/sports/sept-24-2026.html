1. Mirabai Chanu Wins Silver at Asian Games 2026

What happened: Indian weightlifter Mirabai Chanu won the silver medal in the women's 49kg category at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games 2026. She lifted a combined weight of 198kg to finish second in the event.

Why it matters: The medal ended India's long wait for an Asian Games weightlifting medal. India's previous Asian Games weightlifting medal had come in 1998, when Karnam Malleswari won silver.

Exam Fact: Mirabai Chanu won an Olympic silver medal at the Tokyo 2020 Olympic Games.

2. India Wins Two Bronze Medals in Asian Games Skeet

What happened: India won bronze medals in both the men's and women's skeet team events at the Asian Games 2026.

Why it matters: Shooting has continued to contribute medals for India's Asian Games campaign and remains one of the country's important medal sports at multi-sport competitions.

Exam Fact: Skeet is a shooting discipline in which athletes fire at moving clay targets released from different stations.

3. India's Women's Cricket Team Wins Asian Games Gold

What happened: India's women's cricket team won the gold medal in the women's T20 cricket event at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games, defeating Sri Lanka in the final.

Why it matters: The victory gave India its first gold medal of the 2026 Asian Games campaign and marked another major achievement for Indian women's cricket.

Exam Fact: Cricket was included in the Asian Games programme at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya edition.

4. Indian Athletes Begin Major Asian Games Events

What happened: Indian athletes are competing in several important events on 24 September, including rowing, wushu, shooting, athletics, swimming, squash and canoe sprint.

Why it matters: The Asian Games provide an important international platform for Indian athletes and also serve as a major continental multi-sport competition.

Exam Fact: The Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games 2026 are being held in Japan from 19 September to 4 October 2026.

5. India Continues Campaign in Hockey and Kabaddi

What happened: India's men's hockey team is scheduled to face South Korea, while India's men's and women's kabaddi teams continue their group-stage campaigns at the Asian Games on 24 September.

Why it matters: Hockey and kabaddi are traditional strengths in Indian sport and remain important team events during the Asian Games.

Exam Fact: Kabaddi is a traditional South Asian sport and is part of the Asian Games programme.
