1. India Women Win Record Eighth Women's Asia Cup Title

India's women's cricket team defeated Sri Lanka by 72 runs in the Women's Asia Cup 2026 final in Dubai to win a record-extending eighth Asia Cup title. India scored 183/5, while Sri Lanka were restricted to 111.

Shafali Verma scored 68 runs and Smriti Mandhana made 59, while Sree Charani played an important role with the ball.

Cricket News Exam Point: India — Women's Asia Cup 2026 champions; eighth title.

2. India Beat Afghanistan by Seven Wickets in First T20I

India defeated Afghanistan by seven wickets in the first T20I at the Arun Jaitley Stadium in New Delhi. Afghanistan scored 156/8 before India chased the target in 13.4 overs.

Abhishek Sharma produced an aggressive innings of 82 runs from 32 balls, helping India take a 1–0 lead in the three-match series.

India Cricket News Exam Point: India defeated Afghanistan by 7 wickets and took a 1–0 lead in the T20I series.

3. Dhiraj Bommadevara Wins Historic Recurve Archery World Cup Final Gold

Indian archer Dhiraj Bommadevara created history by becoming the first Indian male recurve archer to win gold at the World Cup Final.

He defeated Japan's Nakanishi Junya in a dramatic shoot-off at Saltillo, Mexico. The achievement adds another major milestone to India's international archery record.

Sports News Exam Point: Dhiraj Bommadevara — first Indian male recurve archer to win World Cup Final gold.

4. India Win Men's and Women's Junior Asia Cup Hockey Titles

India's men's and women's hockey teams won the Junior Asia Cup titles in Moqi, China. The Indian men's team defeated South Korea 4–1 in the final to successfully defend its title.

The double success highlights India's strong development pipeline in hockey ahead of major international competitions.

Hockey News Exam Point: India won both the men's and women's Junior Asia Cup hockey titles.

5. Asian Games 2026 Preparations Enter Final Stage

Indian athletes are entering the final phase of preparations for the 2026 Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan. The Asian Games are scheduled from 19 September to 4 October 2026.

India will compete across multiple sports, with cricket, hockey, archery, athletics and other disciplines expected to attract major attention.

Asian Games Exam Point: Asian Games 2026 — Aichi-Nagoya, Japan; 19 September to 4 October 2026.
