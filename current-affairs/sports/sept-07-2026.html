Sports Current Affairs 7 September 2026: China Masters, Asian Games & Cricket

Badminton, Asian Games and India women's cricket and hockey developments selected for sports current-affairs revision.

Published: 7 September 2026 · Exam-focused current affairs.

1. Satwiksairaj Rankireddy and Chirag Shetty win maiden China Masters title

India's men's doubles badminton pair Satwiksairaj Rankireddy and Chirag Shetty won their first China Masters Super 750 title in Shenzhen. They defeated China's He Ji Ting and Ren Xiang Yu 11-21, 21-13, 21-17.

TournamentChina Masters Super 750
WinnersSatwiksairaj Rankireddy & Chirag Shetty

2. China Masters victory boosts preparations for Asian Games 2026

The China Masters title gives the Indian men's doubles pair confidence ahead of the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games 2026. The 20th Asian Games will be held in Japan from 19 September to 4 October 2026.

3. India Women continue strong run in Women's Asia Cup 2026

India Women defeated Pakistan by seven wickets after Pakistan were bowled out for 55.

Revision: India won by 7 wickets and chased the target in 8.4 overs.

4. Junior Asia Cup hockey: India draw with Japan

India's junior men's hockey team rallied to draw against Japan in the Junior Asia Cup.

5. Women's junior hockey: India defeat Malaysia 11-1

India's women's junior hockey team recorded an 11-1 victory over Malaysia in the AHF Junior Asia Cup.
