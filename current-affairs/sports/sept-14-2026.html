1. India win Women's Asia Cup 2026 for a record eighth time

India defeated Sri Lanka by 72 runs in the Women's Asia Cup 2026 final in Dubai to win a record-extending eighth title. Shafali Verma and Smriti Mandhana powered India's innings with a 129-run opening partnership.

2. Shafali Verma and Smriti Mandhana lead India's final victory

Shafali Verma scored 68 and Smriti Mandhana 59 in the final, building a 129-run opening stand. Shafali's 24-ball half-century was reported as the fastest in Women's T20 Asia Cup history.
