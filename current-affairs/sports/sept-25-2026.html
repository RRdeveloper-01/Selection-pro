1. India Begins Day 7 Asian Games Campaign

What happened: Indian athletes are scheduled to compete in shooting, athletics, kabaddi, squash, swimming, badminton, hockey, fencing, canoe sprint and other disciplines on 25 September.

Why it matters: The packed schedule creates multiple opportunities for Indian athletes and teams during the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games.

Exam Fact: The Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games 2026 are being held in Japan from 19 September to 4 October.

2. India's Men's and Women's Kabaddi Teams in Semifinals

What happened: India's men's and women's kabaddi teams are scheduled to play their respective Asian Games semifinals against Thailand and Nepal.

Why it matters: The knockout stage puts both Indian teams in contention for medals.

Exam Fact: Kabaddi is part of the Asian Games programme.

3. Shooting Provides Multiple Medal Opportunities

What happened: Indian shooters Aishwary Pratap Singh Tomar, Rudrankksh Patil and Niraj Kumar are competing in the men's 50m rifle 3 positions event, while the mixed-team 10m air-pistol event also features Indian competitors.

Why it matters: Shooting remains an important medal sport for India's Asian Games contingent.

Exam Fact: The 50m rifle 3 positions event includes different shooting positions.

4. Indian Squash Players Feature in Knockout Matches

What happened: Indian squash players are competing in singles and mixed-doubles knockout matches, including an all-Indian women's singles quarterfinal involving Anahat Singh and Tanvi Khanna.

Why it matters: The results can affect India's medal prospects in squash.

Exam Fact: Squash is an indoor racket sport played on a four-walled court.

5. Athletics Features Several Indian Medal Events

What happened: Indian athletes are scheduled in events including men's 100m semifinals, women's shot put, pole vault, men's 10,000m and hammer throw.

Why it matters: Athletics is one of the largest medal-producing disciplines at multi-sport events.

Exam Fact: Athletics includes track and field events such as running, jumping and throwing.
