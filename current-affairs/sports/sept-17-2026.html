1. India's Asian Games 2026 Campaign Begins with Teqball

India's competitive campaign at the 2026 Asian Games begins on September 17 with teqball. Indian athletes will participate in men's doubles, women's singles and mixed doubles events.

The Asian Games are being hosted in the Aichi-Nagoya region of Japan. The main Games are scheduled from September 19 to October 4, while selected competitions begin earlier.

Sports News Exam Point: India's Asian Games campaign begins on September 17 with teqball.

2. India Women's Cricket Team to Face Japan in Asian Games Quarter-final

India's women's cricket team will begin its Asian Games 2026 campaign against Japan on September 18.

The match is scheduled as a quarter-final, with the Indian team entering the knockout stage directly. Harmanpreet Kaur is leading the Indian women's cricket team.

Cricket News Exam Point: India Women vs Japan — Asian Games 2026 quarter-final — September 18.

3. India Men's Cricket Team Enters Asian Games Quarter-finals Directly

India's men's cricket team will enter the Asian Games 2026 competition directly at the quarter-final stage.

The men's quarter-finals are scheduled for September 28. Shreyas Iyer is the captain of India's men's Asian Games cricket team.

India Cricket News Exam Point: India men's Asian Games quarter-final — September 28, 2026.

4. Vipraj Nigam Replaces Varun Chakaravarthy in Asian Games Squad

Young leg-spinner Vipraj Nigam has been added to India's Asian Games men's cricket squad after Varun Chakaravarthy was ruled out with a side strain.

The change comes shortly before India's Asian Games campaign and adds another spin option to the squad.

Cricket Current Affairs Exam Point: Vipraj Nigam replaces Varun Chakaravarthy in India's Asian Games squad.

5. Pakistan Seeks Change of Venue for Asian Champions Trophy

The Pakistan Hockey Federation has requested that the upcoming Asian Champions Trophy, scheduled to be held in India, be moved to a neutral venue.

The tournament is scheduled to take place in Mohali and Jalandhar from October 27 to November 5. Hockey India has rejected the request and said there would be no change in venues.

Hockey News Exam Point: Asian Champions Trophy 2026 is scheduled in Mohali and Jalandhar from October 27 to November 5.
