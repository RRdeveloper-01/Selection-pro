Top 10 Current Affairs – 5 August 2026

Here are today's most important current affairs selected from national, international, economy, science & technology, government schemes, awards, defence and sports. Each topic is explained from an examination perspective to help you revise quickly.

1. RBI Keeps Monetary Policy in Focus

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) kept investors' attention on its latest monetary policy as inflation remained within the target range while global economic uncertainties continued. The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) reviews the repo rate to maintain price stability and support economic growth.

Exam Focus

  • Institution: Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
  • Committee: Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)
  • Keyword: Repo Rate

2. Indian Rupee Supported by Lower Crude Oil Prices

The Indian Rupee showed strength as international crude oil prices declined. Since India imports a large amount of crude oil, lower prices help reduce import bills and inflationary pressure.

Why It Matters for Exams

Questions related to inflation, crude oil, imports, exports, and the Indian economy are frequently asked in SSC, Banking, and UPSC examinations.

3. India Continues Digital Governance Initiatives

The Government of India is expanding digital public services to improve transparency, faster delivery of welfare schemes, and better access to government services for citizens.

Exam Point

Remember important government digital initiatives and their objectives for competitive examinations.

4. Census 2027 Digital Self-Enumeration

Citizens will be able to submit their census details through the online self-enumeration system before field verification. This initiative aims to make the census process faster and more efficient.

Remember

  • Project: Census 2027
  • Feature: Digital Self-Enumeration

5. Border Trade Through Shipki La Pass Continues

Trade activities through Shipki La Pass are improving regional connectivity and economic cooperation. Shipki La is an important mountain pass located in Himachal Pradesh along the India-China border.

Exam Focus

Location-based questions related to mountain passes are common in UPSC, SSC, and State PSC examinations.

6. PM-VBRY Supports First-Time Employees

The PM-VBRY employment initiative has encouraged more first-time workers to enter the formal employment sector by providing financial support and employment incentives.

Important Fact

Government employment schemes and their objectives are important for Banking, SSC, Railway, and UPSC exams.

7. India Hosts BRICS Statistical Meeting

India hosted a meeting of BRICS statistical authorities to strengthen cooperation in official statistics, economic data sharing, and policy planning among member countries.

Remember

  • Organization: BRICS
  • Topic: Official Statistics
  • Category: International Affairs

8. Fit India Movement Promotes Healthy Lifestyle

The Fit India Movement continues to encourage citizens to adopt regular physical activity and healthy habits through nationwide awareness campaigns.

Exam Point

Government campaigns related to health and fitness are important for competitive examinations.

9. Science and Technology Remains a Key Examination Area

Developments related to Artificial Intelligence, Digital India, space technology, cybersecurity, and innovation continue to receive importance in government examinations.

Preparation Tip
