RBI Proposes Changes Related to Interest Rates on Loans

The Reserve Bank of India has proposed changes concerning the way interest rates on certain loans are handled. The proposal is aimed at improving transparency and strengthening the transmission of monetary policy to borrowers.

Why Important?

This development is important for Banking and RBI-related examinations because it concerns lending rates, monetary policy transmission and borrower-related rules.

Current Affair #4

Iran Rejects US Claim Regarding Strait of Hormuz

Iran has rejected claims from the United States regarding control over the Strait of Hormuz. The strategic waterway remains important for international energy transportation and global trade.

Remember

The Strait of Hormuz connects the Persian Gulf with the Gulf of Oman and the Arabian Sea and is one of the world's most strategically important maritime passages.

Current Affair #5

IMD Forecasts Heavy to Very Heavy Rainfall Across Several States

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast heavy to very heavy rainfall across several parts of India due to active monsoon conditions.

States and regions mentioned in the weather alerts include parts of Uttar Pradesh, Delhi, Jammu and Kashmir, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal and several other areas.

Exam Point

IMD is India's national meteorological service and functions under the Ministry of Earth Sciences.

Current Affair #6

India Wins 17 Medals at U-23 Commonwealth Fencing Championship

Indian fencers produced a strong performance at the U-23 Commonwealth Fencing Championship in Lagos, Nigeria.

India secured a total of 17 medals, including 4 gold, 4 silver and 9 bronze medals.

Medal Tally

  • 🥈 Silver – 4
  • 🥉 Bronze – 9
  • Total – 17 medals

Current Affair #8

ED Director Rahul Navin Gets One-Year Tenure Extension

The Central Government has extended the tenure of Enforcement Directorate Director Rahul Navin by one year beyond his existing term.

His earlier tenure was scheduled to end on 13 August 2026. The extension continues his tenure as Director of the Enforcement Directorate.

Exam Point

ED stands for Enforcement Directorate. It is a financial investigation agency under the Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance.

Current Affair #9

India Diversifies Urea Imports Amid Global Supply Disruptions

India has diversified its sources of urea imports amid supply disruptions linked to geopolitical developments in West Asia.

Alternative suppliers mentioned in recent reports include Egypt, Algeria, Nigeria and Georgia, among others. The move is aimed at maintaining adequate fertiliser availability for Indian agriculture.

Why Important?

Fertiliser availability, urea imports and agricultural supply chains are important topics for Economy, Agriculture and General Awareness sections.

Current Affair #10

14 August Observed as Partition Horrors Remembrance Day

14 August is observed in India as Partition Horrors Remembrance Day to remember the suffering, displacement and hardships associated with the Partition of 1947.

The day was officially designated by the Government of India in 2021. Various remembrance programmes are organised to honour the people affected by the Partition.

Important for Exams
