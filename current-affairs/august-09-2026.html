01

Government e-Marketplace (GeM) Completes 10 Years (GeM के 10 वर्ष पूरे)

The Government e-Marketplace (GeM) marks 10 years of its journey in India's public procurement ecosystem. The platform has become an important digital marketplace for government procurement.

Important Exam Facts (महत्वपूर्ण परीक्षा तथ्य)

  • GeM stands for Government e-Marketplace.
  • It was launched in 2016.
  • It is used for online procurement by government organisations.
  • GeM promotes transparency and efficiency in public procurement.
  • It is associated with the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.

Exam Focus

Economy, Digital Governance and Public Procurement.

02

Har Ghar Tiranga 2026 Campaign Begins (हर घर तिरंगा अभियान 2026)

The Har Ghar Tiranga 2026 campaign begins on 9 August as part of the Independence Day celebrations. Citizens are encouraged to display the National Flag and participate in the campaign.

Important Exam Facts

  • Campaign period: 9 August – 17 August 2026.
  • The campaign encourages citizens to display the Indian National Flag.
  • The campaign is connected with India's Independence Day celebrations.
  • The initiative promotes public participation and patriotic engagement.
Exam Focus: National Flag, Independence Day, Culture and Government Initiatives.
03

International Day of the World's Indigenous Peoples (विश्व के स्वदेशी लोगों का अंतरराष्ट्रीय दिवस)

The International Day of the World's Indigenous Peoples is observed every year on 9 August. The day highlights the rights, cultures and contributions of Indigenous Peoples around the world.

Important Exam Facts

  • Observed every year on 9 August.
  • The day is associated with the United Nations.
  • The date commemorates the first meeting of the UN Working Group on Indigenous Populations in 1982.
  • The day promotes awareness of Indigenous Peoples' rights and cultural heritage.
Exam Focus: United Nations, Important Days and Indigenous Peoples.
04

Quit India Movement Anniversary (भारत छोड़ो आंदोलन की वर्षगांठ)

9 August is associated with the launch of the historic Quit India Movement in 1942. The movement played a major role in India's freedom struggle.

Important Exam Facts

  • Year: 1942.
  • The movement was launched on 8 August 1942, while 9 August is widely remembered as the day of mass arrests and the beginning of the movement's major phase.
  • Mahatma Gandhi gave the famous “Do or Die” call.
  • The movement demanded an end to British rule in India.
  • It is an important topic in Modern Indian History.
Exam Focus: Modern Indian History and Freedom Struggle.

The 57th Convocation of IIT Delhi was held on 8 August 2026. The event was an important higher-education and technology-related development.

Important Exam Facts

  • Institution: Indian Institute of Technology Delhi.
  • Event: 57th Convocation.
  • Date: 8 August 2026.
  • Relevant areas include higher education, technology and innovation.
Exam Focus: Higher Education, IITs and Science & Technology.

India Finishes with 39 Medals at Commonwealth Games 2026 (कॉमनवेल्थ गेम्स 2026 में भारत)

India finished the 2026 Commonwealth Games with a total of 39 medals. India secured 13 gold, 17 silver and 9 bronze medals.

Important Exam Facts

  • Total medals: 39.
  • Gold: 13.
  • Silver: 17.
  • Bronze: 9.
  • India finished fourth in the overall medal standings.
  • The Games were held in Glasgow, Scotland.
Exam Focus: Sports, Commonwealth Games and India.

Fit India – Sundays on Cycle (फिट इंडिया संडे ऑन साइकिल)

The Fit India – Sundays on Cycle initiative is designed to encourage cycling and physical activity among citizens.

Important Exam Facts

  • The initiative is associated with the Fit India Movement.

  • The programme is linked with India's sports and fitness initiatives.
Exam Focus: Sports, Health and Government Initiatives.

Vice-President Visits Andaman and Nicobar Islands (उपराष्ट्रपति का अंडमान और निकोबार दौरा)

The Vice-President of India is visiting the Andaman and Nicobar Islands during 8–9 August 2026. The visit has historical and national significance.

Important Exam Facts

  • Location: Andaman and Nicobar Islands.
  • The islands are located in the Bay of Bengal.
  • The Cellular Jail is an important historical site in Port Blair.
  • The islands are strategically important for India's maritime security.
Exam Focus: Indian Polity, History, Geography and Strategic Location.
09

India's Contribution to FAIR Mega Science Project (FAIR मेगा साइंस प्रोजेक्ट में भारत का योगदान)

India is contributing to the international Facility for Antiproton and Ion Research (FAIR) project in Germany. The project is a major international scientific facility for nuclear and particle physics research.

Important Exam Facts

  • FAIR stands for Facility for Antiproton and Ion Research.
  • Location: Darmstadt, Germany.
  • The project focuses on nuclear and particle physics.
  • India is participating in the international scientific collaboration.
  • The project is relevant to advanced science and technology.
Exam Focus: Science & Technology, Nuclear Physics and International Cooperation.
10

Slender-billed Vulture Conservation in India (स्लेंडर-बिल्ड गिद्ध संरक्षण)

The Slender-billed Vulture remains an important species for wildlife conservation in India. Its conservation is significant because of its Critically Endangered status.

Important Exam Facts

  • Scientific name: Gyps tenuirostris.
  • IUCN status: Critically Endangered.
  • It belongs to the vulture family.
  • Vulture conservation in India has been strongly linked with concerns over veterinary drugs such as diclofenac.
  • The species is important for ecosystem health and biodiversity conservation.
Exam Focus: Environment, Biodiversity, IUCN Red List and Wildlife Conservation.
