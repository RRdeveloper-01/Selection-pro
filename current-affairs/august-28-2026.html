Top 10 Current Affairs – 28 August 2026

Here are the most important Current Affairs for 28 August 2026, selected for SSC, UPSC, Banking, Railway, Defence and other competitive examinations.

1. Nepal Flood Disaster and International Relief Efforts

Severe flash floods and landslides in the Nepal-China border region have caused extensive damage and triggered major rescue operations. The disaster has affected local communities as well as foreign nationals and pilgrims travelling in the Himalayan region.

India and several other countries have offered assistance to Nepal. India has provided relief material as part of the international humanitarian response.

Exam Focus

  • Country: Nepal
  • Region: Himalayan border region
  • Topic: Flash Floods and Disaster Management
  • India's Role: Humanitarian and relief assistance

Why It Matters

This topic can be useful for questions related to India-Nepal relations, Himalayan geography, disaster management, climate risks and international humanitarian assistance.

2. India-China Continue Boundary Settlement Dialogue

India and China have continued diplomatic efforts to address their outstanding boundary issues. The latest Special Representatives dialogue in Beijing involved India's National Security Adviser Ajit Doval and Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi.

Both sides stressed the importance of maintaining peace and stability in border areas while continuing discussions towards a mutually acceptable settlement.

Important Facts

  • Indian Representative: Ajit Doval
  • Chinese Representative: Wang Yi
  • Key Issue: India-China Boundary Question
  • Important Term: LAC – Line of Actual Control

3. PM Modi's Upcoming Central Asia Visit and SCO Engagement

Prime Minister Narendra Modi is scheduled to travel to Central Asia for engagements connected with the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO). His programme includes participation in the SCO summit and bilateral discussions with leaders from the region.

A bilateral meeting with Russian President Vladimir Putin is also expected on the sidelines of the SCO summit in Bishkek.

Exam Focus

  • Organisation: Shanghai Cooperation Organisation
  • Upcoming Summit Location: Bishkek
  • Country: Kyrgyzstan
  • Important Bilateral Engagement: India-Russia

4. Partial Lunar Eclipse Occurs on 28 August 2026

A deep partial lunar eclipse is occurring around 28 August 2026. During a lunar eclipse, the Earth comes between the Sun and the Moon and Earth's shadow falls on the Moon.

The August 2026 lunar eclipse is not visible from India because the Moon is below the horizon during the relevant phase.

Science & Technology Exam Point

  • Event: Partial Lunar Eclipse
  • Date: 28 August 2026
  • Type: Deep Partial Lunar Eclipse
  • Visibility in India: Not visible

5. Anil Menon and Sophie Adenot Complete ISS Spacewalk

NASA astronaut Anil Menon and European Space Agency astronaut Sophie Adenot completed a spacewalk at the International Space Station to install a replacement communication antenna.

The mission involved work on the Space-to-Ground communication system of the ISS. The successful installation helped restore communication redundancy for the orbital laboratory.

Remember

  • Location: International Space Station
  • NASA Astronaut: Anil Menon
  • ESA Astronaut: Sophie Adenot
  • Task: Communication antenna installation
  • Field: Space Science

6. TCS to Acquire Porsche's IT Consulting Arm MHP

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) has announced an agreement to acquire 100% of MHP Management- und IT-Beratung GmbH, Porsche's Germany-based management and IT consulting subsidiary.

The transaction is valued at an enterprise value of €320 million. TCS and Porsche have also entered into a five-year strategic partnership focused on AI-powered mobility and digital transformation.

Business & Economy Exam Focus

  • Indian Company: TCS
  • Acquired Company: MHP
  • Parent Company: Porsche AG
  • Deal Value: €320 million enterprise value
  • Major Area: AI and digital mobility

7. India and Nepal Relations Remain in Focus After Flood Disaster

The devastating flood situation in Nepal has once again highlighted the importance of India-Nepal cooperation in disaster response and humanitarian assistance.

India has expressed solidarity with Nepal and has contributed relief assistance during the ongoing emergency response.

UPSC Exam Point

For UPSC preparation, revise India-Nepal relations, open borders, geographical connectivity, hydropower cooperation and disaster-management cooperation.

8. Monsoon Activity and Heavy Rainfall Remain Important

The India Meteorological Department has forecast renewed monsoon activity during the period from 26 August to 1 September, with heavy rainfall possible across several regions.

Weather developments are particularly important for understanding the Indian monsoon, floods, landslides, agriculture and disaster management.

Exam Preparation Tip

Revise the Indian monsoon system, monsoon branches, rainfall distribution, floods, landslides and disaster-management institutions.

9. India's Merchant Fleet Expansion Plans

India is planning to expand its merchant shipping capacity, with plans to add new vessels to the country's merchant fleet over the coming years.

Strengthening the merchant fleet is important for India's maritime economy, international trade and strategic shipping capacity.

Exam Focus

  • Sector: Maritime Transport
  • Importance: International Trade
  • Keyword: Merchant Fleet
  • Related Area: Blue Economy

10. Technology and Artificial Intelligence Continue to Drive Business Transformation

Artificial Intelligence and digital transformation continue to influence India's technology and manufacturing sectors. The recent TCS-Porsche strategic partnership is another example of the growing role of AI in mobility, engineering, manufacturing and customer experience.

Daily Exam Preparation Tip

For technology current affairs, remember the company, technology, purpose, country and major financial or strategic detail associated with the development.
