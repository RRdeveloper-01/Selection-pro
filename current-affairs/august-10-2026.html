Current Affair #1

India-US Strategic Partnership Discussed

Prime Minister Narendra Modi held a telephone conversation with US Vice President JD Vance on 10 August 2026.

The discussion focused on strengthening the India-US Comprehensive Global Strategic Partnership. The two sides discussed areas of cooperation including trade, defence, emerging and critical technologies, energy security and critical minerals.

Exam Point

India-US relations are described as a Comprehensive Global Strategic Partnership. Important areas include trade, defence, technology, energy and critical minerals.

Current Affair #2

Amarnath Yatra Suspended Due to Weather

The Amarnath Yatra has been suspended amid adverse weather conditions in Jammu and Kashmir.

The development comes near the scheduled conclusion of the 2026 pilgrimage period. Authorities have taken the decision keeping weather and pilgrim safety in consideration.

Remember

The Amarnath Cave shrine is located in Jammu and Kashmir and the pilgrimage is traditionally associated with Lord Shiva.

Current Affair #3

Jharkhand Exam Irregularities Remain in Focus

Students in Jharkhand have continued protests over alleged irregularities in recruitment examinations.

The issue has become an important state-level education and governance matter, with students demanding a transparent examination and recruitment process.

Why Important for Exams?

Questions related to examination reforms, transparency, governance and recruitment processes can be relevant to current-affairs based questions.

Current Affair #4

Ashmita Chaliha Wins Korea Masters

Indian badminton player Ashmita Chaliha created a major milestone by winning her maiden BWF World Tour title at the Korea Masters.

She defeated China's Han Qianxi in the women's singles final to claim the title.

Key Fact

Ashmita Chaliha became the first Indian woman to win the Korea Masters women's singles title.

India Test captain Shubman Gill returned to action in a warm-up match ahead of India's upcoming series against Sri Lanka.

Current Affair #7

Jorge Messi Dies at 68

Jorge Messi, father and longtime agent of Argentine football star Lionel Messi, died at the age of 68.

Jorge Messi played an important role in managing Lionel Messi's career and was involved in several major professional decisions during his son's rise in international football.

Sports Current Affairs

Jorge Messi was Lionel Messi's father and longtime career agent.

Current Affair #8

Spain Reviews Its Ambassador to India

Spain has moved to review its diplomatic representation in India following reports concerning alleged financial irregularities involving its ambassador.

The development is significant in the context of India-Spain diplomatic relations, especially as 2026 is being observed as the Spain-India Dual Year aimed at strengthening cooperation between the two countries.

Important for International Relations

India and Spain established diplomatic relations in 1956.

Current Affair #9

Proposal for Social Media Restrictions for Children

A BJP Member of Parliament has proposed a private member's bill seeking restrictions on social media access for children.

The proposal has brought issues such as child online safety, digital wellbeing, parental responsibility and social media regulation into public discussion.

Exam Relevance

Current Affair #10

Nagasaki Day Observed on 9 August

Nagasaki Day is observed every year on 9 August to remember the atomic bombing of Nagasaki, Japan, in 1945.

The day is associated with discussions on the humanitarian consequences of nuclear weapons and the importance of peace and nuclear disarmament.
