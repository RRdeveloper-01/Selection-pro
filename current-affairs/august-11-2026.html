Current Affair #1

Amit Shah Agrees to Address Parliament Amid Protest Row

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has agreed to address Parliament amid the Opposition's questions regarding the alleged use of force against protesters during the 'Sansad Chalo' march.

Union Parliamentary Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju announced that the government was ready for the Home Minister to address the Monsoon Session and appealed to the Opposition to maintain the decorum of Parliament.

📌 Exam Facts

  • Home Minister: Amit Shah
  • Parliamentary Affairs Minister: Kiren Rijiju
  • Session: Monsoon Session 2026
  • Topic: Parliamentary proceedings and protest-related issues
Current Affair #2

Assam Flood Death Toll Reaches 100

According to the Disaster Reporting and Information Management System (DRIMS) bulletin reported on 10 August, the death toll from the ongoing floods in Assam reached 100.

Among the affected districts, Sivasagar was reported to have the highest number of deaths, with 49 fatalities.

📌 Important for Exams

Assam is one of India's major flood-prone states because of its geographical location, river systems and monsoon rainfall.

Current Affair #3

Abu Dhabi Offers Free UAE Entry Visa to Eligible Indian Travellers

The Department of Culture and Tourism – Abu Dhabi has launched a tourism programme under which eligible Indian citizens can receive a UAE entry visa at no additional cost.

The programme is scheduled to run from 1 August to 31 October 2026.

To qualify, Indian travellers must book an eligible package that includes at least a three-night stay in Abu Dhabi through participating travel partners or online travel agencies.

🎯 Remember

Country: United Arab Emirates
Destination: Abu Dhabi
Programme: Free UAE entry visa for eligible Indian travellers
Period: 1 August – 31 October 2026

Current Affair #4

Sarfaraz Khan Replaces Injured Sai Sudharsan

Indian batter Sai Sudharsan has been ruled out of India's upcoming Test series against Sri Lanka because of a right-toe injury.

Mumbai batter Sarfaraz Khan has been named as his replacement in the Indian squad.

The Test series between India and Sri Lanka is scheduled to begin on 15 August 2026.

🏏 Sports Revision

  • Injured player: Sai Sudharsan
  • Replacement: Sarfaraz Khan
  • Opponent: Sri Lanka
  • Format: Test cricket
  • Series start: 15 August 2026
Current Affair #5

India Wins Two Medals at U20 World Championships

India achieved a historic double at the U20 World Championships in Eugene, USA.

Basant Kumar won the silver medal in the high jump event.

Shahnavaz Khan won the bronze medal in the long jump event.

🏆 Important Records

  • Basant Kumar became the first Indian high-jumper to win a medal at the U20 World Championships.
  • Shahnavaz Khan became the first Indian male long-jumper to win a medal at the championships.
Current Affair #6

NBFC Gold Loans Rise 69.3% Year-on-Year

According to data released by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), credit against gold jewellery provided by non-banking financial companies (NBFCs) increased sharply.

Gold-jewellery-backed credit increased by 69.3% year-on-year to around ₹3.41 lakh crore in June 2026.

The growth was significantly higher than the 40.6% recorded in June 2025.

💰 Banking Exam Facts

  • Institution: RBI
  • Sector: NBFC
  • Loan category: Gold jewellery-backed credit
  • Growth: 69.3% YoY
  • Amount: ₹3.41 lakh crore
  • Reference month: June 2026
Current Affair #7

Indian Railways Approves New Train Services

The Ministry of Railways approved the induction of two new trains in Uttar Pradesh and Gujarat.

The ministry also approved the extension of services of four trains and new stoppages for seven trains.

The changes are aimed at improving connectivity and providing passengers with additional travel options.

🚆 Quick Fact

The update is particularly relevant for questions related to Indian Railways, transport infrastructure and connectivity.

Current Affair #9

India's Ambassador-Designate to Ukraine Presents Letter of Credence

India's Ambassador-designate to Ukraine Anjani Kumar presented a copy of his Letter of Credence to Ukraine's Deputy Foreign Minister Olexandr Mischenko.

The meeting took place at Ukraine's Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Kyiv. Both sides discussed ways to further strengthen India–Ukraine bilateral relations.

🌍 International Relations

  • Country: Ukraine
  • Ukrainian official: Olexandr Mischenko
  • Key topic: Strengthening bilateral relations
Current Affair #10

Khudiram Bose Martyrdom Day Observed on 11 August

Khudiram Bose Martyrdom Day is observed every year on 11 August to remember one of India's youngest revolutionary freedom fighters.

Khudiram Bose was executed by the British colonial government on 11 August 1908 at the age of 18.

His sacrifice became an important symbol of courage and resistance during India's freedom struggle.

🇮🇳 Important History Facts

  • Name: Khudiram Bose
  • Martyrdom: 11 August 1908
  • Age at martyrdom: 18 years
  • Martyrdom Day: 11 August
