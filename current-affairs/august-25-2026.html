Current Affair #1

UPI Completes 10 Years of Operations

What happened?

India's Unified Payments Interface (UPI) completed ten years of operations in August 2026. UPI was launched in 2016 under the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) and has become one of India's most important digital payment systems.

Why it matters?

UPI has transformed digital payments in India by enabling instant bank-to-bank transactions through mobile applications. Its growth is important for India's digital economy and financial inclusion.

🎯 Exam Fact

UPI was launched in 2016 and is operated by NPCI.

❓ Possible Exam Question

UPI was launched under which organization?

Answer: National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)

Current Affair #2

India-China Hold 25th Round of Special Representatives Talks

What happened?

The 25th round of India-China Special Representatives talks on the boundary question is being held in Beijing on 25 August 2026. India's National Security Adviser Ajit Doval is participating in the discussions.

Why it matters?

The talks are important for India's foreign policy, border management and efforts to maintain peace and stability along the Line of Actual Control (LAC).

🎯 Exam Focus

Remember: Ajit Doval represents India as the Special Representative in the boundary talks.

Current Affair #3

India Changes Wheat Export Policy

What happened?

India changed the export policy for wheat, durum wheat and related wheat flour products from "Prohibited" to "Free" with immediate effect.

Why it matters?

Export policy decisions on agricultural commodities can influence domestic supply, prices, farmers and India's international agricultural trade.

📌 Quick Fact

Wheat export policy was changed from Prohibited to Free.

Current Affair #4

RBI Announces ₹1.75 Lakh Crore VRRR Auction

What happened?

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) scheduled a two-day Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) auction under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF). The notified amount was ₹1.75 lakh crore.

Why it matters?

VRRR operations are used by the RBI to manage liquidity conditions in the banking system.

🎯 Banking Exam Fact

VRRR = Variable Rate Reverse Repo
Tenor: 2 days
Notified amount: ₹1.75 lakh crore

Current Affair #5

Three Part-Time Directors Appointed to RBI Central Board

What happened?

The Central Government appointed Annie George Mathew, Janmejaya Sinha and Syed Akbaruddin as part-time, non-official directors on the Central Board of the RBI.

Their appointments are for a four-year term and became effective from 24 August 2026.

Why it matters?

RBI appointments are important for Banking and General Awareness sections of competitive examinations.

❓ Possible Question

Who is the central bank of India?

Answer: Reserve Bank of India (RBI)

Current Affair #6

SEBI Proposes Credit Risk-o-Meter for Debt Securities

What happened?

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) proposed a mandatory colour-coded Credit Risk-o-Meter for debt securities.

The proposed framework is intended to help investors understand and compare credit risks associated with debt securities.

Why it matters?

The proposal can improve transparency and make credit-risk information easier for investors to understand.

🎯 Banking & Finance Fact

The proposed Credit Risk-o-Meter uses six categories ranging from lower credit risk to high or very high risk of default.

Current Affair #7

Government Approves Incentive Scheme for Domestic PNG Connections

What happened?

The Central Government approved an Incentive Scheme for Promotion of Domestic Piped Natural Gas (PNG) Connections. The scheme is scheduled to become effective from 1 September 2026.

Key feature

Eligible City Gas Distribution entities can receive an additional 200 SCM of lower-priced domestically produced APM gas for every new billed domestic PNG connection, subject to the scheme's conditions.

Why it matters?

The initiative aims to encourage household PNG connections and promote cleaner cooking fuel.

📌 Quick Revision

PNG = Piped Natural Gas

Current Affair #8

IDFC FIRST Bank Executes Maiden $500 Million Bond Issue Through GIFT City

What happened?

IDFC FIRST Bank executed its maiden $500 million bond issuance through its International Financial Services Centre (IFSC) Banking Unit at GIFT City, Gandhinagar.

Why it matters?

The transaction marks the bank's entry into the global debt capital markets through its IFSC banking unit.

🎯 Exam Fact

GIFT City is located in Gandhinagar, Gujarat.

Current Affair #9

NITI Aayog Highlights Skilling Gaps in India

What happened?

NITI Aayog's Skill Development, Labour and Employment Division released a report titled "Reimagining Skilling for Viksit Bharat@2047".

Why it matters?

The report focuses on India's vocational training system, skill development and youth employability. Skill development is an important component of India's long-term development goals.

🎯 UPSC Exam Focus

The report connects skill development with India's broader vision of Viksit Bharat@2047.

Current Affair #10

India Launches First Non-GMO High-Expansion Popcorn Maize Hybrid

What happened?

India launched its first non-GMO high-expansion popcorn maize hybrid seed in Andhra Pradesh.

The launch took place at the Gourmet Popcornica facility in Musunuru, Eluru district.

Why it matters?

The development is relevant to agricultural science, crop innovation and India's efforts to develop improved agricultural varieties.

🌾 Quick Fact

The development relates to a non-GMO popcorn maize hybrid.
