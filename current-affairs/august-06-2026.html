1. Lok Sabha Passes Banking Laws (Evidence) Amendment Bill

The Lok Sabha passed the Banking Laws (Evidence) Amendment Bill, 2026 to modernise the legal framework governing banking records and digital evidence. The amendment is aimed at strengthening India's digital banking ecosystem by recognising electronic banking documents and improving the efficiency of legal proceedings involving financial institutions.

📌 Why is it in News?

The Bill was passed in the Lok Sabha to update existing banking laws in line with rapid digitalisation, online banking services and electronic record management.

Key Highlights

  • Electronic banking records receive stronger legal recognition.
  • Supports paperless banking and digital documentation.
  • Reduces dependency on physical records during legal proceedings.
  • Improves transparency and efficiency in banking operations.
  • Strengthens India's digital financial infrastructure.

🎯 Exam Relevance

Important for UPSC Economy, SSC General Awareness, Banking Awareness, RBI Grade B, NABARD, IBPS PO, SBI PO and State PCS examinations.

📚 Static GK

  • Reserve Bank of India (RBI) was established on 1 April 1935.
  • RBI Headquarters: Mumbai.
  • Current objective of RBI includes maintaining monetary stability and financial system stability.

2. RBI Plans Introduction of Polymer Currency Notes

The Reserve Bank of India is preparing to introduce polymer-based currency notes in selected denominations. Polymer notes are expected to last longer than traditional paper notes and include advanced security features to reduce counterfeiting.

📌 Why is it in News?

RBI has initiated preparations for the phased introduction of polymer currency notes as part of efforts to modernise India's currency management system.

Key Highlights

  • Polymer notes are more durable than cotton paper notes.
  • Better resistance against moisture, dust and tearing.
  • Advanced security features help reduce fake currency.
  • Longer circulation life lowers replacement costs.
  • Already used successfully in several countries.

🎯 Exam Relevance

Important for UPSC Economy, Banking Awareness, RBI, SSC, Railway, State PCS and other competitive examinations.

📚 Static GK

  • RBI Governor is appointed by the Central Government.
  • RBI regulates India's monetary policy.
  • Currency notes are issued under the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.

Practice MCQ

Q. Polymer currency notes are mainly introduced to improve:

  1. Tax collection
  2. Durability and security
  3. Foreign trade
  4. Gold reserves

Answer: B) Durability and security
