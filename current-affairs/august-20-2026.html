NTA Examination System Overhaul: The National Testing Agency is undergoing a major restructuring, including a four-tier question-paper checking system and changes to its expert panel.

Current Affair 01

NTA introduces four-tier checking system for question papers

The National Testing Agency has begun a major overhaul of its examination system. Around 600 existing experts have been removed from its examination team and new specialists are being inducted.

The agency is also introducing a four-tier scrutiny system for question papers to strengthen accuracy and examination security.

Why It Matters

NTA conducts several major entrance and eligibility examinations in India. Therefore, changes in its examination system are important for students.

📌 Exam Fact

NTA stands for National Testing Agency.

Possible Question

Which agency conducts examinations such as JEE Main and NEET-UG?

Answer: National Testing Agency (NTA)
Current Affair 02

CAT issues notice to UPSC over 8 disputed Prelims questions

The Central Administrative Tribunal has issued notice to the Union Public Service Commission after candidates challenged eight questions from the UPSC Civil Services Preliminary Examination 2026.

The petitioners have argued that errors or ambiguities in the questions could affect scores by more than 16 marks.

Why It Matters

The development is related to the fairness and evaluation process of one of India's most competitive examinations.

📌 Exam Fact

CAT stands for Central Administrative Tribunal.

Possible Question

Which tribunal issued notice to UPSC regarding disputed questions in Civil Services Prelims 2026?

Answer: Central Administrative Tribunal (CAT)

Current Affair 08

Maharashtra declares snakebite a notifiable disease

Maharashtra declared snakebite a notifiable disease under the state's public health framework.

The move requires reporting of suspected, probable and confirmed snakebite cases and deaths by relevant healthcare institutions.

Why It Matters

The decision strengthens disease surveillance and helps authorities track snakebite cases for public-health planning.

📌 Exam Fact

A notifiable disease is one that healthcare authorities require to be reported to government authorities.

Possible Question

Which state recently declared snakebite a notifiable disease?

Answer: Maharashtra

Why It Matters

CBAM is an important topic linking climate policy, international trade and carbon pricing.

📌 Exam Fact

CBAM stands for Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism.

Possible Question
