Current Affair 01

RBI Classifies Tata Sons as NBFC-Upper Layer

Why in News?

The Reserve Bank of India has classified Tata Sons Ltd. as an NBFC-Upper Layer (NBFC-UL) for 2026–27. Its application for de-registration as an NBFC is still under examination.

Key Facts

  • NBFC-UL is part of RBI's Scale Based Regulation framework.
  • NBFCs are regulated according to their size, complexity and systemic importance.
  • The Upper Layer contains systemically important large NBFCs.
  • For 2026–27, an asset-size threshold of ₹1 lakh crore is relevant for NBFC-UL identification.
  • NBFC-UL entities face enhanced regulatory, governance and disclosure requirements.

Exam Takeaway

  • Regulator: RBI
  • Framework: Scale Based Regulation
  • Category: NBFC-Upper Layer
  • Important concept: Systemic financial stability
Current Affair 02

₹1 Lakh Crore RDI Fund Faces Transparency Questions

Why in News?

Questions have been raised over the selection process under India's Research, Development and Innovation (RDI) Fund, a major initiative aimed at supporting deep-tech research and innovation in the private sector.

Key Facts

  • The RDI Fund has a corpus of ₹1 lakh crore.
  • It focuses on long-term financing for research, development and innovation.
  • Priority areas include AI, quantum technology, space, defence, robotics and semiconductors.
  • The fund is administered through the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF).
  • The first funding round involved applications from technology companies.

Important Governance Point

Recent reporting has raised concerns about possible conflicts of interest in the selection process. The issue highlights the importance of transparent, independent and merit-based allocation of public funds.

Current Affair 03

New Legislative Framework for UPI Merchant Charges

Why in News?

The Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026 introduced changes that create a legal framework for possible charges, including Merchant Discount Rate (MDR), on notified digital payment transactions.

Important Clarification

This does not mean that a general "UPI tax" has suddenly been imposed on users. The proposed framework concerns the possibility of merchant-side charges on eligible transactions.

Key Points

  • UPI is operated through India's digital payments ecosystem led by NPCI.
  • MDR means Merchant Discount Rate.
  • MDR is generally a fee associated with processing digital payments.
  • The development is important for digital payments, financial sustainability and payment regulation.

Current Affair 06

High-Resolution Images Reveal New Details of the Sun

Why in News?

Scientists have obtained exceptionally detailed images of the Sun using the Daniel K. Inouye Solar Telescope (DKIST) in Hawaii.

Key Facts

  • DKIST is operated by the National Solar Observatory.
  • It is a major ground-based solar telescope.
  • The telescope has a 4-metre primary mirror.
  • The observations revealed extremely fine structures and dynamic patterns in the Sun's outer layers.
  • Such observations can improve understanding of solar activity and space weather.

Exam Takeaway

  • Telescope: Daniel K. Inouye Solar Telescope
  • Location: Maui, Hawaii
  • Mirror: 4 metres
  • Field: Solar astronomy
Current Affair 07

Government School Numbers Decline Over the Decade

Why in News?

Recent analysis of government education data highlighted a significant decline in the number of government schools over the 2014–15 to 2024–25 period.

Important Data

  • Government schools declined by around 94,000 over the decade.
  • Total schools declined by roughly 45,000.
  • The number of teachers increased during the period.
  • The issue has raised questions around school consolidation, accessibility and enrolment.

Education is a subject in the Concurrent List, meaning both the Union and State Governments have legislative responsibilities in this area.

Current Affair 08

National Handloom Day 2026

Why in News?

National Handloom Day was observed on 7 August 2026 to recognise India's handloom sector, traditional weaving communities and the cultural heritage associated with Indian textiles.

Key Facts

  • National Handloom Day is observed on 7 August.
  • The day commemorates the launch of the Swadeshi Movement in 1905.
  • It promotes awareness about India's handloom traditions and weavers.
  • The 2026 celebrations marked the 12th National Handloom Day.
  • The Ministry of Textiles is the key Union ministry associated with the sector.

Quick Memory Trick

7 August → National Handloom Day → Swadeshi Movement → 1905.
