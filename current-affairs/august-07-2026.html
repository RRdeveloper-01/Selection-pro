Q1. The Supreme Court recently rejected the interim bail plea of which self-styled spiritual leader?

A. Gurmeet Ram Rahim

B. Asaram

C. Nithyananda

D. None of the above

Answer: B. Asaram

Explanation: The Supreme Court rejected Asaram's interim bail plea, making it an important legal current affairs topic.

Q2. Which cricketer recently became the highest run-scorer in T20 cricket?

A. Virat Kohli

B. Rohit Sharma

C. Jos Buttler

D. Babar Azam

Answer: C. Jos Buttler

Explanation: England's Jos Buttler achieved the milestone of becoming the highest run-scorer in T20 cricket.

Q3. Which organization issued heavy rainfall alerts for several Indian states?

A. ISRO

B. IMD

C. NDRF

D. CPCB

Answer: B. IMD

Explanation: The India Meteorological Department (IMD) issued rainfall alerts due to active monsoon conditions.

Q4. Delimitation in India is mainly related to:

A. Tax Collection

B. Drawing Electoral Constituencies

C. Defence Planning

D. Banking Regulation

Answer: B. Drawing Electoral Constituencies

Explanation: Delimitation means redrawing the boundaries of parliamentary and assembly constituencies.

Q5. EPFO primarily works under which Union Ministry?

A. Ministry of Finance

B. Ministry of Labour & Employment

C. Ministry of Commerce

D. Ministry of Home Affairs

Answer: B. Ministry of Labour & Employment

Explanation: EPFO functions under the Ministry of Labour & Employment.

Q6. The proposed India–US BTA stands for:

A. Bilateral Trade Agreement

B. Banking Transfer Act

C. Border Transit Agreement

D. Business Tax Agreement

Answer: A. Bilateral Trade Agreement

Explanation: BTA refers to the proposed Bilateral Trade Agreement between India and the United States.

Q7. Which force is primarily deployed during flood rescue operations in India?

A. CRPF

B. BSF

C. NDRF

D. CISF

Answer: C. NDRF

Explanation: The National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) is India's specialized disaster response force.

Q8. IMD functions under which Ministry?

B. Ministry of Environment

C. Ministry of Home Affairs

D. Ministry of Agriculture

Answer: A. Ministry of Earth Sciences

Explanation: IMD operates under the Ministry of Earth Sciences.

Q9. Which season is currently active in most parts of India during August?

B. Summer

C. Southwest Monsoon

D. Retreating Monsoon

Explanation: August is part of the Southwest Monsoon season in India.

Q10. Daily Current Affairs are most important for which of the following exams?
