Economy Current Affairs 15 September 2026: India Economy News, Rupee, Oil & Markets

1. India's Retail Inflation Rises to 4.82% in August 2026

India's annual retail inflation based on the Consumer Price Index (CPI) increased to 4.82% in August 2026 from 4.45% in July. The rise was driven by broader price pressures across several categories, while core inflation also increased. The latest inflation reading has increased attention on India's monetary policy outlook.

Exam Point: August 2026 CPI inflation — 4.82%. July 2026 CPI inflation — 4.45%.

2. RBI Announces ₹1 Lakh Crore Government Bond Sale to Absorb Liquidity

The Reserve Bank of India announced a plan to sell government bonds worth ₹1 lakh crore through open market operations beginning September 16. The move is aimed at absorbing excess liquidity from the banking system and keeping short-term market rates aligned with the monetary policy framework.

Exam Point: RBI's Open Market Operations (OMO) can be used to manage liquidity in the financial system.

3. Indian Rupee Faces Pressure from High Oil Prices

The Indian rupee has remained under pressure as crude oil prices moved higher and global bond yields increased. Higher oil prices can increase India's import bill and demand for US dollars, creating additional pressure on the rupee.

The currency also recorded a sharp weekly decline recently, with market participants closely watching crude prices, foreign capital flows and global monetary policy.

Exam Point: Higher crude oil prices can affect India's import bill, current account, inflation and exchange rate.

4. Crude Oil Prices Rise Above $100 Amid Middle East Supply Risks

Global crude oil prices have remained above the $100-per-barrel level amid concerns over supply disruptions linked to escalating Middle East tensions. The rise in oil prices has increased concerns about inflation and energy security in oil-importing economies such as India.

Exam Point: For India, crude oil prices are closely connected with inflation, import expenditure, transport costs and the current account.

5. Global Interest-Rate Expectations Increase Market Volatility

Rising energy prices and renewed inflation concerns have increased uncertainty in global financial markets. Investors are closely watching the US Federal Reserve, bond yields, currencies and commodity prices. Changes in global interest rates can influence capital flows into emerging markets including India.

Exam Point: Global interest rates can influence foreign investment, bond yields, currency markets and emerging-market financial conditions.
