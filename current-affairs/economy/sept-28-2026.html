1. Private Sector Capex Projected to Rise to ₹3.2 Trillion in FY27

What Happened: An RBI bulletin reported that private-sector capital expenditure is projected to rise to about ₹3.2 trillion in FY27.

Why It Matters: Higher private investment can support capacity creation, infrastructure and economic activity.

Exam Fact: Capex means capital expenditure on long-term assets.

2. India Monitors Oil Supply and Price Risks

What Happened: Indian refiners continue to monitor global crude supply and prices as geopolitical developments affect the energy market.

Why It Matters: International crude prices influence India's inflation and external-sector conditions.

Exam Fact: Crude oil is one of India's major import commodities.

3. Government Plans ₹7.8 Trillion Gross Market Borrowing in H2 FY27

What Happened: The government plans to raise ₹7.8 trillion through government securities in the second half of FY27.

Why It Matters: Government borrowing helps finance expenditure and also affects bond markets and interest rates.

Exam Fact: Government securities are debt instruments issued by the government.

4. CBIC Eases Paperwork for Eligible UK Imports

What Happened: CBIC has eased documentation requirements for claiming concessional duty on eligible imports from the UK.

Why It Matters: Trade facilitation can reduce paperwork and improve cross-border commerce.

Exam Fact: CBIC stands for Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs.

5. India Advances Bilateral Investment Treaty Engagement

What Happened: India is close to concluding investment treaties with several partners while work continues on an updated investment-treaty framework.

Why It Matters: Investment treaties establish rules relevant to cross-border investment.

Exam Fact: BIT stands for Bilateral Investment Treaty.
