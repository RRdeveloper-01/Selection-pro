1. India's Goods Exports Rise 26% to $43.81 Billion in August

India's merchandise exports increased by about 26.12% year-on-year to $43.81 billion in August 2026. The growth was significantly faster than the increase in imports during the month.

Engineering goods, electronics and several other export sectors contributed to the improvement. The stronger export performance also helped narrow India's merchandise trade deficit.

India Economy News Exam Point: August 2026 merchandise exports — $43.81 billion; year-on-year growth — about 26%.

2. UPI Transactions Above ₹2,000 to Attract 0.4% MDR

A new Merchant Discount Rate framework for specified person-to-merchant UPI transactions above ₹2,000 will come into effect from 15 October 2026.

The standard MDR has been set at 0.4% for eligible transactions, while exemptions and lower rates apply to specified categories. Person-to-person UPI transfers remain outside this merchant-fee framework.

UPI News Exam Point: MDR — 0.4% for specified merchant UPI transactions above ₹2,000; effective from 15 October 2026.

3. RBI Uses Foreign Exchange Market to Limit Rupee Volatility

The Indian rupee has remained under pressure because of high crude oil prices, strong dollar demand and expectations surrounding US monetary policy.

Reuters reported that the Reserve Bank of India likely intervened through state-run banks to help limit excessive movement in the currency market.

RBI News Exam Point: RBI uses foreign-exchange intervention as one tool to manage excessive currency-market volatility.

4. India-Canada CEPA Talks Continue in New Delhi

India and Canada have begun the fourth round of negotiations for a proposed Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement.

The talks cover goods, services, rules of origin and technical trade barriers. Both sides have targeted stronger bilateral trade, with an objective of reaching $50 billion in two-way trade by 2030.

Trade News Exam Point: India-Canada CEPA negotiations; target of $50 billion bilateral trade by 2030.

5. Indian Stock Markets Remain Sensitive to Oil and Global Rates

Indian equity markets continue to monitor crude oil prices, global bond yields and expectations around US interest rates.

Higher crude prices can increase India's import costs and influence inflation, while global interest rates can affect capital flows into emerging markets.

Business News Exam Point: Crude oil prices, global interest rates, inflation and foreign capital flows are important factors affecting Indian markets.
