Economy News 22 September 2026: Rupee, RBI & Global Markets

1. RBI Raises FCNR(B) Deposit Inflow Estimate to $133 Billion

What happened: The Reserve Bank of India has revised upward its estimate of foreign-currency non-resident deposit inflows under the special FCNR(B) scheme after a strong response from NRIs.

Why it matters: The inflows can add to foreign-currency resources and influence external-sector conditions.

Exam Fact: FCNR(B) accounts are foreign-currency-denominated deposits for eligible NRIs.

2. Rupee Trades Near ₹95.8 Per Dollar

What happened: The Indian rupee ended the previous session marginally stronger at around ₹95.815 per US dollar while oil prices and importer demand remained important factors.

Why it matters: Currency movements affect import costs, inflation and external payments.

Exam Fact: The rupee-dollar exchange rate shows how many rupees are needed for one US dollar.

3. RBI Liquidity Management Remains in Focus

What happened: Indian bond and money markets are monitoring RBI liquidity operations as surplus liquidity and monetary-policy expectations influence short-term rates.

Why it matters: Liquidity conditions affect money-market rates and policy transmission.

Exam Fact: RBI uses several instruments to manage system liquidity.

4. Crude Oil Remains Important for India’s Inflation Outlook

What happened: Higher global crude prices remain an external risk because India imports a large share of its crude requirement.

Why it matters: Energy prices can affect inflation, the current account and the rupee.

Exam Fact: Crude oil is one of India’s major import commodities.

5. Markets Track Global Rates and Foreign Flows

What happened: Indian equities and bonds are being influenced by global interest-rate expectations, oil prices and foreign portfolio flows.

Why it matters: Global financial conditions can affect capital flows and domestic asset prices.

Exam Fact: FPI means Foreign Portfolio Investment in financial assets.
