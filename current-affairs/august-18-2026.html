Current Affair #2

National Mass Pledge Against Drug Abuse Held on 18 August

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment has organised a nationwide National Mass Pledge Against Drug Abuse on 18 August 2026. The programme marks the 6th anniversary of Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan.

📌 Important Facts

  • Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan was launched on 15 August 2020.
  • The campaign is under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.
  • De-addiction helpline: 14446.

Edition: 15th
Countries: India and Thailand

Current Affair #6

India's Foreign Exchange Reserves Cross $700 Billion

India's foreign exchange reserves rose to around $707 billion as of 7 August 2026, reaching a four-month high. The increase was mainly supported by a rise in foreign currency assets and strong dollar inflows.

💰 Important Numbers

  • Total forex reserves: $707 billion
  • Foreign currency assets: $574.6 billion
  • Gold reserves: $108.7 billion
Current Affair #7

RBI to Close Discounted FX Deposit Swap Facility Earlier

The Reserve Bank of India decided to close its discounted foreign-exchange swap facility for banks earlier than originally planned. The deadline was moved from 30 September to 31 August 2026 after strong inflows through the facility.

🏦 Exam Point

Institution: Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
New deadline: 31 August 2026

Current Affair #8

India Sets Cooking Gas Production Target

The Indian government has directed oil companies to increase domestic cooking gas production amid global energy-supply concerns. The government order set a maximum daily production target of 63,810 metric tonnes for cooking gas by state-run and private refineries.

⛽ Important Fact

Daily production target: 63,810 metric tonnes

Current Affair #9

FIH Hockey World Cup 2026 Underway

The FIH Hockey World Cup 2026 is being jointly hosted by Belgium and the Netherlands from 15 to 30 August 2026. India's men's team is placed in Pool D along with England, Pakistan and Wales. The Indian women's team is also in Pool D with China, England and South Africa.

🏑 Exam Point

Host countries: Belgium and Netherlands
Dates: 15–30 August 2026
India Men's Pool: Pool D
