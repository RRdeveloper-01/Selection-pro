CURRENT AFFAIR 01 SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY

National Space Day 2026 observed on 23 August

India observes National Space Day on 23 August 2026. The day commemorates the successful soft landing of the Chandrayaan-3 Vikram lander near the Moon's south pole on 23 August 2023.

The observance highlights India's achievements in space science, technology and exploration and encourages interest in space research among students and young innovators.

Why it matters for exams?

National Space Day is highly relevant for SSC Science GK, Railway GK, UPSC Prelims and Defence exams.

Exam Fact

23 August is observed as National Space Day in India in connection with the Chandrayaan-3 lunar landing.

Possible Question

National Space Day is observed in India on which date?

Answer: 23 August

CURRENT AFFAIR 04 GOVERNANCE

High-level committee formed to work on a political solution to Gorkha issues

Union Home Minister Amit Shah has announced the formation of a high-level committee to work towards a permanent political solution to issues concerning the Gorkhas.

The committee will work within the framework of the Indian Constitution. The announcement followed a meeting with stakeholders in Siliguri.

Why it matters?

The development is relevant to questions related to Indian federalism, regional issues, governance and constitutional processes.

Exam Fact

The committee is associated with finding a permanent political solution to Gorkha-related issues.

Possible Question

Which Union Minister announced the formation of a high-level committee on Gorkha issues?

Answer: Amit Shah

Government takes measures to control rising sugar prices

The Government of India has taken measures to ensure adequate availability of sugar and contain the impact of rising sugar prices during the festive season.

Why it matters?
