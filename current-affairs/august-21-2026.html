01 DEFENCE & INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS

India and Japan Deepen Defence and Maritime Security Cooperation

India and Japan held their Defence Ministers Meeting in New Delhi. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and Japanese Defence Minister Shinjiro Koizumi co-chaired the meeting.

Both sides agreed to deepen defence technology cooperation, maritime security cooperation and strategic coordination. They also signed a Memorandum of Arrangement on Maritime Security Cooperation.

Why It Matters

The development is important for India's Indo-Pacific strategy and maritime security. The two countries also discussed cooperation related to defence technology and freedom of navigation.

Exam Fact

India and Japan signed a Memorandum of Arrangement on Maritime Security Cooperation in New Delhi.

Possible Question

India and Japan recently signed an MoA related to which area?

Answer: Maritime Security Cooperation

04 INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS

4th India-Singapore Ministerial Roundtable Held in Singapore

The fourth round of the India-Singapore Ministerial Roundtable (ISMR) was held in Singapore on 20 August 2026.

India's Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman, External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar, Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal and Minister of State Jitin Prasada participated.

Discussions covered digital technology, Digital Public Infrastructure, semiconductor supply chains, AI governance, cybersecurity and advanced manufacturing.

Why It Matters

India-Singapore relations and the Comprehensive Strategic Partnership are important areas for international-relations questions in competitive examinations.

Exam Fact

ISMR stands for: India-Singapore Ministerial Roundtable

Possible Question

What is the full form of ISMR?

Answer: India-Singapore Ministerial Roundtable

05 POLITY & GOVERNANCE

Union Cabinet Approves High Court Bench for Ladakh

The Union Cabinet approved the establishment of a Bench of the High Court of Jammu and Kashmir in Ladakh.

The decision is aimed at improving access to justice and strengthening judicial infrastructure for people living in the Union Territory of Ladakh.

Why It Matters

The development is relevant to Indian Polity, governance and current-affairs questions, particularly for UPSC, SSC and State PCS examinations.

Exam Fact

The approved High Court Bench is for: Ladakh

Possible Question

Which Union Territory will get a Bench of the High Court of Jammu and Kashmir?

Answer: Ladakh
