1. Kyiv comes under Russian missile attack

What happened: Ukraine's capital Kyiv came under attack by Russian missiles on Friday, according to Kyiv mayor Vitali Klitschko; there were no immediate reports of casualties or damage in the cited report.

Why it matters: The incident remains relevant to international relations and European security.

Source: Reuters summary

2. Russia-China veto blocks extension of UN Iran sanctions monitoring

What happened: Russia and China vetoed a UN Security Council resolution seeking to extend the mandate of an independent panel monitoring sanctions on Iran.

Why it matters: The vote is significant for the UN Security Council's sanctions regime and diplomacy around Iran.

Source: Reuters

3. IAEA informed of drone strike affecting Kursk nuclear plant cooling tower

What happened: The International Atomic Energy Agency was informed that a cooling tower at a reactor unit of Russia's Kursk Nuclear Power Plant was hit by a drone.

Why it matters: The incident highlights the nuclear-safety dimension of the Russia-Ukraine conflict.

Source: AFP / NDTV

4. Global AI governance remains a major international policy issue

What happened: International discussions continue to focus on responsible AI, safety, technology standards and cooperation while major powers compete over advanced AI capabilities.

Why it matters: The issue connects technology with diplomacy, regulation and global governance.

Source: Associated Press

5. Bolivia's Congress approves a $1.9 billion IMF agreement

What happened: Bolivia's Senate ratified a three-year $1.9 billion IMF financing programme after the lower house approved it.

Why it matters: The programme is intended to replenish foreign reserves and stabilise an economy facing high inflation and weak growth.

Source: AP
