1. BRICS Leaders Call for Restraint Amid Rising Middle East Tensions

BRICS leaders adopted the New Delhi Declaration during the 2026 summit in New Delhi. The declaration expressed concern over the worsening situation in the Middle East and called for restraint, dialogue and diplomatic efforts to address international conflicts.

The development is important for world politics because BRICS includes major countries from Asia, Africa and other parts of the Global South.

World News Exam Point: New Delhi Declaration 2026, BRICS, Global South, multilateral diplomacy and international cooperation.

2. India-China Relations Remain in Focus After Modi-Xi Meeting

India-China relations remained a major topic in international news after Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Chinese President Xi Jinping met during the BRICS Summit in New Delhi.

Both sides discussed improving bilateral ties, business and transport connectivity and maintaining peace and stability along the border.

International News Exam Point: India-China relations, BRICS diplomacy, border stability, trade and regional connectivity.

3. Trump-Xi Meeting Expected to Focus on Trade, AI and Global Issues

US President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping are expected to meet later in September. The proposed discussions are expected to cover major issues including trade, technology, artificial intelligence, Taiwan and international conflicts.

The meeting is being closely watched because US-China relations have a major impact on global trade, technology supply chains and international diplomacy.

Global News Exam Point: US-China relations, trade, AI, Taiwan, technology and global diplomacy.

4. Middle East Developments Continue to Affect Global Energy Security

Continuing tensions in the Middle East are creating concerns for international shipping and global energy supplies. Recent attacks and disruptions involving important regional routes have increased attention on the security of oil transportation.

The developments are being closely followed by countries around the world because disruption to major energy routes can influence oil prices, inflation and international trade.

World News Exam Point: Middle East, energy security, international shipping, oil supply routes and global trade.

5. Europe Pushes for Greater Independence in Artificial Intelligence

European policymakers are increasingly focusing on building stronger domestic artificial intelligence capabilities and data-centre infrastructure.

European Central Bank President Christine Lagarde has warned that excessive dependence on AI technologies developed outside Europe could create economic and geopolitical vulnerabilities.

International Technology News Exam Point: AI sovereignty, data centres, technology dependence, digital infrastructure and Europe.
