1. US Forces Prepare to Leave Remaining Bases in Iraq

The United States is preparing to vacate its remaining military bases in Iraq after more than two decades of major military presence. The development is being watched for implications for regional security and Iraq's strategic environment.

Exam Fact: Iraq is a West Asian country and a UN member state.

2. Iran Links Regional Security With Oil Exports

Iranian officials have linked regional energy exports with security and stability. The issue comes amid continuing attention to oil supply routes and geopolitical tensions in West Asia that can affect global energy markets.

Exam Fact: Iran is a major Middle Eastern country with significant oil resources.

3. UN General Assembly Continues High-Level Diplomacy

The 81st United Nations General Assembly continues to provide a platform for world leaders and senior representatives to discuss international peace, development, security and cooperation.

Exam Fact: The United Nations General Assembly has 193 member states.

4. Global Energy Markets Closely Watch Middle East Developments

International oil markets remain sensitive to developments affecting crude production, exports and shipping routes in the Middle East. Energy-price changes can influence inflation, trade balances and growth.

Exam Fact: Brent crude is a major international oil-price benchmark.

5. International Attention on Cross-Border Digital Crime

Countries continue strengthening cooperation against crimes involving digital technology and cross-border networks. Effective action requires information sharing, legal coordination and technical capabilities across jurisdictions.

Exam Fact: Cybercrime can involve infrastructure, victims and offenders in different countries.
