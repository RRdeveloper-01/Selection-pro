1. Trump Addresses the 81st UN General Assembly

What happened: US President Donald Trump addressed the high-level General Debate of the 81st United Nations General Assembly in New York.

Why it matters: The UNGA General Debate is a major forum for countries to present positions on international issues and global cooperation.

Exam Fact: The United Nations General Assembly has 193 member states.

What happened: Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian said Iran would not surrender to the United States while indicating openness to diplomatic talks.

Why it matters: The statement is relevant to continuing discussions over regional security and US-Iran relations.

Exam Fact: Iran is a member of the United Nations and is located in West Asia.

3. Zelenskyy Raises Issue of Foreign Nationals in Russia's War

What happened: Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy raised at the UN General Assembly the issue of foreign nationals being involved in Russia's war against Ukraine.

Why it matters: The issue connects the conflict with international recruitment, diplomacy and responsibilities of states.

What happened: The 81st UN General Assembly high-level week continues discussions on conflicts, development, climate, technology and international cooperation.

Why it matters: The annual gathering provides a platform for all UN member states to participate in multilateral diplomacy.

Exam Fact: The General Assembly is one of the six principal organs of the United Nations.

5. US Court Orders Restoration of White House Press Access

What happened: A US federal judge ordered the Trump administration to restore White House press access for CNN, MS NOW and Politico, according to reports.

Why it matters: The case has raised questions around government press access and constitutional protections in the United States.

Exam Fact: The First Amendment of the US Constitution protects freedom of speech and freedom of the press.
