World News & International Current Affairs 9 September 2026: Global Affairs

World news and international developments selected for competitive exams, with focus on geopolitics, trade, climate and global affairs.

Published: 9 September 2026 · Exam-focused current affairs.

1. Canada's retaliatory tariffs on US goods take effect

Canada brought retaliatory tariffs on about $20 billion of US goods into effect after trade talks stalled. The measures range from 15% to 50% across selected products and have intensified the trade dispute between the two North American partners.

Exam Point: The dispute has implications for the USMCA, the North American free-trade framework.

2. Russia and North Korea open first road bridge across their shared border

Russia and North Korea inaugurated their first dedicated road bridge across the Tumen River. The roughly 1-kilometre, two-lane bridge is intended to strengthen transport, trade and broader economic links between the two countries.

Exam Point: The bridge complements the existing rail connection between Russia and North Korea.

3. Scientists warn of rising risks from climate-linked glacial collapse

Researchers are increasingly examining links between climate change and rapid glacier destabilisation. Such events can raise risks of floods, landslides and downstream disruption, keeping climate adaptation and disaster-risk reduction high on the global agenda.

Exam Point: Glacier retreat and ice instability are important indicators studied in climate-change assessments.

4. Amazon cargo aircraft crashes at Miami airport

A cargo aircraft operated by Amazon crashed at Miami International Airport, with multiple fatalities reported. The incident became one of the major international transport stories in the September 9 news cycle and highlights the importance of aviation safety investigations.

Exam Point: Aviation accidents are investigated to identify operational, technical and regulatory causes.

5. Eiffel Tower closure highlights labour dispute in France

The Eiffel Tower was temporarily affected by a staff protest in Paris. The development drew international attention because the landmark is one of France's most recognisable tourism and cultural symbols.

Exam Point: Paris is the capital of France, and the Eiffel Tower is located on the Champ de Mars.

Quick Revision: Most Important Exam Points

  • The Miami cargo-aircraft crash became a major international aviation story.
  • The Eiffel Tower is a major tourism landmark in Paris, France.
