1. Pakistan Extends Airspace Restrictions on Indian Aircraft Until October 24

Pakistan has extended its restrictions on Indian-registered aircraft and aircraft operated, owned or leased by Indian airlines and operators. The restriction has been extended until October 24, 2026, according to a Notice to Airmen issued by the Pakistan Airports Authority.

The restriction applies to the Karachi and Lahore flight information regions. The development is important for regional aviation, connectivity and India-Pakistan relations.

World News Exam Point: Pakistan's airspace restriction on Indian aircraft has been extended until October 24, 2026.

2. UK Inflation Rises to Five-Month High

Inflation in the United Kingdom has risen to a five-month high, increasing attention on the Bank of England's monetary-policy decisions.

Higher fuel prices and broader price pressures have contributed to the increase. The inflation data is important for global markets because changes in major central-bank policies can influence currencies, bonds and investment flows.

International News Exam Point: UK inflation has reached a five-month high, putting monetary policy and interest rates in focus.

3. India and Nepal Discuss Stronger Bilateral Relations

India's outgoing Ambassador to Nepal, Naveen Srivastava, met Nepal's Foreign Minister Shisir Khanal.

The discussions focused on strengthening the multifaceted bilateral relationship between India and Nepal. India-Nepal relations cover areas such as trade, connectivity, energy, culture and people-to-people links.

International Affairs Exam Point: India and Nepal continue diplomatic engagement focused on strengthening bilateral cooperation.

4. Global Survey Shows Rising Support for International Cooperation

A global survey covering more than 35,000 people across 34 countries found increased support for international cooperation.

The survey was conducted between July 21 and August 5, 2026. It also examined public views about global institutions and leadership amid economic and security concerns.

Global News Exam Point: The survey covered more than 35,000 respondents across 34 countries.

5. China Opens Major Pinglu Canal to Strengthen Regional Trade Connectivity

China has opened the Pinglu Canal, a major infrastructure project designed to create a new maritime route for the country's southwestern region.

The project is expected to provide a shorter connection toward the sea and improve trade connectivity with Southeast Asia.

World News Exam Point: The Pinglu Canal is designed to improve maritime connectivity between China's southwestern hinterland and the sea.
