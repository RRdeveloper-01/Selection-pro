Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy के अनुसार drone ने Volodymyrska Street स्थित SBU building को hit किया। यह building St. Sophia Cathedral के सामने स्थित है। Reuters के अनुसार हमले में पांच लोग घायल हुए। 1

3. Two Workers Rescued Alive After Nine Days from Nepal Hydropower Tunnel

Nepal में एक महत्वपूर्ण rescue operation में दो hydropower workers को Trishuli 3A hydropower tunnel से नौ दिन बाद जीवित बाहर निकाला गया।

Workers Sanjay Sah और Kabir Maharjan को tunnel के अंदर से rescue किया गया। दोनों workers 26 August 2026 को हुए devastating ice, rock और mudslide के बाद tunnel में फंस गए थे। 4

Nepal disaster

यह disaster Trishuli River valley में हुआ और इससे कई hydropower projects प्रभावित हुए। Rescue teams ने कई locations पर trapped workers को निकालने के लिए लगातार अभियान चलाया। 5

Important International Affairs Terms for Competitive Exams

SBU

SBU stands for Security Service of Ukraine. It is Ukraine's main domestic security and intelligence service.

UN General Assembly

The United Nations General Assembly is one of the principal organs of the United Nations where all UN member states are represented.

Hybrid Warfare

Hybrid warfare refers to the combination of different forms of pressure or conflict, including conventional military activity, cyber operations, disinformation and other non-traditional methods.

Climate Justice

Climate justice focuses on fairness in addressing climate change, including the unequal impacts of climate-related disasters on different countries and communities.
