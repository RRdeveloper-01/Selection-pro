International Current Affairs 8 September 2026: World News & Global Affairs

Important international current affairs for 8 September 2026, covering the Nepal flood disaster, West Asia tensions, North Korea's naval developments, Singapore's social cohesion and global climate concerns. These topics are useful for UPSC, SSC, State PCS and interview preparation.

Published: 8 September 2026 · Category: International · Exam-focused Current Affairs

1. Nepal faces severe flood disaster as rescue operations continue

Nepal continues rescue and recovery operations after severe flooding and landslides affected several parts of the country. Reports indicate that the disaster has caused more than 1,300 deaths, while thousands of people remain missing.

The disaster has created major humanitarian and infrastructure challenges and has also highlighted the importance of regional disaster-management cooperation.

Exam Point: Nepal is an important country in India's neighbourhood. Disaster assistance and humanitarian cooperation are significant parts of India's neighbourhood policy.

2. West Asia tensions: evacuation warning issued in southern Lebanon

Tensions in West Asia remain high, with Israel's military ordering residents to evacuate parts of southern Lebanon ahead of military strikes.

The development is important from the perspective of West Asian geopolitics, regional security and the safety of civilians and foreign nationals in conflict zones.

Why important for exams? West Asia is strategically important for India because of energy security, trade routes, diaspora interests and India's relations with countries in the region.

3. North Korea commissions second naval destroyer

North Korea has commissioned its second naval destroyer amid continuing security tensions on the Korean Peninsula.

The development comes as the United States, South Korea and Japan continue military cooperation and exercises focused on regional security.

Region Korean Peninsula
Key countries North Korea, South Korea, United States and Japan
Exam Point: The Korean Peninsula is a major geopolitical flashpoint in East Asia because of North Korea's nuclear and missile programmes.

4. Singapore stresses zero tolerance for prejudice against communities

Singapore Prime Minister Lawrence Wong has stressed that the country should not tolerate prejudice or hostility against any community amid concerns over anti-Indian abuse.

Singapore's response highlights the importance of social harmony and multiculturalism in a diverse society. It is also relevant to India's diaspora relations with Southeast Asian countries.

Interview Point: Singapore is a multi-ethnic society where maintaining social cohesion among different communities is an important element of public policy.

5. UN warns that wildfires and heatwaves threaten air quality and health

The United Nations has warned about the growing impact of wildfires and extreme heat on air quality and public health.

Wildfires can release large amounts of particulate matter and other pollutants into the atmosphere, while heatwaves can increase health risks and worsen environmental conditions.

Exam Connection: The issue connects climate change, extreme weather, disaster risk reduction, air pollution and sustainable development.
