Current Affair 01

Finance Minister holds discussions with bankers on Gen-Z banking

What happened?

Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman held discussions with senior bankers with a focus on the changing banking needs of Gen-Z customers. The discussions highlighted the importance of understanding the digital-first behaviour of younger banking customers and improving customer-oriented banking services.

Why it matters

India has a rapidly growing digital banking ecosystem. Understanding younger customers is increasingly important for banks, fintech companies and financial service providers.

Exam Fact

Gen-Z banking is linked with digital-first financial behaviour, mobile banking, digital payments and technology-driven customer services.

Possible Question

Q. Recent discussions on "Gen-Z banking" are primarily associated with which sector?

A. Agriculture
B. Banking and financial services
C. Defence
D. Railways

Answer: B. Banking and financial services

Current Affair 02

Supreme Court to examine allegations related to CJP protest

What happened?

The Supreme Court has decided to set up a panel involving a former judge and a former CBI chief to examine allegations connected with the protests concerning the Chief Justice of Punjab and Haryana High Court. The development is important from the perspective of judicial administration and institutional accountability.

Why it matters

Questions involving the judiciary, judicial accountability and institutional mechanisms are relevant for UPSC Polity and Governance preparation.

Exam Focus

Remember the distinction between the Supreme Court, High Courts, judicial review and mechanisms concerning judicial administration.

Possible Question

Which constitutional organ is primarily associated with the administration of justice in India?

Answer: Judiciary

Current Affair 03

CAT issues notice to UPSC over disputed Civil Services Prelims questions

What happened?

The Central Administrative Tribunal (CAT) has issued a notice to the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) over a petition concerning eight disputed questions from the UPSC Civil Services Preliminary Examination 2026. The petitioners have argued that the disputed questions could affect their scores and eligibility for the next stage.

Why it matters

The issue is particularly relevant for Civil Services aspirants because even a small change in Prelims evaluation can affect qualification for the Mains examination.

Important Exam Fact

CAT is a specialised quasi-judicial body dealing with service matters of public servants under its jurisdiction.

Possible Question

CAT stands for:

A. Central Administrative Tribunal
B. Central Assessment Tribunal
C. Civil Administrative Tribunal
D. Central Appellate Tribunal

Answer: A. Central Administrative Tribunal

Current Affair 04

India hosts BRICS ICT Track in Pune

What happened?

As part of India's BRICS Chairship 2026, the Department of Telecommunications is hosting the BRICS ICT Track in Pune from 17 to 21 August 2026. The programme focuses on international cooperation in information and communication technologies.

The ICT Track includes the 7th BRICS Working Group for Cooperation in ICTs, the Digital BRICS Forum & Expo and the Communications Ministers Meeting.

Why it matters

The event is important for understanding India's role in digital cooperation, AI, cybersecurity, digital governance and technology-led development within BRICS.

Exam Fact

Theme: "Innovate, Cooperate and Transform (ICT) for a Resilient Future"

Venue: Pune, Maharashtra

Possible Question

BRICS ICT Track 2026 is being hosted in which Indian city?

A. Bengaluru
B. Hyderabad
C. Pune
D. Chennai

Answer: C. Pune

Exam Fact

The visit focuses on strengthening India-Nepal defence cooperation.

Possible Question

Which Indian Army Chief was conferred the honorary rank of General of the Nepali Army in August 2026?

China's Foreign Minister Wang Yi begins South Korea visit

The visit is significant for East Asian diplomacy and regional security. It also comes ahead of the upcoming APEC summit in Shenzhen.

Exam Focus

Current Affair 07

China's Unitree unveils high-speed humanoid robot "Superman"

What happened?

Chinese humanoid robotics company Unitree unveiled a new high-speed humanoid robot called "Superman" ahead of its stock-market debut in Shanghai. According to reports, the robot can reach a speed of around 12.66 metres per second and perform a vertical jump of around two metres.

Why it matters

The development highlights the rapid growth of humanoid robotics and embodied artificial intelligence. Robotics and AI are increasingly becoming important areas of technology and economic competition.

Exam Fact

Unitree is a Chinese robotics company headquartered in Hangzhou. Its Shanghai listing is linked with the country's growing humanoid robotics sector.

Possible Question

The "Superman" humanoid robot was unveiled by which company?

A. Unitree
B. SpaceX
C. Tesla
D. Samsung

Answer: A. Unitree

Current Affair 08

Canada faces new 50% US tariff deadline

What happened?

Canada is facing a new round of 50% US tariffs on a range of Canadian goods, with the deadline falling on 19 August 2026. Canadian and US officials have continued discussions over trade relations as businesses remain concerned about the potential economic impact.

Why it matters

Tariffs influence international trade, supply chains, prices and bilateral economic relations. This topic is useful for questions related to international trade, protectionism and globalisation.

Exam Fact

A tariff is a tax imposed on imported goods. Higher tariffs can increase the cost of imported products and affect international trade flows.

Possible Question

A tariff is primarily a:

A. Tax on imports
B. Tax on salaries
C. Subsidy on exports
D. Domestic income tax

Answer: A. Tax on imports
