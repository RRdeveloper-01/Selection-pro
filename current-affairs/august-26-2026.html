Current Affair #1

UPI Completes 10 Years of Digital Payments

What Happened?

The Unified Payments Interface (UPI) completed 10 years on 25 August 2026. UPI was launched in 2016 by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) under the regulatory oversight of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI).

Why It Matters?

UPI has become one of India's most important Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) platforms, allowing fast and interoperable digital payments.

📌 Exam Fact

UPI was launched in 2016. It is operated by NPCI and works under the regulatory framework of the RBI.

🎯 Possible Question

Q. UPI was launched in which year?
Answer: 2016

India-China Hold High-Level Border Talks

Senior Indian and Chinese officials held talks in Beijing on 25 August 2026 regarding the boundary issue and broader bilateral relations.

Current Affair #4

DRDO Technologies to be Transferred to Indian Industry

What Happened?

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh approved the transfer of technologies developed by DRDO for conventional missile systems to eligible Indian defence industries.

Why It Matters?

The move is aimed at strengthening India's domestic defence manufacturing ecosystem, increasing indigenous production and reducing dependence on imports.

🇮🇳 Aatmanirbhar Bharat

The initiative supports the broader goal of Aatmanirbharta in Defence and greater participation of Indian industries and MSMEs.

🎯 Possible Question

Q. DRDO comes under which ministry?
Answer: Ministry of Defence

Current Affair #5

India Launches Indigenous Non-GMO Popcorn Maize Hybrid Seed

What Happened?

Vice-President C. P. Radhakrishnan launched India's first non-GMO, high-expansion popcorn maize hybrid seed at Musunuru in Eluru district of Andhra Pradesh.

The indigenous seed was developed by Gourmet Popcornica.

Why It Matters?

The development can help reduce dependence on imported hybrid technology and promote indigenous agricultural innovation.

📌 Exam Fact

Location: Musunuru, Eluru district, Andhra Pradesh

🎯 Possible Question

Q. India's first non-GMO high-expansion popcorn maize hybrid seed was launched in which state?
Answer: Andhra Pradesh
