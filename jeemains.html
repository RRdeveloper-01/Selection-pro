JEE Main 2026 Preparation Strategy

JEE Main is one of India's most competitive engineering entrance examinations. Success depends on consistent practice, conceptual clarity, proper revision and regular mock tests.

  • Master NCERT: Build a strong foundation in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics using NCERT books.
  • Practice PYQs: Solve Previous Year Question Papers regularly to understand the latest exam pattern.
  • Daily Revision: Revise formulas, reactions and important concepts every day.
  • Mock Tests: Attempt full-length mock tests every week and analyse your mistakes.
  • Time Management: Practice solving questions within the actual exam duration.
  • Focus on Accuracy: Since negative marking exists, avoid random guessing.
  • Stay Consistent: Small daily improvements lead to excellent ranks.
