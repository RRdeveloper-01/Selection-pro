Banking Exams 2026 Overview

Banking examinations are among the most popular competitive exams for candidates looking for careers in public sector banks. IBPS conducts recruitment processes for participating public sector banks, while SBI conducts its own recruitment examinations.

The most commonly prepared banking exams include IBPS PO, IBPS Clerk/CSA, SBI PO and SBI Clerk. Their syllabus has several common subjects, especially English Language, Reasoning Ability and Quantitative Aptitude.

IBPS vs SBI Banking Exams

Exam Level Main Subjects Selection Stages
IBPS PO Officer English, Reasoning, Quant, General/Banking Awareness Prelims + Mains + Further Selection
IBPS Clerk / CSA Clerical English, Reasoning, Quant, General/Financial Awareness Prelims + Mains
SBI PO Officer English, Reasoning, Data Analysis, Banking Awareness Prelims + Mains + Further Selection
SBI Clerk Clerical English, Quant, Reasoning, General/Financial Awareness Prelims + Mains + Language Test

Banking Mains Exam Pattern & Syllabus

The Mains examination is more comprehensive than Prelims. Along with the core aptitude subjects, candidates generally need strong preparation in General Awareness, Banking Awareness and financial/economic topics.

💡 Mains Preparation Rule

Do not wait until clearing Prelims to start General Awareness. Banking and current affairs preparation should continue throughout the preparation cycle.

Major Mains Areas

  • Reasoning & Computer Aptitude
  • Data Analysis & Interpretation
  • English Language
  • General Awareness
  • Banking & Financial Awareness
  • Economy and current events
  • Descriptive English where applicable

IBPS PO & SBI PO Exam Preparation Guide

PO-level examinations require preparation beyond basic Prelims concepts. Candidates should focus on advanced reasoning, data interpretation, banking awareness, economy and current affairs.

🎯 PO Preparation Priorities

  • Build strong Prelims fundamentals.
  • Practice difficult puzzles regularly.
  • Improve Data Interpretation speed.
  • Read banking and financial current affairs.
  • Practice descriptive English where required.
  • Attempt full-length Mains mock tests.

SBI PO Mains Focus

SBI PO Mains includes objective sections covering areas such as Reasoning & Computer Aptitude, Data Analysis & Interpretation, General Awareness about Economy/Banking and English Language. SBI's official PO pattern also includes a descriptive English test in Mains.

IBPS Clerk & SBI Clerk Preparation Guide

Clerk-level examinations focus strongly on speed and accuracy. Candidates should develop fast calculation, quick reasoning and strong reading comprehension skills.

Clerk Preparation Priorities

  1. Complete basic concepts of all Prelims subjects.
  2. Practice 20–30 minutes of calculation daily.
  3. Solve puzzles and seating arrangements regularly.
  4. Read current affairs consistently.
  5. Revise banking awareness every week.
  6. Take sectional mocks before full-length mocks.

⚠️ Important

Exact section names, marks, timings, eligibility, selection stages and other conditions can vary by recruitment cycle. Always check the latest official notification before applying or appearing for an exam.

Banking Exam Preparation Strategy

A good banking exam preparation plan should balance concept building, practice, revision and mock tests. Studying for many hours without analysing mistakes is usually less effective than focused practice.

Phase 1: Build Fundamentals

  • Complete basic concepts of Quant, Reasoning and English.
  • Create a short formula and rule notebook.
  • Start daily reading practice.

Phase 2: Topic-Wise Practice

  • Solve previous-year questions.
  • Practice topic-wise questions.
  • Track accuracy and average solving time.

Phase 3: Mock Tests

  • Start with sectional tests.
  • Gradually move to full-length mocks.
  • Analyse every incorrect and skipped question.
  • Maintain a mistake notebook.

Phase 4: Revision

  • Revise formulas regularly.
  • Revise banking awareness.
  • Revise monthly current affairs.
  • Reattempt previously incorrect questions.

⏰ Simple Daily Banking Study Routine

  • Quant: Concept + practice questions
  • Reasoning: Puzzle + miscellaneous questions
  • English: Reading + grammar + vocabulary
  • Current Affairs: Daily revision
  • Banking Awareness: Short topic-wise revision
  • Mock Test: Regular sectional/full-length practice

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the syllabus of IBPS PO 2026?

IBPS PO preparation mainly covers English Language, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning Ability, Data Analysis, General Awareness, Banking Awareness and other Mains-level areas as prescribed in the latest recruitment notification.

What is the syllabus of IBPS Clerk 2026?

IBPS Clerk/CSA preparation primarily includes English, Numerical Ability/Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, General or Financial Awareness and other areas specified for the Mains examination.

Is SBI PO syllabus similar to IBPS PO syllabus?

Yes, there is substantial overlap in core areas such as English, Reasoning, Quantitative/Data Analysis and General/Banking Awareness. However, the exact pattern and selection stages can differ.

Is SBI Clerk syllabus similar to IBPS Clerk syllabus?

The major aptitude subjects are similar, but candidates should check the latest SBI and IBPS notifications for exact examination structure, language requirements and selection conditions.

Which subjects should I study first for banking exams?

Start with English, Reasoning and Quantitative Aptitude because these subjects form the core of the Prelims preparation. Alongside them, gradually build Banking Awareness and Current Affairs for Mains.

How can I prepare for Banking Awareness?

Study basic banking terms, RBI-related concepts, monetary policy, financial institutions, digital payment systems, government schemes and recent banking and financial developments.

Is current affairs important for banking exams?

Yes. Current affairs and banking/financial awareness are important parts of Mains-oriented preparation. Regular revision is more useful than trying to cover everything at the last moment.

Where should I check the latest banking exam pattern?

Always verify the latest official recruitment notification issued by IBPS or SBI because exam pattern, dates, eligibility and selection rules may change between recruitment cycles.
